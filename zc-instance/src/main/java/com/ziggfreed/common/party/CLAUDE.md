# party/

- zc holds the default `PartyService` (`ZcParty`, game and settings id `ziggfreedcommon`, built on first use; its words `ZcPartyMessages` and `ZcPartyScreenMessages` over `ziggfreedcommon.party.lang`; `/zigparty`, `invite`, `accept` and `leave` in `command/`, the adventurer group); a consumer uses it, adding only its own policy. A consumer supplies policy plus pre-built `Message`s (`PartyMessages`, `page/PartyScreenMessages`); nothing mod-specific enters this package. The page shows Queue and the privacy toggle only with a queue handler, and paints its Labels (title, count, empty state, badge) on `.TextSpans`, so a `memberCount` is the translation with its params, and its buttons (tabs, footer, row actions) are `ZigRichButton`s labelled through `ZigRichButton.text`; the chosen tab's `#Label` turns white and bold.
- Delivery is packet-only (`Universe.getPlayer` plus `Notify`/`EventTitles`), never a `Store` or `Ref` read. Mutations are `synchronized`, and a player belongs to one party at a time (`partyOf`).
- A party queues as a unit: the Queue button calls the consumer's `PartyQueueHandler`, which routes into `LobbyService.queueParty`. A private party queues under `QueueKey.privateQueue(gameId, presetId, partyId)`, and a null `presetId` (the page opened standalone) falls back to the consumer's default preset.
- Party settings are pack-authored under `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Party/`; read `PartySettingsConfig.getInstance().resolveOrDefault(...)` lazily, after assets load.
- The invite search is the shared `ZigSearchRow`: Search submits and nothing binds per keystroke.
- An open party page repaints itself on its service's changes, the Party tab only: the page registers its own `PartyListener` (one per service) and reopens the same instance on the viewer's world thread, so a consumer wires nothing. Every mutation fires the listener, a disband and an emptying leave included.

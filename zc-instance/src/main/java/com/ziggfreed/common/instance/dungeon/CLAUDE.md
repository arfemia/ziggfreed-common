# instance/dungeon/ - the walk-in dungeon

- A dungeon is a type of its own beside the queue's `InstancePresetAsset`: `DungeonAsset` (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Dungeons/`, owner file `mods/ziggfreedcommon/dungeons.json`); its copies start from zc's void template `Server/Instances/ZiggfreedCommon_Dungeon_Void/` unless a file names another. `DungeonValidator` is the boot audit's pass, and `/zigdungeon` the admin family.
- `CopyRouter` (pure, asked through `LiveWorlds`) is the one place a party's area is chosen and reserved, copies named `<dungeon>-<n>`. `Areas` pastes and clears an area on the world thread (waking its sections first, sweeping Intangible entities the box query skips), `AreaLayout` fixes a copy's areas when it opens, `AreaMembers` is the 1 Hz membership ticker off the world thread, and `AreaSpawnProvider` (type `ZigDungeonArea`) respawns each player in their own area; never per-player respawn points, which persist by world name while copy names repeat.
- `DungeonEntry` is the one way in (its class javadoc lists the steps), and it refuses a dungeon `DungeonValidator.entryErrors` finds an ERROR in, since the boot audit runs only when asked. A player's way in is `DungeonEntry.enterWithParty` (their zc party's area, a party of one without one; only members who walk in are routed or counted), run by the `ZigEnterDungeon` interaction Type (`ZigEnterDungeonInteraction`, the chain's command buffer as its accessor, Finished only when sent; a pack puts the gate in front, `[ZigRequires, ZigEnterDungeon]`) and the `Dungeon` destination (`DungeonDestinations`, for a placement's press-F or a conversation, its entry queued on a world task of its own since a press-F arrives mid-system); both tell a refusal through `DungeonRefusals` (`entry.*` keys), never a second map. The admin's `enterAlone` keeps its own lines and never pays. `EntryCost` is spent only through `DungeonEntryCost`, inside `DungeonEntry` (its javadoc says who pays), behind `DungeonEntryPrompt`'s confirm screen, and given back only through it: on the spot, before the player leaves (a short take, a teleport the engine refuses), through its `refund`, which hands the items over through zc-loot's `LootRewardKinds.handToPlayer` (the bag with the pickup notice, the rest at the feet), never a raw inventory give (`DungeonRefundLandsLikeAPickupTest`); once the loading screen has begun (a copy or paste that fails, the `ready` future completing exceptionally), through its `refundAsClaim`, a Claim titled `entry.refund.title` (B212).
- `DungeonRuns` is an area's run, `DungeonGoals` what a player arriving in it is told to do (`Text.GoalKey`, else lines built from `Complete`, then the run's rules) and `DungeonResults` what its end shows (each class javadoc is the reference: begin, a late joiner's own start, clear, fail, the round contract, the completion Claim through `claims/ClaimRolls.award`, no Play Again). A dungeon's name reads through `DungeonResults.nameOf`, the one helper. A run's members (who a start counted, who is in it now, a clear's winners) are a `RunMembers`, the one tracker `portal/PortalDungeonRuns` keeps too. zc-objectives' `DungeonObjectives` listener counts a `Complete.Objective` through `countObjective`, since this module never sees the moment stream, and zc-encounter's run-tag seam is filled from `DungeonRuns.runOf`. Its lock is a leaf, and every round event, payout and line is posted to the copy's world thread, never run under a lock.
- An endless dungeon (`Run.Endless`, `DungeonEndless`) is never cleared: `DungeonRuns` banks each `zc:cleared` its area's fight beats for every member standing there (`onWaveCleared`, the area found by where the fight stands), pays a leaver as they go and the rest from one chest at its end (`DungeonResults.endlessPaid`, the dungeon's `Rewards.Diminish` counting the run once), answers the fight's wave seam (`rulesAt`), and fires `DungeonWavesEvent` per player, which zc-stats keeps as `Dungeon_Best_Wave`.
- The default rules (R347): five players to a party's area, PartyLocked, a copy closing 60 seconds after it empties, no building, respawn in your own area, and an audit-ERROR dungeon refusing entry; the first listed boss clears it unless `AllBosses`, and a member dead or offline at the end gets no results page.
- A dungeon's gameplay config, `Server/GameplayConfigs/ZiggfreedCommon_Dungeon.json` (its `$Comment` the reference), lets nobody but a creative player break a block and nobody place or gather one (R386, R388). Known limit: a carryable block (its Use a `CarryBlock`) still lifts there, since the carry path fires `BreakBlockEvent` and never the damage event `ZigBlockRules` refuses, but it can never be set down (`CarryPlaceBlockInteraction` runs `BlockPlaceUtils.placeBlock`, which the world's placement switch refuses, as does `RefusedPlacement`), and no native leaf allows carried blocks alone; so a room takes a carried block from the carrier's hands (`ItemCondition`, Location Carried) or counts a dropped item (`TargetTypes` ItemDrop), never a block set down.
- `gen/` paints a generated dungeon (`Area.Layout`): `ZigDungeonWorldGenProvider` is the `ZigDungeon` world generator Type (registered into `IWorldGenProvider.CODEC` at setup, a failed registration rethrows) that the `ZiggfreedCommon_Dungeon_Generated` template names, and each copy's instance holds that copy's runs; `GeneratedRuns` lays a party's run out off the world thread and registers it before the party is sent (`DungeonEntry` then passes no arrival, so the engine asks `AreaSpawnProvider`, which lands them at the run's entry room), and logs its `[dungeon]` line when its lane frees (`AreaMembers`); `RoomPainter` writes rooms into generating chunks on `DungeonPainters`' threads (started at setup, stopped at shutdown without interrupting a chunk), never touching a store. A copy's areas are lanes along X and each run takes fresh ground along Z (`RunLanes`, `AreaLayout.runStride`, `CopyRouter.Rules.maxRunsPerArea`); a room's overlay is read only through `RoomOverlays`. The painter places no entities, so a room's `Keep.Markers` is the room logic's (`room/`) to honour, never the painter's; it honours only `Keep.Containers`.
- `room/` places a generated run's room logic (`RunLogic`, its class javadoc the reference): prepared with the run on a painter thread (an `Overlay`'s entities, else the prefab's volumes, groups, points and encounters; a base's markers, NPCs and props only with `Keep.Markers`), placed on the copy's world thread once per room per run when `AreaMembers`' ticker finds a member in the room or one door from it. It lasts until the party leaves, through a clear, a wipe or the time limit (R1000: a party goes back for skill-locked secrets and treasure rooms after the last boss), and is removed when the lane frees (`AreaMembers.settle`, `RunLogic.removeOn`), or with an older copy's run met under the same name (`DungeonRuns`); a closing copy forgets it (`GeneratedRuns.copyClosed`). The NPCs a placed spawn marker raised are not removed with it, since each run stands on fresh ground whose chunks unload once the party has gone; an entity in a chunk that unloads mid-run is gone, never placed again in the run, since a copy saves nothing. Keys ending `@room` or `@run` become the room's or run's own token (`RoomKeys`); `ZigVariant: "<Group>:<n>"` on a volume, group or point (encounters and markers carry no tags) keeps the run's one pick per group (`RoomVariants`); a turn is about the anchor CELL's centre, as the blocks turn (`RoomTurns`: the box turned in the JSON, each effect by the engine's `rotateInPlace` once, handed the volume's origin before the turn so a block target's cell, `floor(origin + offset)`, is exact wherever the origin stands, but `Teleport` and `PastePrefab`, turned in the JSON; never `PrefabRotation.getYaw()`). `RoomTurnsTest` and `RoomScopeTest` are its gates; `RoomLogicAudit` is the boot audit's pass over the logic. A room's foes are the plan's (`Rosters`, `Foes`, `Bars`): `RoomFoes` is the pure sizing and pick (`RoomFoesTest`), `GeneratedRuns` hands each room a `RunLogic.FoeSite` (its rule by the role it fills, its roster by its tags) and `RunLogic` raises them as it places the room, sized for the run's party then (`DungeonRuns.partySize`), through one generated `RoomFoeVolume` of native `SpawnNpc`s, so Mob Scaling rolls them as any spawn; never a manual marker, which Mob Scaling skips.
- The skill lock blocks a room's SkillLock template stands are assets, `Server/Item/Items/Dungeon_Locks/Zc_Lock_*` (R990, one per gathering skill's look): a Use of type Simple that does nothing, so `UseBlockInteraction` fires `UseBlockEvent.Post` and the volume's `BLOCK_USED` decides; no `Gathering`, so `BlockHarvestUtils` refuses every non-creative breaker and nothing drops; `Variant`, no recipe, no category, so the library hides them; no `Support`, so they never fall. A `Parent` on a vanilla block would inherit its `Gathering`, so they copy the look instead.
- `kit/` is the room kit, trigger-volume effect Types any zc dungeon uses, generated or not, registered unguarded into `TriggerEffect.CODEC` by `InstanceBootstrap.registerRoomKit` (their editor names live in zc-objectives' `server.lang`, the jar's one). Each finds its run through `RoomKitTrigger` (`DungeonRuns.liveRun`: the stepping player's area, else the area holding the volume). `ZigCheckpoint` moves only that run's respawn through `RunCheckpoints`, which `AreaSpawnProvider` asks first for every arrival (never the world's spawn or a personal point; a late or returning member lands at the current checkpoint, joining the party where it is), heals with `Zc_Checkpoint_Heal` and shows the banner through `EventTitles`; its settings are flat leaves the in-game inspector edits with real controls (R989: `RespawnOffset` on the engine's `Vector3dUtil.CODEC`, `RespawnYaw`, `HealEffect` with its picker, `BannerTitleKey`, `BannerSubtitleKey`), so a new knob there stays a flat leaf of a shape the inspector renders, labelled in that `server.lang`. `ZigRunMilestone` counts once per run per id and placement (`DungeonRuns.milestone`) and raises `DungeonMilestoneEvent` (zc-stats' `Dungeon_Milestones`); a milestone is no quest step, since zc-objectives has no objective kind for it. `RunDeaths` counts a member's death; a clear's results list deaths, and rooms, puzzles and secrets when counted. The kit's condition Type `ZigHasEffect` (`kit/ZigHasEffectCondition`, registered into `TriggerCondition.CODEC`) asks whether the event's entity wears an effect, so a volume reacts to a creature in one state only (a charging boss and a pillar); its effect `ZigFeedback` (`kit/ZigFeedbackEffect`) shows a FeedbackMoment to the players within a radius of where it fired, in any world, never only a run's. Both effects find their run through `DungeonRuns.liveRun`, which answers until the party leaves (R1000), after a clear too: a checkpoint keeps answering (`RunCheckpoints.respawnAt` asks `DungeonRuns.held`, per run, so the area's next run never sees it) and goes when the area frees, and a milestone reached after the end still raises its event but stays out of the run's tally, so the results already shown keep their numbers.
- A run's seed has one source, `RunSeeds`: a dungeon with `Seed.Daily` (a child file of the normal one, so its runs, diminish and Records line are its own) gives every run that day the seed made from the UTC day and its id, else a fresh random one; `GeneratedRuns.open` takes it as the party sets off and `DungeonRuns` reads a generated run's back (`GeneratedRuns.seedOf`), never a second draw. The Records daily board is the `Daily` stat period (`StatPeriods/Daily.json`, the Today tab), `Marked: Daily` (R1005): it ranks only the lines `StatBoardDungeons.marks` gives that mark (a dungeon with `Seed.Daily`), and shows on the Dungeons tab alone, only while today's window holds one. Known limit: a daily run begun before midnight UTC and cleared after keeps the day before's rooms but ranks on the new day's board, since the stat board files a write by the moment it lands. `Seed.Daily` on a dungeon with no `Area.Layout` changes nothing (the audit warns, and its runs say nothing of a shared layout).
- `tier/` is a dungeon's tiers (B207): a tier is a dungeon file with `Tier` {`Rank`, `Opens`} that `Parent`s the tier below, so it needs no store of its own. `TierLadder` is the pure rule (`TierLadderTest`); `DungeonTiers` builds a ladder (every enabled file reaching the same lowest rung down `Opens`, plus a file naming one of them as its `Parent`, the base tier's daily) and reads who opened what from the `Dungeon_Runs_Completed` line through `ziggfreedcommon:counter`, never a new record. A rung is open to a party only when every member standing in the asker's world (lead's call) has cleared that rung's own `Tier.Opens`; rank only orders the list. `DungeonEntry` refuses a tier the party has not opened (`TIER_LOCKED`, a player's way in only). `DungeonGate` decides whether a way in (`ZigEnterDungeon`, the `Dungeon` destination) opens `DungeonGatePage` (a ladder of two or more rungs: the pickable tiers, the daily, this week's omens, what opens the next tier) instead of entering. A tier's `Tier.Health` and `Tier.Damage` step its boss fights up: `DungeonRuns.scaleAt` answers zc-encounter's run-scale seam from the run standing at the fight, never a factor a script reads.
- `omen/` is the weekly omens: `DungeonOmenAsset` (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/DungeonOmens/`, `Mobs.Effects`, `Players.Effects`, `Text`), `OmenDraw` the pure draw (R1006, Mythic+ style: the week's set is a dungeon's `Omens.Pool` in an order drawn by the week alone, Monday 00:00 UTC, cut to `Omens.PerWeek`, and a tier meets its first `Omens.Count`, so every run of a tier that week meets the same omens, a higher tier's include a lower one's, and the gate lists each tier's; never the run's seed), `RunOmens` the running side (`draw` kept on each run at its start, a player's effects renewed each second on the ticker's world post and taken off as they leave, the `ziggfreedcommon:dungeon_omen` factor a room's `ZigRequires` reads) and `OmenMobs`, the system putting the omens' creature effects on each NPC that comes into being in a run's area (on `SPAWN` and `LOAD` alike, since a sleeping section's creatures come back through a load), plus `RunOmens.putOnStanding` once at a pasted run's start for the creatures its prefab brought. Only a foe gets them: a role whose default attitude to players is hostile (`WorldSupport.getDefaultPlayerAttitude`), so a friendly character, a pet or a summon that ignores players (the family's do) is left out; one already carrying an effect is never given it again. A Rallying omen names an effect whose `Parent` is zc-effects' `Zig_War_Cry` (the war cry rides the effect); zc never imports Mob Scaling. `DungeonReplayAudit` is the boot audit's pass over tiers and omens.
- `layout/` is the procedural layout (PD1): a pure generator (`LayoutGenerator`, no engine type, so it runs in a plain unit JVM; `LayoutGeneratorTest`'s 10,000-seed sweep is its gate) over folded `RoomSpec`s and a `LayoutPlan`, a pool's variant count asked through the `PoolIndex` seam, and `RoomCatalogueAudit`; its stores, `DungeonRooms` and `DungeonLayouts`, and the server's `PrefabPoolIndex` sit in `layout/asset/`. A turn is the engine's `PrefabRotation` index (`QuarterTurns`). A `PlacedRoom.fill` (the room's `Fill`, else its stratum's) is for the painter alone: the generator resolves it and never acts on it. A room file's `Overlay` leaf (`RoomSpec.overlay`, one prefab over a `Prefab` that is one prefab too, `OVERLAY_POOL` in the audit otherwise) is a second prefab the painter paints after the base, same anchor and turn, its entities the room's logic; the generator never reads it, since `Box` covers both. `tools/rooms/build_room.py`'s overlay mode writes the overlay prefab and its room file. A door's `ExitOnly` (R1012, `DoorSpec.exitOnly`) is never the door a room is entered by, only one it may be left by (a puzzle's sealed way on; `build_room.py` writes it for every door a spec seals); the audit's `NO_WAY_IN` refuses a room with no other door unless its role is Entry or Cap. A plan's `Apart` (`LayoutPlan.Apart`, Tag and Blocks) keeps any two rooms carrying the tag that far apart, nearest cell to nearest cell (`Box.distanceTo`): `fit` and the caps refuse a closer spot, so a try that cannot keep it fails and the next seed runs, and no relax step loosens it; once a tagged room stands, the path's next rooms are steered away from it (the target's farthest open door first, and of the picked room's fits the one whose open doors lie farthest), so the seed still picks every room. A plan's `MaxPerRun` (`LayoutPlan.MaxPerRun`, Role and/or Tag, Max) lets each matching room stand at most Max times in a run, counted per room id in `fit`, never loosened by a relax step. `LayoutGeneratorTest`'s apart and max-per-run cases are their gates.

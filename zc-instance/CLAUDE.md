# zc-instance

- Depends on `zc-core`, `zc-presentation`, `zc-loot`, `zc-encounter` (read only: `EncounterBoards`, `StatBoardBossFights` and `/zigleaderboard` read the binding rows that name a fight's records, `DungeonRuns`, `portal/PortalDungeonRuns` and `claims/SpoilsChests` listen for `EncounterDefeatedEvent`, the chest reading a row's `Spoils`, `DungeonValidator` asks `EncounterScripts` whether a `Complete.Bosses` id is a loaded script, and `InstanceBootstrap` hands `EncounterAudit` the `RecordGroups` a row's `Leaderboard.Group` may name), `zc-world` (only `TickingSections`: `wake`, which a dungeon area's paste and clear need first, and `whenTicking`, which a spoils chest waits on: an entity added to a sleeping chunk section is parked out of the world, where neither the paste nor the clear sees it) and `zc-cast` (only the interaction-Type toolkit `interaction/type/`, for `ZigEnterDungeon`). `zc-objectives` and `zc-stats` are its library dependents.
- Never add an edge to `zc-objectives`, `zc-dialogue` or `zc-progression`. A round leaves as two events: `InstanceRoundStartedEvent` (`InstanceRounds.fireStarted`) when it begins and `InstanceRoundCompletedEvent` (`InstanceRounds.fireCompleted`) when it ends, each on the instance world thread; `winners` is empty on a loss or an abort.
- Every page takes an immutable `*PageDeps` plus a locale-free messages provider; the consumer supplies pre-built client-resolved `Message`s.
- A page names a player only through zc-presentation's `ui/name/PlayerDisplayNames.displayName`, on a Label's `.TextSpans` (a decorated name is a parameterized Message). `PlayerNamesGoThroughTheSeamTest` fails a page that reads `getUsername()` or writes a name on `.Text`; a bare username kept as data (a search haystack) carries `// NAME-DATA-OK: <reason>`. A notice about a player (the invite-sent toast) names them through `PlayerDisplayNames.plainName`, which carries no title.
- `world/InstanceWorlds` is the one engine-instance launcher: every copy spawns under zc's own name (never an `InstanceKey`) with its removal (WorldEmpty AND IdleTimeout) and delete flags set in the spawn's config step, and `removeWorld` runs only on its remover thread; a copy's area is keyed by `world/CopyKey`.
- `dungeon/` is the walk-in dungeon, a type of its own beside the queue's `InstancePresetAsset` (its router).
- `portal/` counts the game's own portal dungeons as dungeon runs, never runs them: a `PortalDungeons/<PortalType id>.json` file (`PortalDungeonAsset`) names what clears one, and `PortalDungeonRuns` (its class javadoc is the reference) watches the worlds the game opens for it (the world's `PortalWorld` resource, read on its own thread) and fires the dungeon round contract (`DungeonRuns.MOD_ID`, mode empty, preset the PortalType id) as `DungeonRuns` does: queued under its leaf lock, posted to the world thread.
- `claims/` is the Claims tab (`MenuSlot.CLAIMS`, shown while a Claim waits) over zc-loot's `loot/claim/ClaimStore`: `ClaimsPage` reads and pays, `ClaimGrants` hands a Claim over through the bag guard, puts back what did not fit and announces what it delivered (`ClaimCollectedEvent`), `ClaimNotices` raises the toast when a Claim arrives or is topped up (one per burst, each Claim named once, leaving out a Claim the page the player just opened offers itself, `offeredOnPage`) and the notice once a session at login (never a grant, M428), and `/zigclaims give` rolls a loot table into a player's Claims, online or not. A payout that writes a Claim calls `ClaimStore.award` (or `awardOrTopUp`), never this package.
- The spoils chest is `claims/SpoilsChests` (its javadoc is the reference): a won bound fight's first Claim stands one `BlockEntity` prop where the boss fell (`EncounterDefeatedEvent.deathPosition`), marked by the saved `SpoilsChest` component, whose Use runs `ZigOpenSpoils` through the shipped root interaction `Zig_Open_Spoils`. It IS the player's Claims of that fight (`Claim.fight`), so it never holds a reward of its own: `SpoilsView.open` is the one place its view is decided (the game's chest window, `SpoilsWindow`, with the Claims page filtered to the fight behind one switch); the window shows the items and writes back by item and amount through `ClaimStore.take` and `putBack`, never by slot, announces what leaves it per Claim through `ClaimGrants.announceCollected` (the press's own `ClaimCollectedEvent`, never twice for an item that came back), and a drop from it is refused (`SpoilsChestSystems.NoDrop`). `SpoilsChests.inChest` (what a notice says) is true only while the fight's chest stands or is rising. `SpoilsChestSystems` takes a chest down once its credited players hold nothing from the fight, its lifetime is over, or this session does not know it (a restart's leftover, swept on load).

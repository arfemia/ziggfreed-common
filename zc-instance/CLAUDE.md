# zc-instance

- Depends on `zc-core`, `zc-presentation`, `zc-loot`, `zc-encounter` (read only: `EncounterBoards`, `StatBoardBossFights` and `/zigleaderboard` read the binding rows that name a fight's records, `DungeonRuns`, `portal/PortalDungeonRuns` and `claims/SpoilsChests` listen for `EncounterDefeatedEvent`, the chest reading a row's `Spoils`, `DungeonValidator` asks `EncounterScripts` whether a `Complete.Bosses` id is a loaded script, and `InstanceBootstrap` hands `EncounterAudit` the `RecordGroups` a row's `Leaderboard.Group` may name and the `RewardChests` its `Spoils.Chest` may name), `zc-world` (only `TickingSections`: `wake`, which a dungeon area's paste and clear need first, and `whenTicking`, which a spoils chest waits on: an entity added to a sleeping chunk section is parked out of the world, where neither the paste nor the clear sees it) and `zc-cast` (only the interaction-Type toolkit `interaction/type/`, for `ZigEnterDungeon`). `zc-objectives` and `zc-stats` are its library dependents.
- Never add an edge to `zc-objectives`, `zc-dialogue` or `zc-progression`. A round leaves as two events: `InstanceRoundStartedEvent` (`InstanceRounds.fireStarted`) when it begins and `InstanceRoundCompletedEvent` (`InstanceRounds.fireCompleted`) when it ends, each on the instance world thread; `winners` is empty on a loss or an abort.
- Every page takes an immutable `*PageDeps` plus a locale-free messages provider; the consumer supplies pre-built client-resolved `Message`s.
- A page names a player only through zc-presentation's `ui/name/PlayerDisplayNames.displayName`, on a Label's `.TextSpans` (a decorated name is a parameterized Message). `PlayerNamesGoThroughTheSeamTest` fails a page that reads `getUsername()` or writes a name on `.Text`; a bare username kept as data (a search haystack) carries `// NAME-DATA-OK: <reason>`. A notice about a player (the invite-sent toast) names them through `PlayerDisplayNames.plainName`, which carries no title.
- `world/InstanceWorlds` is the one engine-instance launcher: every copy spawns under zc's own name (never an `InstanceKey`) with its removal (WorldEmpty AND IdleTimeout) and delete flags set in the spawn's config step, and `removeWorld` runs only on its remover thread; a copy's area is keyed by `world/CopyKey`.
- `dungeon/` is the walk-in dungeon, a type of its own beside the queue's `InstancePresetAsset` (its router).
- `portal/` counts the game's own portal dungeons as dungeon runs, never runs them: a `PortalDungeons/<PortalType id>.json` file (`PortalDungeonAsset`) names what clears one, and `PortalDungeonRuns` (its class javadoc is the reference) watches the worlds the game opens for it (the world's `PortalWorld` resource, read on its own thread; a player counts once ready in the world and held by its `getPlayerRefs`, never at `AddPlayerToWorldEvent`, which fires before a join that can still fail; a clear counts every player the world holds, ready or not, since a join that outlasts the engine's ready timer never fires the ready event) and fires the dungeon round contract (`DungeonRuns.MOD_ID`, mode empty, preset the PortalType id) as `DungeonRuns` does: queued under its leaf lock, posted to the world thread.
- `claims/` is the Claims tab (`MenuSlot.CLAIMS`, shown while a Claim waits) over zc-loot's `loot/claim/ClaimStore`: `ClaimsPage` reads and pays, `ClaimGrants` hands a Claim over through the bag guard, puts back what did not fit and announces what it delivered (`ClaimCollectedEvent`), listing on the press's `Result` what each reward landed as (`Collected`), which the page's one toast names (`ClaimsView.landedLines`: the kind's receipt, else the row at its own quantity; a section per Claim when several pay), `ClaimNotices` raises the toast when a Claim arrives or is topped up (one per burst, each Claim named once, leaving out a Claim the page the player just opened offers itself, `offeredOnPage`) and the notice once a session at login (never a grant, M428), and `/zigclaims give` rolls a loot table into a player's Claims, online or not. A payout that writes a Claim calls `ClaimStore.award` (or `awardOrTopUp`), never this package.
- The reward chest is `claims/SpoilsChests` (its javadoc is the reference), and `SpoilsChests.stand` is the one entry every source calls (a won bound fight from `onDefeated`, keyed by its run id; zc-objectives' kill and break bonus rows through a `Reward_Chest` reward, keyed `chest:<uuid>`; `/zigclaims chest`). What a chest is comes from the reward chest file it names (`claims/RewardChestAsset`, `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/RewardChests/`, owner file `reward-chests.json`; the shipped `Zc_Spoils_Chest` is today's boss chest and every leaf's default, `Zc_Reward_Chest` the neutral one with a banner), its own `Contents` rolled per credited player through `ClaimRolls`, the one Lootable-to-Claim roll. Its first Claim stands one `BlockEntity` prop where the source left it (a boss: `EncounterDefeatedEvent.deathPosition`), marked by the saved `SpoilsChest` component, whose Use runs `ZigOpenSpoils` through the shipped root interaction `Zig_Open_Spoils`. It IS the player's Claims filed under its key (`Claim.fight`): `SpoilsView.open` is the one place its view is decided, and every chest opens one page, `RewardChestPage` (no rail; one list of reward cards across its Claims, `ClaimsView.chestCards`: what is not an item first, alike ones summed into one card, then the items best quality first, a card's quality being the one its drop's chance reads as (zc-loot's `instance/reward/DropRarity`, a gold card naming it only when rarer than Common), else the item's own; a card's click takes it through `ClaimGrants.claimReward`, a summed card each of its rewards in turn, `ClaimsView.takesOf`, the event still naming one Claim and one `rewardKey`, its toast adding them into one row at what landed, `ClaimsView.summedLines`; Take All `claimFight`, each under the page's own toast; every event answered once through `RewardChestActions.event`, which `RewardChestPageExitsTest` drives), whatever its file's `View.ItemsWindow` says; a payout made after the fight (a dungeon's clear under `Rewards.InChest`) joins the chest by asking `SpoilsChests.takes` and filing its Claim under the fight, never a second view. The game's chest window (`SpoilsWindow`, reached only from `ClaimsPage`'s chest mode, which no chest opens now) writes back by item and amount through `ClaimStore.take` and `putBack`, never by slot, announces what leaves it per Claim through `ClaimGrants.announceCollected` (the press's own `ClaimCollectedEvent`, never twice for an item that came back), and a drop from it is refused (`SpoilsChestSystems.NoDrop`). `SpoilsChests.inChest` (what a notice says, in the chest file's own words) is true only while the chest stands or is rising. A file's `Banner` shows once per player per chest through `EventTitles`; a joining source's banner (`tellInChest` naming the key) takes its place. `SpoilsChestSystems` takes a chest down once its credited players hold nothing from it (unless `Despawn.WhenEmptied` is false), its lifetime is over, or this session does not know it (a restart's leftover, swept on load).

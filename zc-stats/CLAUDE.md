# zc-stats

The player stat store: one saved record of every player's tallies, fed by the library's own producers on any server.

- One library module depends on this one, zc-almanac (its season pages' lines and their one-time import), and no other may; it depends on zc-core, zc-progression, zc-instance and zc-encounter. A consumer counts and reads through `playerstats/PlayerStats` only, never a record of its own. The package is `playerstats`, never `stats` (already split between zc-core and zc-entity).
- `PlayerStatsComponent` (save key `ZiggfreedCommon:Stats`) holds three strings that only grow: `Tallies` (lifetime, `key:value|...` through zc-core's `counter/PackedTallies`), `Seasons` (`<event>@<year>/<stat key>` through `StatKeys.season`: every counted write while a season is live moves its copy the stat's own way, and a season page's line its own, one copy per season-year, read off zc-core's `Occurrences.liveAllCached` (once a second) on the server's clock, never the board's trial clock) and `Imports` (claim ids). Append a field, never rename one; a damaged pair costs only itself and a key no stat registers rides along.
- Keys come only from `StatKeys`: `<Stat>` is the total, `<Stat>/<thing>` one breakdown line, matched without regard to case. A stat id never carries `/ @ | :`; a thing never carries `|`.
- It holds counted stats only (Sum, Max, Min). A Gauge and a native reading are ranked on the board, never stored here.
- A Native stat is read, never counted: its reader is registered in zc-core's `counter/NativeReaders` under the stat's id by the one module that may ask the engine (this module's `reader/`; zc-reputation's standing reader), so zc-reputation needs no edge here. `PlayerStats.readNative` asks every reader whose stat file says `Kind: Native` at the ready pass and as Records opens (`StatBoardSource.refresh`), and sets the answers through `setAllTime`. The engine keeps no per-player count of Memories recorded (`PlayerMemories` holds only what a player carries until they record it at the temple, into a server-wide set), so no Memories reader exists.
- A stat is a file, `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Stats/<Id>.json`, shipped by whoever feeds it, so a zc-only server shows no stat nobody counts. Its `From` entries are the shared objective leaf plus `Count` (`Moment` or `One`); the leaf's own `Amount` means nothing here.
- Count off the shared moment stream (`StatsMomentListener`); an ECS system only for what no producer fires (a player's death, playtime). A stat counts a moment once, at its first matching `From` entry.
- A season page's line is no stat file: `SeasonLines` (an owner's whole set through `PlayerStats.season`, the Almanac's from `AlmanacStatLines`; both javadocs are the reference, the page's Records section `season_<event>` included) keeps the page's own keys, folded through zc-core's `Counters.fold` as `AlmanacKeys` folds, so byte-identical to it, and counts while its owner's switch is on, whatever `$Enabled` says. It adds every matching line, a `CALENDAR_ATTENDED` line included (the `$` marks stay the Almanac listener's), and the generic season copy skips a key a line of that event owns; `sectionLines` (the audit) reads the authored sections only.
- `importOnce` builds the merge aside (the lifetime bag, and with a seasons bag both) and swaps and claims in one synchronized step; it runs with the store switched off, since it moves data rather than counting.
- Off means absent: `$Enabled` in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/stats.json` (`StatsSwitch`) stops all counting and ranking but a season page's lines, and the `ziggfreedcommon:counter` source answers null. The file's other entries belong to the board.
- Boss fights (`encounter/`, the edge to zc-encounter) are `EncounterStats` (counted on the fight's bucket line, on the player's own world thread) and `EncounterBoardImport` (the frozen encounter board's one-time carry), each javadoc the reference.
- The store writes zc-instance's stat board only through `StatRankFeed` (`board/StatBoardRankFeed`, installed at setup): a feed that throws costs its ranking, never the count. It answers the board's `StatBoardSource` from the stat files (`board/StatsBoardSource`); the board owns sections, periods and windows.

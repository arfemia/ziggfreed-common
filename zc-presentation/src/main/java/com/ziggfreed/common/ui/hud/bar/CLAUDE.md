# ui/hud/bar/ - the shared progress-bar HUD

Router for `com.ziggfreed.common.ui.hud.bar`. Two semi-transparent panels any mod drives with one call per movement; the library creates a row on demand, dresses it from what the reporting mod says, and knows nothing about what a value measures. Nothing is authored for a row to appear. The parent [`ui/hud/`](../CLAUDE.md) router holds the `KeyedCustomHud` base this builds on, the per-player `HudPreferenceComponent` the panels read, and the settings page + `/zighud` family that edit it.

- **THE RULING (maintainer, 2026-09-09): there is NO value-source seam here and none is to be rebuilt.** The asset store is the extension point (the root `CLAUDE.md`'s "The ASSET STORE is the extension point" rule); a `Source: <namespace>:<local>` string paired with a Java `register(namespace, provider)` call re-invents what the `HudBars` folder already does, so the seam was deleted outright rather than re-keyed. Whoever reports a movement hands the reading over WITH it (`HudBars.moved` takes a `HudBarReading`), the row keeps it, the paint draws from what the row holds, and nothing is asked of anyone at paint time. A bar only exists because something moved, so there is nothing to read back for. A row id is OPAQUE: no namespace convention, nothing split on a colon, matched whole (case-insensitively) against an override's `Source`; a mod names its row by the id of the thing measured.
- **TWO PANELS, and which one a row lands on is the CALLER's choice** (maintainer, 2026-09-09), made by which call it makes, never by anything this package reads out of the row: only the mod reporting a movement knows what the player is in the middle of. [`HudBarStackHud`](HudBarStackHud.java) is the tall ledger (`HudBars.moved` / `itemMoved`), [`HudBarGridHud`](HudBarGridHud.java) the wide block for values moving while a player is simply out in the world (`HudBars.movedOnGrid`). Both are attached to every player at ready, LATE, and `HudBars.panels()` lists them (a `HudBarLayout` each) for whatever offers a panel by id.
- **WHERE a panel sits is a PLACEMENT, an asset, never a corner hardcoded in Java** (maintainer, 2026-09-09; the closed-roster trap the root `CLAUDE.md` bans). [`HudBarPlacementAsset`](HudBarPlacementAsset.java) at `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/HudBarPlacements/<id>.json` is one named spot: `LabelKey`, a `Position` group ([`HudBarPosition`](HudBarPosition.java): `Preset` + `OffsetX` + `OffsetY`, every leaf nullable), `Columns`, `RowsPerColumn`, a `Gap` group ([`HudBarGap`](HudBarGap.java): `AfterRow` + `Pixels`, every leaf nullable, `authoredAfterRow()` / `authoredPixels()` reading a leaf exactly as the file states it, a zero included, for the settings page that must show a stated zero as zero - a band left clear across every column after so many rows COUNTED FROM THE PINNED EDGE, so a bottom-pinned stack straddles the player's own health and mana bars; absent, or either number not positive, or no row past `AfterRow` in a column = no band), a `Cutout` group ([`HudBarCutout`](HudBarCutout.java): `Column` + `Rows`, every leaf nullable, `authoredColumn()` / `authoredRows()` the same raw reads - the cells at the PINNED END of ONE column left empty so the panel steps around something a client draws under that column alone; `Column` is ONE-BASED and counts from the panel's own FIRST column, the one at the spot's origin (at a right-pinned spot column 1 is the rightmost), i.e. the paint's `ordinal`, never the slot-column index; `rowsAt(ordinal)` answers what it leaves at one column; absent, either number not positive, or a column the rows never open = no cut), `MinHeight` (a floor on the pushed panel height; absent = none), `Color` (the frame's colour at this spot, the one hex [`../card/HudCardLook`](../card/HudCardLook.java) explains: a multiply over the shipped patch, eight digits carrying a transparency; absent = the shared card look, a value that is not a hex warning once and reading as absent), `Panels` (the panel ids it was measured for; absent = every panel), `Order`, `Enabled`. The library ships three: `TopLeft` (the ledger's home, at the SAME origin as the zone card mob-scaling draws there, (16, 90), deliberately OVER it because the ledger attaches later and is transparent behind its frame; 2 columns, 13 deep, offered to both panels, and `MinHeight` 124, the card's own height, so a one-row ledger still covers it - the floor is the SPOT's, authorable and overridable, and applies to whichever panel sits there), `TopRight` (6 columns growing leftward, for the grid) and `BottomLeft` (the grid's shipped default: 3 columns of 200px, (16, 16), above the hotbar, growing UPWARD, `RowsPerColumn` 1 so it spreads sideways before it goes deep, `Gap {AfterRow 3, Pixels 66}` - the mana bar sits in the band and rides its top edge, so 66 puts it near the band's middle - as a starting pair to retune against a server's own screen, and `Cutout {Column 3, Rows 3}` because the utility slot and the hotbar's left end sit under the far-right column's lowest rows). A pack adds a spot by dropping one file and every picker offers it; the owner layer is `mods/ziggfreedcommon/hud-bar-placements.json`, accepting `Gap` and `Cutout` per leaf like every other group. Fold: [`HudBarPlacementConfig`](HudBarPlacementConfig.java) (`placement(id)`, `offeredFor(panelId)` in `Order`-then-id order, enabled + fitting only; `ids()` feeds the Asset Editor pick list `ziggfreedcommon:hud_bar_placements`).
- **[`HudBarPanelAsset`](HudBarPanelAsset.java) is a panel** at `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/HudBarPanels/<id>.json`: `Enabled`, `LabelKey`, `Placement` (the spot's id), inline `Position` / `Columns` / `RowsPerColumn` / `Gap` / `Cutout` / `MinHeight` / `Color` (restatements OVER the spot, leaf by leaf, for an owner stating custom values; `authoredColor()` is validated the same way as the spot's), `MaxVisible`, `RepaintMs`. It carries no slot ceiling of its own: `maxVisible(documentSlots)` takes it from the caller, because that belongs to a DOCUMENT. `Default.json` names `TopLeft`, `Grid.json` names `BottomLeft`; a consumer's same-id file wins by pack order and `mods/ziggfreedcommon/hud-bar-panels.json` wins over both. [`HudBarPanelConfig`](HudBarPanelConfig.java)`.current()` / `.grid()` / `.panel(id)` always answer. [`HudBarPanelOwnerWriter`](HudBarPanelOwnerWriter.java) is the ONE path the panels owner file is written from in game (the settings page's Server tab, which offers every one of the seven inline leaves as a field, and `/zighud default`): `setPlacement` (clears the inline leaves, since a nudge measured from one corner is nonsense against another; the inline `Color` is left alone, a tint being measured from no corner), `setEnabled`, `setLeaves`; it keeps an existing entry's key spelling, re-reads through the same codec and repaints everyone.
- **[`HudBarPlacement`](HudBarPlacement.java) is the RESOLVED answer** every paint reads, per player: the player's own pick (`HudPreferences.placementPick`) when it exists, is enabled and fits the panel, swapping the WHOLE group (corner, offsets, spread, band, cut, floor, colour) because an owner's inline nudge belongs to the owner's corner; else the spot the panel names with the panel's inline leaves (`Position`, `Columns`, `RowsPerColumn`, `Gap`, `Cutout`, `MinHeight`, `Color`) folded over it, the `Gap` and `Cutout` groups leaf by leaf; else the document's fallback (`HudBarLayout.defaultPosition`, 1 column, 1 row per column, no band, no cut, no floor, no colour). A panel naming a spot nothing authored warns ONCE and sits at the fallback. `columns(documentColumns)` clamps to the document; `bottomUp()` / `rightToLeft()` read the corner; `gap()` is the folded band (whether it APPLIES is `HudBarGap.applies`, the paint's guard, so an owner's `{"Gap": {"Pixels": 0}}` still folds), `cutout()` the folded cut (`HudBarCutout.applies` the same way, so `{"Cutout": {"Rows": 0}}` uses every cell again), `minHeight()` the floor (0 = none) and `color()` the spot-or-panel colour (null = the shared card look; the paint folds it through `HudCardLook.resolve(placement.color(), HudCardConfig.sharedColor())`, so the shared record is the layer under every colour here and lives in [`../card/`](../card/CLAUDE.md)). Every layer is a nullable leaf, never a mode.
- **Two parts to a row, orthogonal, never a Kind/Type enum.** A row DRAWS A FILL when it was moved with a reading ([`HudBarReading`](HudBarReading.java): `current` out of `maximum`, `fraction()` held to 0..1, a non-positive maximum reading full, `FULL` the bar drawn complete), and it CARRIES AN ITEM when it was moved as one (`HudBars.itemMoved`; its default row id is `item:<ItemId>`, `HudBars.itemRowId`, the ONE id shape this package owns). A fill row is two lines (name + gain over the bar); an item row is the first line alone (`#Bar` hidden). Fill rows sort above item rows within a panel.
- **A row may be HELD rather than fade on a clock** (maintainer, 2026-09-09). `HudBarLook.LINGER_HELD` is a linger meaning the row stays until something sends it away, and `HudBarDisplay.held()` carries it; `HudBars.fadeAll(playerRef, withinMs)` is what ends a set of them together. `HudBarHud.moved` gives a held row `Long.MAX_VALUE` outright rather than `now + linger`, so the arithmetic cannot overflow into a time already past and drop the row on its first sweep, and neither `moved` nor `rearmSweep` arms a sweep for one.
- **A row's number may be its own TOTAL rather than a delta** (same ruling): `HudBars.totalled` / `itemTotalled` state it outright, for a mod that already keeps a running total for that stretch of activity. `HudBars.moved` / `itemMoved` keep adding deltas, for a mod that only knows what just happened.
- **A row may word its own number**: `HudBarDisplay.counting(key)` names the localization key the figure binds on, instead of the panel's plain `+{0, number}`, for a figure that is not a gain and reads wrong with a plus sign. The key takes the number as its one parameter so each client still writes its own digits.
- **[`HudBarDisplay`](HudBarDisplay.java) is what a consumer says about a row** beside every move: `label` (a client-resolved `Message`), `icon` (the shared `IconSpec`), `color`, `order`, `lingerMs`, plus `leadCaption` / `trailCaption` and `countKey`, every part nullable; `over(under)` folds two part by part, and `between(lead, trail)` / `colored(c)` / `counting(key)` / `held()` restate one part per move; `forItem(itemId)` is the display an item derives for itself. **The two captions are the words at a bar's ends**, DELIBERATELY not authorable on `HudBarAsset`: a caption is per-move data. An end with nothing to say is painted BLANK, never hidden, because hiding it would collapse its width out of the row. **[`HudBarLook`](HudBarLook.java) is the settled look**: `resolve(id, override, display)` folds the authored override OVER the display OVER the defaults (`DEFAULT_COLOR` `#7fb2e0`, `DEFAULT_ORDER` 1000, `DEFAULT_LINGER_MS` 5000); a row with no name from either layer shows its own id.
- **[`HudBarAsset`](HudBarAsset.java) is an OPTIONAL override, never a requirement** (Pattern A, PascalCase, every leaf nullable and inheriting under `Parent`), one file per override at `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/HudBars/<id>.json`: `Source`, `LabelKey`, `Icon`, `Color`, `Order`, `LingerMs`, `Enabled`. The library ships none, and no consumer needs to. Fold: [`HudBarConfig`](HudBarConfig.java) (`AbstractKeyedAssetConfig`, plus `bySource(rowId)`); owner layer `mods/ziggfreedcommon/hud-bars.json` through [`HudBarOwnerLayers`](HudBarOwnerLayers.java) + `OwnerLayerReader` (the same class reads the panels and placements owner files).
- **[`HudBars`](HudBars.java) is the way in**: `install(plugin)` (attach BOTH panels at `PlayerReadyEvent` LATE, detach at disconnect, live panels in `LIVE` by uuid then by panel key, and a `HudPreferences.watch` so a player's own pick or hide repaints theirs), `panels()` / `panel(id)`, **`moved` / `movedOnGrid` for a value**, **`itemMoved(playerRef, itemId, quantity)` for an item** and the caller-named `itemMoved(playerRef, rowId, itemId, quantity, display)`, `totalled` / `itemTotalled`, `fadeAll`. `repaintAllOnline()` covers both panels for the three asset load events and every owner-file write; a repaint re-anchors as it draws, so there is no separate position push.
- **[`HudBarHud`](HudBarHud.java) draws BOTH**, reading a [`HudBarLayout`](HudBarLayout.java) for everything that differs (panel id, hud key, document, root, declared columns and slots, the widths, the document's fallback corner); the two concrete classes are that layout and nothing else. Per-player state is one `LiveBar` per row id. Paints fold per tick through `RepaintCoalescer` and are held to one per the panel's authored `RepaintMs`, a **leading edge with a trailing flush**. ONE sweep is armed at the earliest expiry. `collectLive` drops expired and switched-off rows; `slotCap` (pure, tested) holds the count to `MaxVisible` and to what the spot's columns can hold; `choose(rows, cap, declaredSlots)` takes the most recently moved rows and draws them in `STACK_ORDER`; `columnsFor` / `rowsPerColumnFor` / `usedColumnsFor` / `ordinalInColumn` (pure, tested) split them across columns, a spot deeper than the document read at the document's depth; `paint` addresses every slot of every column, fills COLUMN BY COLUMN, hides an unused column outright, pushes the root anchor at `panelWidthFor(columns)` AND an EXPLICIT height, because the panel is an absolutely anchored box inside a full-viewport wrapper (the shape a Bottom pin needs) and such a box has no content to size to. **Every in-use column has a `ColumnShape`** (`shapeOf`, pure, tested), measured FROM THE PINNED EDGE: `drawn` (its rows, held to `slotsPerColumn - cut`: a cut column has fewer usable cells and draws the ones that fit, the rest undrawn exactly like rows past the document's ceiling), `reservedPx` (its cut cells at fill-row height, PLUS the band when the cut swallows the split, `AfterRow <= cut`), `bandSlot` / `bandPx` (the band where the split falls BETWEEN its own rows, `cut < AfterRow < cut + drawn`), `rowsPx` (its rows summed one by one, a fill row `rowMarginPx + lineHeightPx + barBlockPx` and an item row without the bar block); `extentPx` is their sum and `innerHeightFor` (pure, tested) the tallest extent across in-use columns, so `panelHeightFor` = 2 x vertical padding + that, floored by the spot's `MinHeight` (a cut column is therefore the tallest and the frame grows to hold it, with empty frame above the shorter columns: wanted, not a defect). **Every column is aligned by its FIRST VISIBLE slot's margin** (`ColumnShape.leadingPx`, pure, tested): a column is a `LayoutMode: Top` stack that lays rows from its own top, so bottom-up it is pushed down by `innerHeight - extent` (a short last column of an uneven split bottom-aligns by construction) and top-down by `reservedPx` (its cut stays at the top). The band costs NO element either: `gapSlotFor(gap, cut, drawn, perColumn, bottomUp)` (pure, tested in both directions, with and without a cut) names the ONE slot per column whose row margin is pushed taller (top-down the slot holding ordinal `AfterRow - cut`, bottom-up the slot holding ordinal `AfterRow - cut - 1`, i.e. `perColumn - AfterRow + cut`; with the shipped numbers the cut column carries NO band, every one of its rows being above the split). `ColumnShape.pushAt(slot, ordinal, innerHeight, bottomUp)` (pure, tested slot by slot against the shipped 18-row picture) is what a slot is pushed by over the document's margin, the leading space and the band ADDED where they are one slot; `paintRow` pushes every visible slot's `Anchor` every paint so a stale push never survives a re-spread. **The frame's colour is one hex, pushed only when it says something**: `paint` takes the resolved `HudCardLook` (`cardLook(placement)`, the spot-or-panel colour over the shared record, read per paint) and retints the root's `.Background.Color` through `UiRetint.retintColor` only for a non-identity value, so the shipped look costs no command; the dressing inside every painted fill row FOLLOWS the card's opacity through [`HudBarDressing`](HudBarDressing.java) (`at(opacity)`: null at full opacity, else the well, the gloss and the shade at their own hue with their shipped alpha multiplied by the card's opacity, pushed on `#Track`, `#Track #Fill #Gloss` and `#Track #Fill #Base`), and `HudBarDressing`'s six constants MIRROR both documents' `@TrackWell` / `@Gloss` / `@BaseShade` (`HudBarDressingTest` holds them to the documents). The paint mirrors for the pinned edge: a RIGHT-pinned panel's first column is the rightmost, a BOTTOM-pinned column's first row is its lowest, so what is already on screen stays put when the next thing opens beside or above it. A panel the player hid paints only its own `Visible` false and keeps its rows. The engine's `UICommandBuilder` cannot be built in a unit JVM (its static init reaches `ItemStack.CODEC`), so the pushes are proven through the pure shape and the drawing itself is confirmed in game.
- **The bar is a retinted `Group`, NOT a native `ProgressBar`, and this is load-bearing.** The native linear element carries no colour knob at all and every vanilla HUD bar ships one fill image per colour; a per-category texture set is the "one file per member of an OPEN roster" trap. So the fill stays an `Anchor` width push with a `.Background.Color` retint, dressed by COLOUR-AGNOSTIC overlays in the document (a dark well, a gloss, a shade, a `#Pulse` band for `PULSE_MS` after a move). Evidence: [[hud-progressbar-no-colour-knob]].
- **Geometry lives once in each `.ui`** and every slot references it; the widths AND the heights the Java mirrors (`HudBarLayout`'s `paddingPx` / `columnWidthPx` / `columnGapPx` / `trackInnerWidthPx` and `verticalPaddingPx` / `rowMarginPx` / `lineHeightPx` / `barBlockPx`; both documents state 12 / 5 / 18 / 12 for the vertical four) are named in each document's header GEOMETRY note. Slots are positional (`#ZigBarCol<c>` holding `#ZigBarC<c>R<r>`, letters between the indices because a UI element id is `[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9]*` and an underscore bricks the client) and their counts must match the layout: the grid declares 6 columns of 9 (54 slot blocks; a column that grows gets its new blocks generated from an existing one, then the id set is checked by grep), the stack 2 of 13. Each document's wrapper is a full-viewport box (`Anchor: (Top: 0, Bottom: 0, Left: 0, Right: 0)`, NO `LayoutMode`) so the panel inside pins to the screen's real edges; a `LayoutMode: Top` wrapper would put a Bottom-pinned panel at the TOP of its flow. Both documents draw the native `ObjectivePanelContainer.png` patch, copied in beside them because a Custom UI document resolves texture paths only under `Common/UI/Custom/`.
- **Captions are SHORT, and the slot is 30px.** What goes in one is a number or a word of three or four letters (the MMO puts the level in and `MAX` at the cap, in every locale). A caption that does not fit is ellipsised by the client and reads as nonsense.
- **Consumers today**: the MMO's `skill/SkillHudBar` derives a skill's row from its skill registry and `SkillService.notifyXpGain` hands all of it over in one call, choosing the panel by whether the player is working a station and holding the row while they are; rpg-stations calls `itemMoved` for every produced or retrieved stack, the caller-named form for a lucky find, and `moved` for the session's own row.
- **Tests**: `HudBarAssetCodecTest`, `HudBarPanelAssetTest` (the leaves read back incl. `Color`, a child inherits, the `MaxVisible` clamp, a malformed colour reading as unauthored, the fold always answers), `HudBarPlacementTest` (the placement file, `fits`, the picker listing, every layer of the resolution incl. `Gap` / `Cutout` / `MinHeight` through pick, spot and inline, the colour through all four layers - shared record, spot, panel inline, player pick - and an owner entry restating one leaf of the band, the cut or the colour over the pack's file), `HudBarDressingTest` (nothing at full opacity, each overlay at its own hue and a fraction of its alpha, and the Java mirrors matching both documents), `HudBarSlotsTest` (`choose`, `slotCap`, `columnsFor`, `rowsPerColumnFor`, `usedColumnsFor`, `ordinalInColumn`, `HudBarReading.fraction`, the 3 x 6 and 3 x 9 ceilings), `HudBarHeightTest` (`rowHeightPx` / `panelHeightFor` incl. a column of bar-less rows and the floor, `gapSlotFor` in both directions with and without a cut and every no-band guard, `shapeOf` / `innerHeightFor` with a cut column as the tallest, the cut swallowing the band, a cut naming a column not in use, a cut deeper than the document and every no-cut guard, `leadingPx` for a short bottom-pinned column, a cut column bottom-pinned and top-pinned, `pushAt` adding the two pushes, and the shipped 18-row / 3-column / 9-deep / `AfterRow 3` / `Cutout {3, 3}` picture slot by slot), `HudBarLookTest`. The drawing itself is packets and is validated in game (the `.ui` is not compiled).
- **Conventions**: `@Nonnull`/`@Nullable`; `util/SafeLog`; no product vocabulary anywhere in this package (no skill, no XP, no level: a row has a label, a reading, two captions and maybe an item, nothing more - an item is an engine concept); no em-dashes.

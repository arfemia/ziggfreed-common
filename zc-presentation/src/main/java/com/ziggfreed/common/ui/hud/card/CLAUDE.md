# ui/hud/card/ - the one look every HUD card is drawn in

Router for `com.ziggfreed.common.ui.hud.card`. A card is any panel wearing a shipped frame on the [`ui/hud/`](../CLAUDE.md) `KeyedCustomHud` base (both bar panels, the tracked-quest tracker, RPG Stations' session summary), and this package is how a server owner colours and dims every one of them from ONE authored hex, without touching a `.ui`.

- **THE VALUE is one hex with an alpha, and the identity is `#ffffffff`** (maintainer, 2026-09-10). The colour is pushed on the card's root `.Background.Color` through `ui/UiRetint.retintColor`, the ONE retint primitive, and that push is a MULTIPLY over the shipped 9-slice: `#ffffff` is exactly the shipped look, a darker hex darkens, a hue tints, and an EIGHT-digit hex carries the alpha in its last two digits (`#ffffffb8` is the shipped card at about 72 percent, the pure transparency case). Absent at every layer means the shipped look and NOTHING is pushed; a malformed value is ignored with one `SafeLog` warn naming where it was authored and what it said, never a crash and never a black card. `retintColor` accepts both the six- and the eight-digit form (`UiRetint.isHex`; the eight-digit spelling is the engine's own `ColorAlpha` grammar); the button-state and patch forms stay six-digit.
- **[`HudCardLook`](HudCardLook.java) is the pure arithmetic**: `parse` (six or eight digits, hash optional, either case, else null), `authored(hex, source)` (what an asset accessor calls: the normalised hex, null for nothing, null plus ONE warn for malformed, so every fold above only sees valid-or-none), `of` / `SHIPPED`, `resolve(own, shared)` (the card's own leaf, else the shared record, else shipped), `cardColor()` (null for the identity, so the common case costs no command), `opacity()` (the alpha as a 0..1 factor), and `dimmed(rgbHex, shippedAlpha, opacity)`, the ONE derivation rule the dressing follows.
- **[`HudCardAsset`](HudCardAsset.java) is the SHARED record** at `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/HudCards/<id>.json` (Pattern A, PascalCase, one leaf `Color`, inheriting under `Parent`); the record every card reads is `Default` (`SHARED_ID`), shipped with the identity value and a `$Comment` that teaches the model. Fold: [`HudCardConfig`](HudCardConfig.java) (`shared()`, `sharedColor()`; null = shipped look); owner layer `mods/ziggfreedcommon/hud-cards.json` through [`HudCardOwnerLayers`](HudCardOwnerLayers.java) + `OwnerLayerReader`, keyed `Default`. Registered by the root's `FrameworkAssetRegistrar`; a reload repaints every bar panel and every tracker.
- **PER-CARD OVERRIDE, folded per leaf, in each card's own vocabulary.** The two bar panels: shared look, then the SPOT (`HudSpotAsset.Color`), then the PANEL inline (`HudPanelAsset.Color`), exactly the layering `Position` / `Gap` / `Cutout` / `MinHeight` use in `HudSpot.resolve` / `under`, a player's pick swapping the spot's group whole (`HudSpot.color()` is the folded answer, `HudCardLook.resolve(placement.color(), HudCardConfig.sharedColor())` the look). The tracker: shared look, then `TrackedQuestHudDeps.color` (a supplier seam the consumer fills off its own file). RPG Stations' summary: shared look, then `RpgStationsSettingsAsset.SummaryHud.Color`. Each card pushes where it already pushes its paint-time commands, so a reload lands on the next paint, and an authored card colour lands AFTER a consumer theme's build-time paint, so the owner's file is the last word and an absent value keeps the theme.
- **The bar dressing FOLLOWS the card, derived, with no leaf to author** ([`../panel/HudBarDressing`](../panel/HudBarDressing.java)): the well `#05080c(0.72)`, the gloss `#ffffff(0.22)` and the shade `#000000(0.22)` are mirrored from both bar documents; at opacity 1 `at()` answers null and nothing is pushed; at a fraction each keeps its shipped hue with its shipped alpha multiplied by the card's opacity, pushed per painted fill row on `#Track`, `#Track #Fill #Gloss` and `#Track #Fill #Base`. The card's hue never bleeds into the dressing.
- **Tests**: `HudCardLookTest` (parsing in every spelling, the identity pushing nothing, the shared-over-own fold, the derivation at 1 and at a fraction, an absent record reading shipped), `HudCardAssetTest` (the leaf reads back, a child inherits, malformed reads as unauthored), `../panel/HudBarDressingTest`, and the four-layer bar fold in `../panel/HudSpotTest`. The push itself is packets, confirmed in game (the `.ui` is not compiled).
- **Conventions**: `@Nonnull`/`@Nullable`; `util/SafeLog`; `Locale.ROOT` on every fold and format; no product vocabulary; no em-dashes.

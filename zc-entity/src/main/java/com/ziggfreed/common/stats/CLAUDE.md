# stats/ - item-carried stats as native modifiers

`com.ziggfreed.common.stats` is a split package: `StackStats`, a pure item-metadata record, lives in zc-core so any module can stamp a stack. Gear sets (`gearset/`) keep their own router.

- `stats/` and zc-core's `counter/` never merge: an item-carried stat lives here, a per-subject tally in `counter/`, and a tally that must reach a stat channel is mirrored onto it with `StatMirror`.
- `StackStats.Entries` stores percent channels in whole percent points (`10.0` is +10%) and flat channels raw; which family a channel belongs to is its owner's business. `merge` is the one summing authority, and `stampReplacingWithCount` writes the count it is given (it never increments).
- A retired stat id lives on in zc-core's `StatAliases` (old id to one or more successors, each taking the old value in full, or times the alias's scale for one whose successor counts in another unit, `registerScaled`): `StackStats.entriesOf` and `merge` normalize through it, so a saved roll applies under the new ids and a restamped stack stores them. A consumer registers its own retired ids at setup and routes any other place it reads or writes a stat id by name through `StatAliases.resolve`, times `StatAliases.scale`.
- The engine (`StatModifiersManager`) never applies a held item's own `Utility.StatModifiers`, so the bridge does. It does apply the utility-slot (offhand) item's, so the bridge applies only that item's `StackStats`, or the bonus doubles.
- The library installs the ONE bridge (`EntityBootstrap.installEquipStatBridge`, namespace `ziggfreedcommon`) and its three trigger subclasses. ECS systems are class-keyed, so never install a second bridge or register your own triggers: read it through `EntityBootstrap.equipStatBridge()` and hang derived work on `addAppliedListener`, which runs after every recompute (keep it cheap and idempotent).
- `EquipStatBridge.equippedSnapshot(store, ref)` answers what the entity has on by item id (`EquippedSnapshot`), through the same container reads the stat walkers use, so a listener never re-derives the slot layout.
- `planUtility` and its `UtilityPlan` / `UtilityPut` / `StatKey` types are public and permanent (2.2.0): the bridge and a gear-set tier share one diff-skip and stale-key core so they agree on what "stale" means.
- The library recomputes every player through the bridge at a LATE `PlayerReadyEvent` (`GearSets.onPlayerReady`); a consumer adds no ready-time `recomputeAll` of its own.
- The triggers ride only non-deprecated inventory events, never the `Legacy*ChangeStatSystem` family.
- Touch an `EntityStatMap` only through keyed `putModifier`/`removeModifier`, never `setStatValue` or `addStatValue`.
- Resolve a stat index through `StatIndexCache`, not `util.AssetIndexCache`: a custom channel can legitimately sit at index 0, which `AssetIndexCache` answers as unresolved.
- Run `StatChannelAudit` once, late (the first `PlayerReadyEvent`), after dynamically registered channels exist.
- The `ReputationGain` stat (whole percent points, Min -100) ships here as JSON only: zc-reputation reads it, with a pack's own `ReputationGain_<Id>`, at its one writer (`ReputationGain.scale`), so no code here knows it.
- The SK6d stats (R706, R648, R734) ship here as JSON only, each read by its one owner elsewhere: `MoveSpeed`, `KnockbackResist` and their `*Max` ceilings by zc-effects' `effect/movement/`, `Regen_Health` and `Regen_Mana` by zc-core's `health/StatRegenSystem`, `HealingPower` by zc-core's `HealthUtil.heal(store, healer, target, amount)`, `TrackGain` and `TrackGainMax` by zc-track's `gain/BonusGainFactor`.
- Every living entity with an `EntityStatMap` carries these stats, a creature as much as a player (R860): every per-entity pass of a stat family (zc-effects' stat-to-effect sync and effect-length pass, the conditional holder pass) asks `StatCarriers` (a player every tick, a creature every `CREATURE_EVERY_TICKS` world ticks, staggered by entity index), and every per-hit step reads any attacker. Never add a player-only check to one; a consumer's rule that is about a player's grants (the MMO's world combat rule) answers for players alone.
- Crits and lifesteal (R865, R866) are `com.ziggfreed.common.damage.onhit`, JSON stats here: `CritChance`, `CritDamage` (base 50, a crit deals 1.5 times) and `Lifesteal`, whole percent points. `CritSystem` (Filter, after `WeakSpotSystem`) rolls once per direct hit of any attacker: the chance stat, every conditional stat whose file `Pays.CritChance` and counts on the hit (`ConditionalStats.critPoints`) and every `Crits.registerChanceSource`; a crit multiplies by one plus the crit damage (`registerDamageSource` added), marks the hit (`Crits.hitKey`), fires `ZigCritEvent` (zc-track's Crit procs hear it) and shows `WeakSpotSettings`' `CritText` word to the attacker alone (R896), unless their own Settings switch is off (`Crits.installWordRule`, which the wiring root fills with `PlayerSettings.critWord`; the owner's `CritText.Enabled` stays the switch for everyone). `LifestealSystem` (Inspect, once the hit is applied) heals the attacker that share through `HealthUtil.heal` with no healer (a drain) and fires `ZigLifestealEvent`. One consumer `HitRule` each (`installHitRule`) refuses a hit; a consumer never rolls a crit or heals a percent lifesteal of its own. `RoutedStats` is the one routed-source registry the weak-spot, crit and lifesteal sources share; `HitWords` the one combat-text helper.
- The weak-spot stats `WeakSpotDamage` and its ceiling `WeakSpotMax` (R708), each way's own `WeakSpotDamage_Head`, `_Back` and `_Staggered`, and `WeakSpot_FoeLow` (a health percent at or under which every hit is a weak spot) ship here as JSON only, read by this module's `com.ziggfreed.common.damage.weakspot` step (`WeakSpotSystem`), which pays one weak-spot bonus a hit (a head box, the rear arc, an effect tagged `ZigWeakSpot` or listed in `WeakSpotSettings`, a foe low enough) with each matching way's stat and every consumer source (`WeakSpots.registerSource`, a weapon skill's own on the hits its line counts on) added in, and never on top of a vanilla `Head` TargetedDamage headshot or rear `AngledDamage` backstab branch (the larger of the two, decided once a hit for every cause, `VanillaWeakSpots`). The `WeakSpot` grant kind retired into these stats (R757): a weak-spot rule is a stat, never a grant.
- `condition/` is conditional stats (R757): an ordinary entity stat made conditional by its `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/ConditionalStats/<Stat>.json` file (`ConditionalStatAsset`: `Holder`, `Target` and `Hit` conditions, `PerFoe`, `Max`, and what it `Pays`; `Max` holds every payout, since the engine never clamps the stat maximum its modifiers raise, R761), folded `defaults < pack < owner` (`conditional-stats.json`). The conditions are the engine's own entity `Condition`s plus the library's, registered into `Condition.CODEC` (`ZigFoesNear`, `ZigPlayersNear`, `ZigNight`, `ZigOpenSky`, `ZigHeight`, `ZigStatPercent`, `ZigMoving`, `ZigCrouching`, `ZigFoeFamily`, `ZigEffectFamily`, `ZigHolding`), so an effect's `ApplyConditions` may name them too. Who is near is `Nearby`'s one walk (foes, allies, players); an ally is zc-core's `Allies` rule (R859: the party, else other players while PvP is off; a creature has none), so `ZigPlayersNear` counts allies and every "allies near" reads `Nearby.alliesAround`. `ZigStatPercent` and `hytale:stat_percent` read one range (`HytaleFactors.percentOf`, the engine's min-to-max). Holder conditions are read a few times a second per holder (`ConditionalStatSystems.HolderPass`: every player, and a creature once it holds one of these stats above 0) and cached; a hit reads only the cache, its target and the hit (`HitReads`, `FirstHits`). A damage payout is one route term of the per-type bonus step (`TypedDamageBonuses.registerRouteTerm`), held under `DamageBonusMax`; a `CritChance` payout is the crit step's (`ConditionalStats.critPoints`); `Like` follows another stat's line (`TypedDamageBonuses.countsOn`); a healing payout is zc-core's `HealthUtil` healing term; `Onto` writes one `zigtrack:@cond:<stat>` modifier per stat it names, only on a change, and a player's first pass sweeps any saved before; the key sits under the track grants' owner because it lands on stats 1.7.x knows (`DamageResist_<Type>`), so the 1.7.x rollback sweep of `zigtrack:` keys clears it (R755), and the stat grant writer spares every `zigtrack:@` key (`StatGrantWriter.isRuntimeKey`: this fold's and a counter's `zigtrack:@counter:`; no track id holds an `@`, so no grant key collides). Any other saved modifier on a stat 1.7.x knows takes `zigtrack:` the same way. A foe family is a native NPC group (`Zig_Foe_<Family>`, `Server/NPC/Groups/`), since a role carries no `Tags`; `Zig_Boss_Or_Elite` beside them holds, as one include list, every boss and elite the families exclude (asked with `Inverse` to keep them out), and neither side names the other, since the engine refuses a group that reaches itself through its own descent; an effect family is `EffectFamilyAsset` (vanilla's listed, a mod's own through its `ZigEffectFamily` tag), whose `Duration` group zc-effects' `effect/duration/` reads. `StatTypeCeiling` is the boot audit's count of loaded stat types (R759): every living entity ticks a slot for each, so a new family is merged into an old one before it is added.
- The gathering stats ship here as JSON only, each read by zc-world's `world/gather/` steps (that router lists them): speed, care, thrift, growth (`GrowthSpeed` and its ceiling `GrowthSpeedMax`) and yield (`GatherYield`, its consumer-named lines `GatherYield_Fishing` and `GatherYield_Harvesting`, and its `_While<Condition>` forms with their `ConditionalStats` files). Their ids are frozen (R861).
- The proc families (R758) ship here as JSON only, read by zc-track's `proc/` alone: `ProcChance`, `ProcStrength`, `ProcCooldownCut` and its ceiling `ProcCooldownCutMax`, `CounterSize`. `condition/` also serves the procs: `Hit.Headshot` (the weapon's own head branch, `WeakSpots.headBranch`), the `ZigCrouching` condition, and `Nearby.foesWithin` (nearest first), so a proc reads a hit and a foe exactly as a conditional stat does.
- `damage/` is the per-type damage bonus (R245, R567): a damage type is any loaded cause with a loaded `DamageBonus_<Type>` stat (`DamageTypes`, read off the files, never a list, R858; a pack's cause or Element ships its own file), and the library's `DamageBonus_<Type>` (whole percent points), `DamageBonusFlat_<Type>` and the every-type `DamageBonus` stats and every source a consumer registers (`TypedDamageBonuses.registerSource`, a type or `EVERY_TYPE`, with an optional gate asked only of a source reading nonzero) reach a hit as vanilla adds Enrage: the PERCENT stats through the ENGINE's outgoing step, from the native effects zc-effects' `effect/outgoing/` keeps in step per carrier (each type's sum held to the attacker's `DamageBonusMax`); the FLAT stats per hit in `TypedDamageBonusSystem`, on a direct hit only, never a `DoT` tick. Never sum the percents per hit as well: a fold that did would pay the same points twice. `TypedDamageBonusSystem` (any attacker) also adds the route lines: a `registerRouteSource` line counts on the hits its `Route` names (a Hit tag, a projectile). Both join the hit's engine sum through `NativeOutgoing.addToSum`, the one helper any later step adding a percent or a flat uses, so additions compose as `(a + flat) * (1 + P)`; a consumer fold that must see them orders itself AFTER the system class. `NativeOutgoing` reads the engine's own sum (`of`, `snapshot` for a tooltip). One consumer rule may switch every bonus off for an attacker (`installAttackerGate`, the MMO's per-world combat-bonus rule); with none installed every bonus counts.
- `damage/DamageResistances` is the per-type resistance family: `DamageResist_<Type>` (whole percent points, base `Max` 0) for every loaded cause with such a file (`DamageTypes.resistTypes`: the damage types and the resist-only `Fall`), the every-type `DamageResist` (every damage type, so never `Fall`; a conditional stat's `Onto` folds into it), a consumer's own stats counted toward a type or `EVERY_TYPE` (`registerSource`, with an optional hold), the rolled-landing stat `RollLanding`, and the ceiling stat `DamageResistMax` (its asset's `Max` is the default ceiling), which bounds a type's SUMMED resistance, worn armor and every other effect on the key included. Nothing here applies it: zc-effects' `effect/resist/` keeps the engine's resistance in step, keyed on exactly the type's own cause (R674: `Physical` does not reduce a Crush hit, as armor's does not), and no damage step reads these stats. Its seams are `installGate` and `installCeiling`. `damage/NativeResistance` reads the engine's own sum (`percentFor`, `of`, `percentsByKey`, through the engine's armor merge) and carries a hit's armor penetration (`setPenetration`, a `Damage` meta key): a page or a fold reads resistance there, never by adding the stats up.

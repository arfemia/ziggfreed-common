# effect/resist/ - per-type damage resistance on the engine

- The one applier of zc-entity's `DamageResist_<Type>` stats. Its `DamageResistReconciler.CHANNEL` on the one stat-to-effect pass (`effect/sync/`) compares each living carrier's readings, a creature's as a player's (R860; the stat, the every-type `DamageResist` for a damage type, and their registered sources, `DamageResistances.pointsFor`, for every resistance type the loaded files name) with its `Zig_DamageResist_*` effects after the engine's `EntityStatsSystems.Recalculate` (a worn or utility-slot item's stats land there, so an equip listener reads them a tick early) and reconciles only a difference.
- A bucket is held to its key's headroom: `DamageResistMax` less what worn armor and every other effect resist there, read off the engine's own table (`NativeResistance.percentsByKey`, our share taken out first), never past 99 (R240). A body keys exactly the type's own cause, never its children (R674): a Crush hit is not reduced by `Physical`, as armor's is not.
- `ResistAfterArmorSystem`, after the engine's `ArmorDamageReduction`, applies a hit's armor penetration (`NativeResistance.setPenetration`), a rolled landing's `RollLanding` share on a Fall hit (the engine's own landing queue entry decides the roll) and the 1-point minimum while our share is in a sum under a whole hit. `ResistAfterArmor.evaluate` is the same reading for a consumer that tells a player what our share took.
- A consumer adds only rules and sources (`DamageResistances.installGate`, `installCeiling`, `registerSource`) and calls `sync` after its own write; it never applies these effects or keeps a second copy.

# effect/control/ - the family's crowd control (R740)

- `ControlLanding` is the one way any control lands on a foe, whoever deals it (a proc's `ApplyEffect`, `ZigStun`, a consumer's ability or minigame): the foe's rules (zc-entity's `BossRules`, a talent's asked as one, `Landing.talent`), its immunity, the unowned landing on a boss or an elite (R672), the effect families' Duration rules, the shrink by the foe's Immunity share and the Immunity ramp, every one read off data: each kind's row (zc-entity's `ControlKinds`, the `ControlKinds/` files and the Control Kinds owner form) and the Crowd Control settings (the `Control_Settings` form), never a per-kind branch or a number in code. A step may override its kinds' shrink and ramp (`Landing.shrinks`, `ramp`), a fight's own stun passes the immunity (`ignoringImmunity`), a fixed control (its effect tagged `"ZigControl": ["Fixed"]`) skips what the settings' `Fixed` says, and a consumer whose one cast lands several controls on one foe keeps one `Tally.perCast()` on the cast and hands it to every landing (one share, one step per foe per cast, `CastRecord` its pure half), never a per-foe map of its own. `ControlLanding.steps` is the pure decision; `refuses` is the gate alone for a move that holds a foe its own way (`CreatureHold.canMove`). Never a second ramp, immunity check or boss check beside it.
- `StunControl` is the bare hold under a stun (stun, stagger, wake, the immunity and ramp pieces the landing reads) and `AggroControl` (taunt, drop, suppress), with the `ZigStun`, `ZigTaunt`, `ZigAggroDrop` and `ZigAggroSuppress` Types, registered through zc-cast's `InteractionTypes` (`ControlBootstrap.specs`). A stun is any effect of the `Stunned` family: `isStunned`, `isFullStun`, `remainingSeconds` and `wake` read the family, so a mod's own stun effect (built on the game's `Stun`, tagged `ZigEffectFamily` Stunned) is seen by every reader; a stagger never cuts a full stun short.
- Immunity to stuns is `StunControl.isImmune`, the one read the landing asks: the game's `Immune` effect, or an effect tagged `ZigStunImmune` (a boss only its fight may stun; no look of its own; `Zig_Stun_Immune` is the library's). A fight's own stun writes past it (`ZigStun`'s `IgnoreImmune`, the landing's `ignoringImmunity`). The game's own stuns never ask, so the tag's values name the effects a wearer sheds (`Stun`, `Root`) and `StunImmunitySystem` takes them off the tick they land, before the tick's effect changes go out (`StunImmunity` reads the table off the effect files); only the ids named, never their children, so the library's own stun effects stay. A rule effect the wearer has on (a fight's window or phase, zc-entity's `BossRules.opened`) opens the kinds it says land from anyone: `isImmune(accessor, target, kindMask)` gives way for those kinds, and the shedding keeps such an effect while the window lasts, so a tripped boss takes slows and roots; the game's `Immune` (the diminishing ramp) is never opened, and the two-argument `isImmune` opens nothing.
- Whether a control lands on a foe is zc-entity's `BossRules` (per boss and per phase, R1122), asked here at every site: the landing, `CreatureHold.canMove` (a Carry, through `ControlLanding.refuses`), `AggroControl.tauntOne` (its pin's kind, `TAUNT` or `DECOY`, asked as the pin is set and every sweep after: `AggroSweepSystem` ends a pin whose creature comes to shrug it and passes a creature that shrugs a taunt over in every taunt window), and `StunImmunitySystem`, which also takes off every worn effect whose families' kinds (`EffectFamilies.controlKindMask`) the wearer's rules shrug, whoever put it on (the game's own included), free while no loaded rule shrugs a kind any family names (`BossRules.mayShrugMask`), and tells its owner the shrug word (`BossRules.shrugged`). A shrug holds against every source: `StunControl`'s stun and stagger refuse a target whose rules shrug a Stun (a fight's own `ZigStunTarget` included, so no hold lands without its effect), and `ShruggedKnockbackSystem` empties to an Add of nothing, right before the engine's `KnockbackSystems.ApplyKnockback`, the knockback waiting on a creature whose rules shrug a Push (never a Set of zero, which stops it dead). A kind going back to usual leaves what already landed to run out. Never ask `Zig_Boss_Or_Elite` inline here.
- An aggro window lives in the world's `AggroWindows` resource, never on the source entity; its attitudes go through the world's `AttitudeView` provider (`AggroAttitudes`), never a write into a creature's override memory, and `AggroSweepSystem` only edits fields of components a creature already has.
- `CreatureHold` is the one way a grab or a drive moves a creature (a per-tick `Set` velocity instruction toward a point, never the zero vector, never a raw component write) and `CreatureHold.canMove` the one rule for whom it may take: never a player, one the landing's gate refuses a Carry (by the Carry kind's default every boss and elite, and an immune one), one whose role's `KnockbackScale` is 0, or one taller than the caller's cap.
- `AggroControl.tauntOne` pins one creature onto one taunter (zc-track's proc `Taunt`), kept beside the windows in the same resource and swept with them, ahead of any window's taunt on that creature; a creature takes a pin only as it takes an area taunt (its own attitude hostile or neutral, recorded on the pin, which alone makes the provider answer hostile), a pin ends with its time or its taunter (dead or gone), and when it ends the creature's default target slot is cleared only if it still holds the taunter. Never a second taunt mechanism elsewhere. `AggroControl.tauntedBy` is the one read of "a creature this taunter holds": a pin it took, or its target slot on the taunter while their area taunt is open.

# effect/movement/ - speed and knockback on the engine

- The one applier of the `MoveSpeed` and `KnockbackResist` stats (R706), shaped like `effect/resist/`: its `MovementReconciler.CHANNEL` on the one stat-to-effect pass (`effect/sync/`) keeps each living carrier's `Zig_Movement_Speed_<pct>` (native `HorizontalSpeedMultiplier`, held to -90 and `MoveSpeedMax`; a creature's movement reads it too, `NPCEntity.getCurrentHorizontalSpeedMultiplier`, R860) and `Zig_Movement_Knockback_<pct>` (native `KnockbackMultiplier`, held to `KnockbackResistMax`) in step with the stats, through `RuntimeEffectGrid.carriesExactly` then a reconcile only on a difference.
- `ThrowSpeeds` is the one applier of the `ThrowSpeed` stat: a projectile the game's `LaunchProjectile` throws (the legacy `ProjectileComponent`, a spear, a potion) is noted as it spawns with its thrower's stat and sped once its first physics step has written the launch speed onto its `Velocity` component, which each later step reads back first (the spawn hides the launch speed inside the deprecated `SimplePhysicsProvider`, which nothing here calls). A projectile config's own launch (an arrow, a bomb) is left alone; a consumer that launches one itself and wants a speed stat to move it reads `ThrowSpeeds.factorOf(stats, statId)`, the one rule (whole percent points, held to the same floor and ceiling), never its own.
- A consumer raises the stats and never applies these effects.

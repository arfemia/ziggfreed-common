# zc-effects

- zc-effects and zc-loot never import each other: a reward that grants an effect is the `Effect` reward kind the wiring root registers (`reward/EffectRewardKind`).
- `NativeEffectUtil.has` answers false whenever it cannot tell, so a caller reconciling a wanted effect applies on false; `remaining` answers null for the same, read as not on. `applyInfinite` is the no-expiry overload for an entity with no `EntityStatMap` (a puppet or prop, where the engine's effect timer never runs): pair it with `remove`.
- An unresolved effect id is named at WARN once per id per process (`warnUnresolvedOnce`, from `apply` / `applyFor` / `remove`) and at FINE after that, so a caller reconciling on every change never repeats the line.
- An asset-authoritative or no-expiry apply goes to the engine through `EffectAdds` (`asAuthored`, `infinite`), never a raw `addEffect` or `addInfiniteEffect`: the engine sends viewers an Add on every infinite add, and the client starts another set of the effect's particles for each (a re-applied aura dragged a trail). A timed effect still reaches the engine on every call, which refreshes or extends it.
- `AppliedEffectTracker.removeAll` clears unconditionally, and a round or encounter `stop()` relies on that.
- `effect/costume/` is the costume a player can always take off; zc-cast is this module's one other library edge, for its Type (its own router).
- `effect/buff/` is the timed-buff binding and the `Zig_Buff_*` slot pools (its own router).
- `effect/grid/` carries a per-entity number through a native effect field that only reads assets, one shared asset per bucket (its own router).
- `effect/sync/` is the one stat-to-effect pass (`StatEffectSync`, R860): every carrier with stats and effects, a player every tick and a creature on zc-entity's `StatCarriers` cadence, runs each registered `StatEffectChannel` (outgoing, resist, movement), which owns its grid, the in-step check, the reconcile, the one failure line, `sync` and `removeAll`; a family writes only its `wanted` buckets and registers its channel from its bootstrap. Never add a second sync system for a family.
- `effect/control/` is the family's crowd control: the one landing every control takes (`ControlLanding`), stuns and aggro (R740; its own router).
- `effect/resist/` is the one applier of zc-entity's `DamageResist_<Type>` stats, with the after-armor step (its own router).
- `effect/movement/` is the one applier of `MoveSpeed` and `KnockbackResist` (its own router).
- `effect/outgoing/` is the one applier of the per-type percent damage bonuses (its own router). This module's edge to zc-entity (`api`: `AggroControl.tauntOne` takes a `ControlKind`) is for these three stat families, the effect families below and the crowd-control rules `effect/control/` asks (`BossRules`).
- `effect/warcry/` is the war cry carried by an effect (`WarCries` reads an effect's `ZigWarCry*` tags, `WarCrySystem` shouts and rallies): a cry is authored on the effect, never a role, so an omen, an affix or any `ApplyEffect` gives it to any creature; the rally waits on `ZigScheduler` (never a sleep or a system queue) and checks every creature again on the world thread before applying.
- `effect/DamageOverTime` puts a damage-over-time effect (one authoring its own `DamageCalculator`) on for an owner at a strength: the engine ticks it, credited to the owner, a repeat adds a stack up to a cap and starts its time again (one instance per entity and effect, the stacks as its damage multiplier), and its length goes through the families' Duration rules as it lands (`EffectDurations.lengthAsLanded`, `markLanded`), so a refresh that shortens what is left still counts as a landing.
- `effect/landing/` is the one watch on effects landing, refreshing and ending (`EffectLandings`): the engine has no landing moment, so one pass looks at every living carrier (a player every tick, a creature on the carrier cadence, and every tick a creature a hit or a control landed on in the last two seconds, `EffectLandings.watch`) and hands each look to its readers (`EffectLandings.Watcher`, registered from their own bootstraps): the length pass below, and zc-track's effect moments. A new reader of "an effect landed" registers a watcher; never a second poller.
- `effect/duration/` changes how long an effect lasts as it lands, by stats an effect family's `Duration` group names (zc-entity's `EffectFamilyAsset`: the owner's `AddedBy` seconds, the carrier's `CutBy` percent under `CutCap`), held to its kinds of control's `MaxSeconds` (zc-entity's `ControlKinds`); a fixed control (an effect tagged `"ZigControl": ["Fixed"]`) skips the cut and the added seconds as the Crowd Control settings say. `EffectDurations` reads each effect through the landing watch the first time it is seen active and again whenever its remaining time grew (a refresh), and rewrites the remaining time once through the engine's own `addEffect` with the owner kept. A timed effect a caster puts on a target lands through `EffectDurations.landOwned` (the rules read as it lands, then marked), never a raw `addEffect` beside it; a control on a foe lands through `effect/control/`'s `ControlLanding`, which lands through it. A look-only effect whose damage a mod deals on its own clock never joins a family with a `Duration` rule, or its look outlasts its damage.

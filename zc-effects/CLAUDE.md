# zc-effects

- zc-effects and zc-loot never import each other: a reward that grants an effect is the `Effect` reward kind the wiring root registers (`reward/EffectRewardKind`).
- `NativeEffectUtil.has` answers false whenever it cannot tell, so a caller reconciling a wanted effect applies on false; `remaining` answers null for the same, read as not on. `applyInfinite` is the no-expiry overload for an entity with no `EntityStatMap` (a puppet or prop, where the engine's effect timer never runs): pair it with `remove`.
- An unresolved effect id is named at WARN once per id per process (`warnUnresolvedOnce`, from `apply` / `applyFor` / `remove`) and at FINE after that, so a caller reconciling on every change never repeats the line.
- An asset-authoritative or no-expiry apply goes to the engine through `EffectAdds` (`asAuthored`, `infinite`), never a raw `addEffect` or `addInfiniteEffect`: the engine sends viewers an Add on every infinite add, and the client starts another set of the effect's particles for each (a re-applied aura dragged a trail). A timed effect still reaches the engine on every call, which refreshes or extends it.
- `AppliedEffectTracker.removeAll` clears unconditionally, and a round or encounter `stop()` relies on that.
- `effect/costume/` is the costume a player can always take off; zc-cast is this module's one other library edge, for its Type (its own router).
- `effect/buff/` is the timed-buff binding and the `Zig_Buff_*` slot pools (its own router).
- `effect/grid/` carries a per-entity number through a native effect field that only reads assets, one shared asset per bucket (its own router).
- `effect/control/` is the family's crowd control, stuns and aggro (R740; its own router).
- `effect/resist/` is the one applier of zc-entity's `DamageResist_<Type>` stats, with the after-armor step (its own router).
- `effect/movement/` is the one applier of `MoveSpeed` and `KnockbackResist` (its own router).
- `effect/outgoing/` is the one applier of the per-type percent damage bonuses (its own router). This module's edge to zc-entity is for these three stat families and the effect families below.
- `effect/DamageOverTime` puts a damage-over-time effect (one authoring its own `DamageCalculator`) on for an owner at a strength: the engine ticks it, credited to the owner, a repeat adds a stack up to a cap and starts its time again (one instance per entity and effect, the stacks as its damage multiplier), and its length goes through the families' Duration rules as it lands (`EffectDurations.lengthAsLanded`, `markLanded`), so a refresh that shortens what is left still counts as a landing.
- `effect/duration/` changes how long an effect lasts as it lands, by stats an effect family's `Duration` group names (zc-entity's `EffectFamilyAsset`: the owner's `AddedBy` seconds, the carrier's `CutBy` percent under `CutCap`). The engine has no landing moment, so `EffectDurations` reads each effect the first time it is seen active and again whenever its remaining time grew (a refresh), on every living player and on whatever a player's hit struck in the last two seconds, and rewrites the remaining time once through the engine's own `addEffect` with the owner kept. A look-only effect whose damage a mod deals on its own clock never joins a family with a `Duration` rule, or its look outlasts its damage.

# health/

- `HealthUtil.scaleMaxHealth` is add-only: it no-ops while its keyed MAX modifier exists and heals to the new max exactly once, which `EncounterScaling`'s first apply relies on. `reconcileMaxHealth` converges that modifier to the current factor without a second heal, for a spawn hook that re-derives the scale on chunk reload. Never fold one into the other.
- Heal after a modifier write only through `fillToMax` (an Add by the gap), never `EntityStatMap.maximizeStatValue`: on a shared stat the engine rewrites the queued `PutModifier` into the Maximize (`EntityStatMap.tryMergeUpdate`), so viewers never get the modifier and see an empty bar on a living mob (`HealthFillTest`). Any family code filling a raised pool calls it, as zc-dialogue's `applyFortify` does.
- Both have `Holder` overloads for a pre-add `onEntityAdd` spawn hook, where no `Ref` exists yet.
- `HealthUtil.heal(store, healer, target, amount)` is the one healing applier (R648): every heal a player gives passes the giver, so their `HealingPower` stat raises it (`scaledHeal`); a drain or a regeneration passes no healer. Never add a heal of your own with `addStatValue`. A heal with a giver that finds room fires `ZigHealGivenEvent` as it lands, before the health moves (through `HEAL_EVENTS`, behind its listener check), so every reader of heals given (zc-track's HealGiven procs, zc-objectives' `HEAL_GIVEN` producer and the healing XP it feeds) hears one door; a heal that bypasses this applier is a heal nobody hears. A heal that should earn a skill names its `source` (the five-argument form: a proc its talent's track, a consumer's spell the skill it trains), carried on the event for a track source's `When.Qualifier`. A tick of a heal over time passes `overTime` (the six-argument form), carried as `ZigHealGivenEvent.overTime`: healing credit still pays it, a HealGiven proc never hears it.
- `RecentHostileDamage` is the one reading of what creatures' hits took from a player in the last ten seconds (R788), the most a heal given is ever credited for (`draw`, which takes it down): filled only by `HostileDamageSystems.Taken` (Inspect group, the hit as applied, its source a non-player NPC: its blow, projectile or damage-over-time effect), never a fall, fire, a self-hit or a player's hit; held to the health the player is missing (every applied hit, any source, first clamps it to what they were missing before it, and every heal before it draws), so health a potion or regeneration gave back is never credited; forgotten at death, a world change and a disconnect (`HealCreditBootstrap`). `HostileDamagePool` is its pure, bounded half (`HostileDamagePoolTest`). Never pay healing credit past it.
- `StatRegenSystem` (registered by `RegenBootstrap`) is the one payer of `Regen_Health` and `Regen_Mana` (percent of max a second, R706); health pauses `StatRegen.HEALTH_PAUSE` after any hit, read off the engine's `DamageDataComponent` clock, mana never.
- Every put goes through `wholeMaxFactor`, so a scaled max is a whole number: damage lands in whole points, and a fractional max leaves a living mob on a sliver a rounded readout shows as 0. Keep any new scale seam on it.

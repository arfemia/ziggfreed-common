# zc-reputation

Standing with a faction or one NPC, on Hytale's native reputation system.

- A reputation IS a native `ReputationGroup` (`Server/NPC/Reputation/Groups/<Id>.json`); `ReputationAsset` (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Reputations/<Owner>/<Id>.json`) is zc's companion keyed by the same id, matched without regard to case. The engine's maps are keyed exactly, so every native call takes the engine's own spelling (`ReputationDef.id()`), never the authored one.
- zc ships the one global ladder (`Server/NPC/Reputation/Ranks/`, seven files). The engine reads ranks and groups once at start: a change needs a restart. A group with no `NPCGroups` key, or one naming an unloaded NPC group, makes the engine throw on every NPC add.
- A reputation may add ranks of its own above the shared top: a `Ranks` entry whose id is not a shared rank, with a `From` floor above the top's. `ReputationLadder.of(global, floors)` is that reputation's ladder and `ReputationService.ladderOf` / `ladderFor` hand it to every reading: the factor, the rank watch and `REPUTATION_RANK` credit, the bar, the page, Beyond (past its own top) and the audit; `ladder()` is the shared ranks alone. A tier the audit reports as an ERROR (`ReputationValidator`'s javadoc lists the cases) is ignored, so the shared floors, and every NPC's attitude with them, never move.
- A `reputation_rank` lock line words itself: `ReputationFactors.contribute` registers a `FactorNames` param namer that composes `factor.rank_with` from the rank's name with that reputation and the reputation's `TitleKey`. A pack's `ParamNames` Keys entry for an exact Param still wins, and a reputation with no `TitleKey` keeps the generic line.
- Every engine call goes through `ReputationNative` (production `EngineReputationNative`; tests `FakeReputationNative`). `ReputationService.change` is the only zc writer: the worn gain, the cap, the engine's `changeReputation`, then the fan-out (change event, the bar, Beyond parked) and a rank check. The bar is the World bar, moved only for a change made with no custom page open (`EngineReputationFanOut.movesBar`, R290, its javadoc the reference); a change made under a page shows only in the toast of the action that paid it, so a page that pays reputation lists its receipt there (`ReputationRewardKind.chipFor`, the amount that actually moved). The bar is labelled with the reputation's name alone, never its rank. The admin `/reputation` writes the native value directly and raises nothing; every reading still reads the native value.
- Standing is earned standing alone (the `effective` accessors equal it): gear never adds standing. It speeds gains instead: `ReputationService.change` raises a POSITIVE delta by `ReputationGain` plus `ReputationGain_<Id>` (`<Id>` the engine's spelling of the group id) through the pure `ReputationGain.scale`, before the Cap (`ReputationGain`'s javadoc is the arithmetic); a loss is never scaled, and Beyond counts the scaled gain like any other (R370).
- Beyond is collected (M428, R221): a crossing parks on the player's `ReputationComponent` (`ZiggfreedCommon:Reputation`; its javadoc holds the high-water rule, so a fall and a climb back pay nothing twice) and raises `Reputation_Beyond`; the page's Collect (`ReputationBeyond.collect`) pays the CURRENT Rewards once per owed step, 20 a press, refused whole without bag room. `Beyond.Collect: false` pays on crossing instead, for a third party's file. A switched-off reputation or one with no Beyond keeps its record and shows nothing.
- Ranks are self-healing (`ReputationRankWatch`, its javadoc the reference): a check runs on every change and once at login, never on an equip (gear moves no rank), so a write outside zc heals at the next of either; it credits through `ReputationLadder.credited` and `ZigReputationRanksHeldEvent`, which zc-objectives turns into `REPUTATION_RANK`; only a rise during play raises the `Reputation_Rank` notice.
- A kill's change runs on a later world task, never inside the death system that fired the moment.
- `page/` is the Reputation page: `ReputationView` decides its rows; what a rank opens comes from zc-progression's `GatedContent` (zc-commerce lists its shop offers there at setup), so this module never imports commerce; `ReputationPage` paints that into `ZigReputationPage.ui` in the shared menu frame with the kit's painters and decides nothing.
- Off means absent: `$Enabled` in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/reputation.json` (read at setup and with the companions) or a companion's `Enabled: false` makes the reputation unknown (factors null, rewards refused, no page row); native standing is untouched.
- `ReputationStandingReader` is the `Reputation` stat's native reader (registered in zc-core's `counter/NativeReaders`; its javadoc says what it reads, through the service), so the stat board's `Reputation/*` line gives each switched-on reputation its own column. The stat file (`Stats/Reputation.json`) ships here, with the reader.
- Never import zc-objectives here: it depends on this module, not the reverse.

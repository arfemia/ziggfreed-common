# zc-reputation

Standing with a faction or one NPC, on Hytale's native reputation system.

- A reputation IS a native `ReputationGroup` (`Server/NPC/Reputation/Groups/<Id>.json`); `ReputationAsset` (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Reputations/<Owner>/<Id>.json`) is zc's companion keyed by the same id, matched without regard to case. The engine's maps are keyed exactly, so every native call takes the engine's own spelling (`ReputationDef.id()`), never the authored one.
- zc ships the one global ladder (`Server/NPC/Reputation/Ranks/`, seven files). The engine reads ranks and groups once at start: a change needs a restart. A group with no `NPCGroups` key, or one naming an unloaded NPC group, makes the engine throw on every NPC add.
- A reputation may add ranks of its own above the shared top: a `Ranks` entry whose id is not a shared rank, with a `From` floor above the top's. `ReputationLadder.of(global, floors)` is that reputation's ladder and `ReputationService.ladderOf` / `ladderFor` hand it to every reading: the factor, the rank watch and `REPUTATION_RANK` credit, the bar, the page, Beyond (past its own top) and the audit; `ladder()` is the shared ranks alone. A `From` on a shared rank, at or below the top's floor, or on another tier's floor is an audit ERROR and ignored, so the shared floors, and every NPC's attitude with them, never move. A second tier whose id another holds in another case is an ERROR and ignored too (ids match without case). An own rank with no `Name` is a WARNING (`TIER_WITHOUT_NAME`): it would read as its id.
- A `reputation_rank` lock line words itself: `ReputationFactors.contribute` registers a `FactorNames` param namer that composes `factor.rank_with` from the rank's name with that reputation and the reputation's `TitleKey`. A pack's `ParamNames` Keys entry for an exact Param still wins, and a reputation with no `TitleKey` keeps the generic line.
- Every engine call goes through `ReputationNative` (production `EngineReputationNative`; tests `FakeReputationNative`). `ReputationService.change` is the only zc writer: the worn gain, the cap, the engine's `changeReputation`, then the fan-out (change event, the bar, Beyond parked) and a rank check. The bar is the World bar, moved only for a change made with no custom page open (`KeyedCustomHud.coveredByPage`, `EngineReputationFanOut.movesBar`); a change made under a page goes on no HUD panel at all (R290) and shows only in the toast of the action that paid it, as its reward row (`ReputationRewardKind.chipFor`, receipted at the amount that actually moved), so a page that pays reputation lists its receipt in its toast. The bar is labelled with the reputation's name alone (a World bars column leaves a name about 120 px, and a bar's end captions hold three or four letters), never its rank. The admin `/reputation` writes the native value directly and raises nothing; every reading still reads the native value.
- Standing is earned standing alone (the `effective` accessors equal it): gear never adds standing. It speeds gains instead: `ReputationService.change` raises a POSITIVE delta by `1 + (ReputationGain + ReputationGain_<Id>)/100` (zc-entity's global stat plus the reputation's own, `<Id>` the engine's spelling of the group id, whole percent points, folded MAX), through the pure `ReputationGain.scale` (total held to [-100, 1000], rounded half up, never under +1), before the Cap; a loss is never scaled, and Beyond counts the scaled gain like any other.
- Beyond is collected (M428, R221): a crossing parks on the player's `ReputationComponent` (`ZiggfreedCommon:Reputation`, one packed leaf: per id the payouts owed and the highest EARNED STANDING reached, never a step; a change owes `steps(after) - max(steps(before), steps(highest))`, each step read from the current top floor and Every, so a fall and a climb back pay nothing twice, a retune neither repays nor stalls, and a player with no record starts at `before`) and raises `Reputation_Beyond`; the page's Collect (`ReputationBeyond.collect`) pays the CURRENT Rewards once per owed step, 20 a press, refused whole without bag room. `Beyond.Collect: false` pays on crossing instead, for a third party's file. A switched-off reputation or one with no Beyond keeps its record and shows nothing.
- Ranks are self-healing: `ReputationRankWatch` checks on every change and once at login, never on an equip (gear moves no rank), so a write outside zc (the admin `/reputation`) heals at the next of either. A check credits the ranks from the reputation's starting rank (where its `InitialReputationValue` lands) up to the current one, or below the start only the ranks reached going down (`ReputationLadder.credited`, `ZigReputationRanksHeldEvent`, which zc-objectives turns into `REPUTATION_RANK`); only a rise during play raises the `Reputation_Rank` notice. The last-seen ranks are transient and single-process, dropped at disconnect; the first check after login is a silent hydrate.
- A kill's change runs on a later world task, never inside the death system that fired the moment.
- `page/` is the Reputation page: `ReputationView` decides its rows; what a rank opens comes from zc-progression's `GatedContent` (zc-commerce lists its shop offers there at setup), so this module never imports commerce; `ReputationPage` paints that into `ZigReputationPage.ui` in the shared menu frame with the kit's painters and decides nothing.
- Off means absent: `$Enabled` in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/reputation.json` (read at setup and with the companions) or a companion's `Enabled: false` makes the reputation unknown (factors null, rewards refused, no page row); native standing is untouched.
- `ReputationStandingReader` is the `Reputation` stat's native reader (registered in zc-core's `counter/NativeReaders`, never through an edge to zc-stats, which no library module but zc-almanac may take): the earned standing per met reputation, read through the service and so through `ReputationNative`, and every switched-on reputation as a thing named by its `TitleKey`, so the stat board's `Reputation/*` line gives each its own column. The stat file (`Stats/Reputation.json`) ships here, with the reader.
- Never import zc-objectives here: it depends on this module, not the reverse.

# zc-reputation

Standing with a faction or one NPC, on Hytale's native reputation system.

- A reputation IS a native `ReputationGroup` (`Server/NPC/Reputation/Groups/<Id>.json`); `ReputationAsset` (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Reputations/<Owner>/<Id>.json`) is zc's companion keyed by the same id, matched without regard to case. The engine's maps are keyed exactly, so every native call takes the engine's own spelling (`ReputationDef.id()`), never the authored one.
- zc ships the one global ladder (`Server/NPC/Reputation/Ranks/`, seven files). The engine reads ranks and groups once at start: a change needs a restart. A group with no `NPCGroups` key, or one naming an unloaded NPC group, makes the engine throw on every NPC add.
- A reputation may add ranks of its own above the shared top: a `Ranks` entry whose id is not a shared rank, with a `From` floor above the top's. `ReputationLadder.of(global, floors)` is that reputation's ladder and `ReputationService.ladderOf` / `ladderFor` hand it to every reading: the factor, the rank watch and `REPUTATION_RANK` credit, the bar, the page, Beyond (past its own top) and the audit; `ladder()` is the shared ranks alone. A tier the audit reports as an ERROR (`ReputationValidator`'s javadoc lists the cases) is ignored, so the shared floors, and every NPC's attitude with them, never move.
- A `reputation_rank` lock line words itself: `ReputationFactors.contribute` registers a `FactorNames` param namer that composes `factor.rank_with` from the rank's name with that reputation and the reputation's `TitleKey`. A pack's `ParamNames` Keys entry for an exact Param still wins, and a reputation with no `TitleKey` keeps the generic line.
- Every engine call goes through `ReputationNative` (production `EngineReputationNative`; tests `FakeReputationNative`), and every standing is read and written through the service's `ReputationLedger`. `ReputationService.change` is the only zc writer: the worn gain, the cap, the ledger's write, then the fan-out (change event, the bar, Beyond parked) and a rank check. The bar is the World bar, moved only for a change made with no custom page open (`EngineReputationFanOut.movesBar`, R290, its javadoc the reference); a change made under a page shows only in the toast of the action that paid it, so a page that pays reputation lists its receipt there (`ReputationRewardKind.chipFor`, the amount that actually moved). The bar is labelled with the reputation's name alone, never its rank.
- Each reputation is a track (SK4, R602; `ReputationTracks`, its javadoc the reference): a track of the reputation's id whose curve is its ladder (floors as one `Floors` piece, ranks as tiers, `Beyond.Every` as the curve's Beyond, an open top without one), provided to zc-track's stores under `ReputationTracks.OWNER` at boot and after every companion fold, in the `Reputation` category, prestige off unless the reputation's own file writes a `Prestige` group (R852, R643; `ReputationTracks.prestigeOf`: its Base defaults to the top rank, and an owner may still turn it on in `tracks.json`), and a prestiged standing gets no gain bonus (zc-track's default `Prestige.GainPercent` 0) unless the group writes one. Its gains run `World`, `Boost`, `Prestige` and `Rested` (`ReputationTracks.GAIN_FACTORS`), never zc-track's `Bonus`: `ReputationGain` already raises a gain in `ReputationService.change`, so the `TrackGain` stats (a Bonus XP meal) never speed standing; an owner adds `Bonus` back in `tracks.json`. Production's ledger is `TrackReputationLedger` (its javadoc the reference): the track holds the standing, the engine's value is a copy held in the engine's bounds and written after every track move (the service's own, and any other through `ZigTrackChangedEvent`; a move onto a fresh climb gets the starting value put under it first). In a `PerWorld` world (one boot warning) no copy is written and the record marks it behind (`<id>/unsynced`, saved, so a restart keeps it): a copy that is behind is written again in the next per-player world, never folded back, so a move made there is never taken off the track. The one-time import (per player and reputation, marked on `ReputationComponent`, never on a reload, and only in a world that keeps the player's own value, so it waits through `PerWorld` worlds) seeds the climb from the engine's value with the best level and the Beyond steps already parked or collected (the `reputation.beyond` mark) read from the record's highest; moves that landed before it stay on top. The admin `/reputation` and a native objective's reward move only the copy: readings see it at once, and it is folded into the track silently (source `native`; the track's event fires but the service skips its own move, `ownMove`: no reputation change event, bar move or Beyond payout, as the engine's write raised none, so the next change counts Beyond from the set standing) at the next change, world entry or outside move of the track (a Track reward folds it in first, against the copy before the reward, so it never writes over it). A player whose track cannot be read keeps the engine's value as the truth, said once (`NativeReputationLedger`, also what a service built with two arguments uses, which is what the tests drive).
- The track's best level and its `reputation.beyond` mark are LEVELS on the curve as it stood when they were written (`ReputationTracks.imported`, `markBeyond`): change `Beyond.Every`, the top floor or the ranks and a stored level reads as another standing, while the record's `highest` and `owed` stay standing. So the page (SK11) reads Beyond owed and reached from `ReputationComponent`, never from the mark or `best`, unless the track learns to keep standing.
- After a prestige the ranks, the factors, NPC attitude and the copy read the held standing (`ReputationLedger.Reading.held`, at least the best level's floor), while the bar, the page's earned line and a receipt read the current climb (R641).
- Standing is earned standing alone (the `effective` accessors equal it): gear never adds standing. It speeds gains instead: `ReputationService.change` raises a POSITIVE delta by `ReputationGain` plus `ReputationGain_<Id>` (`<Id>` the engine's spelling of the group id) through the pure `ReputationGain.scale`, before the Cap (`ReputationGain`'s javadoc is the arithmetic); a loss is never scaled, and Beyond counts the scaled gain like any other (R370).
- Beyond is collected (M428, R221): a crossing parks on the player's `ReputationComponent` (`ZiggfreedCommon:Reputation`; its javadoc holds the high-water rule, so a fall, a climb back and a prestige pay nothing twice), raises the track's `reputation.beyond` mark to match, and raises `Reputation_Beyond`; the page's Collect (`ReputationBeyond.collect`) pays the CURRENT Rewards once per owed step, 20 a press, refused whole without bag room. `Beyond.Collect: false` pays on crossing instead, for a third party's file. A switched-off reputation or one with no Beyond keeps its record and shows nothing.
- Ranks are self-healing (`ReputationRankWatch`, its javadoc the reference): a check runs on every change and once at every world entry, never on an equip (gear moves no rank), so a write outside zc heals at the next of either; it credits through `ReputationLadder.credited` and `ZigReputationRanksHeldEvent`, which zc-objectives turns into `REPUTATION_RANK`; only a rise during play raises the `Reputation_Rank` notice.
- A kill's change runs on a later world task, never inside the death system that fired the moment. A `Kills` row's `Season` (zc-core's `SeasonLeaf`) is asked on every kill through `Kill.live`, never folded, and the page's kills line asks the same.
- `page/` is the Reputation page: `ReputationView` decides its rows; what a rank opens comes from zc-progression's `GatedContent` (zc-commerce lists its shop offers there at setup), so this module never imports commerce; `ReputationPage` paints that into `ZigReputationPage.ui` in the shared menu frame with the kit's painters and decides nothing.
- Off means absent: `$Enabled` in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/reputation.json` (read at setup and with the companions) or a companion's `Enabled: false` makes the reputation unknown (factors null, rewards refused, no page row); native standing is untouched.
- `ReputationStandingReader` is the `Reputation` stat's native reader (registered in zc-core's `counter/NativeReaders`; its javadoc says what it reads, through the service), so the stat board's `Reputation/*` line gives each switched-on reputation its own column. The stat file (`Stats/Reputation.json`) ships here, with the reader.
- Never import zc-objectives here: it depends on this module, not the reverse.

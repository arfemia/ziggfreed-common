# proc/ - procs, counters and held effects (a package of zc-track)

- A knack is data, never a stat of its own (R758): a proc file (`ProcAsset`, `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Procs/`) opens on its `Requires` (a talent's `ziggfreedcommon:track_pick`, its rank read in the numbers' formulas), answers one `ProcTrigger`, and runs `ProcAction` groups. Its numbers are `FactorFormula`s; the bounded families gear and buffs raise for every proc (`ProcChance`, `ProcStrength`, `ProcCooldownCut` held at `ProcCooldownCutMax`, `CounterSize`, zc-entity stat JSON) are read by `Procs` alone. Never add a stat per knack.
- The package lives in zc-track because it is the one module that sees every part a proc touches (zc-entity's hit reads and conditions, zc-effects' effect adds and crowd control, zc-cast's chain fire, zc-progression's moments, the picks a reach audit reads); it names no track concept beyond the `track_pick` audit and stays generic: a proc gated on a held item works the same.
- Every trigger has one producer: Hit and SprintHit ride zc's `DEAL_DAMAGE` moment (`ProcMoments`, through `HitPayload`, which zc-objectives' `DamagePayload` implements), Kill, BlockBreak and Harvest the kill, break and pickup moments, HealGiven zc-core's `ZigHealGivenEvent` (fired by `HealthUtil.heal` for a heal with a giver, before the health moves); HitTaken, Blocked, Dodged, Landing and CrouchStill are `ProcSystems`'; HitIncoming and AllyHitIncoming are `ProcSystems.Incoming`'s (after every Filter-group step, before the engine's `ApplyDamage`, so a cancelled hit, the Forgotten Temple's included, never reaches them); GuardBroke is `GuardBreaks`' (the one blocked hit of the holder's whose drain takes the guard below nothing, noted by `Taken` after the blocker's own Blocked procs, so only the breaker is credited, answered once the engine's guard-break effect lands on that foe within its window, or a proc's `BreakGuard`, answered on the next pass); Dodge, GuardStart and Cast fire only once the move truly happened (`MoveProofs`, settled by the holder pass): a Dodge once a `Dodging`-family effect (zc-entity's file) lands after the player's own Dodge chain starts, a GuardStart when the engine's current wielding goes from none to one, a Cast once the player's own Ability chain finished or still runs half a second on (a chain whose root a consumer claims through `ProcAbilities.Namer.castsItself` never counts: that consumer fires `Procs.cast` as its cast goes off, and calls `Procs.handBack` before it runs the move its key displaced, whose server chain is then proven like a press). One dodge landing proves one press; a Dodged needs every other cancel reason the engine has ruled out (`ProcSystems.Taken.otherCancel`), and one that arrives while a dodge of the holder's still waits for its proof is held on that dodge and runs right after its Dodge (`MoveProofs.deferDodged`), so what a Dodge clears (the stamina payback mark) is never cleared after a Dodged paid it. EffectLanded and EffectEnded are `EffectWatch`'s looks (the engine fires no event). Crit only through `Procs.crit`, Finisher (a consumer's combo closing) only through `Procs.finisher`. Never add a second ECS system on an event one of these covers, and never `setOnCompletion` on a chain (one slot, which a third party may own): poll its state.
- Procs on one moment run in the order of their ids compared lower-cased (`ProcConfig`), and content relies on it (Shatter's proc is named to run before every freezing one): keep the explicit sort, which `ProcOrderTest` pins.
- A proc's strength (its `Strength` times `ProcStrength`) counts once, where an action gives the world something: an applied effect's damage multiplier, a stat change. A counter's add is never scaled (a stored share of a hit is spent by an effect that takes the strength), nor a push or a taunt's time (`ProcActions.counterAdd`, `scaledMultiplier`). A refund that gives back a cost writes `ChangeStat.Scaled: false` (`statAmount`): gear must never make it pay back more than was spent. A block's own cost is spared before it is paid, never refunded after: `Taken` runs before the engine's `DamageStamina`, hands the stamina the guard is about to pay as the Blocked moment's `cost`, and `GuardCost.Cut` lowers the hit's own stamina-drain multiplier (a refund after a breaking drain would still break the guard). A `Soak` (a share of a coming hit, paid out of a stat or not, then the `Leave` floors) and a `Heal` (CommonLib's one heal, the holder its giver) never take the strength twice: a soak none, a heal once. A `Heal` names its proc's track as its source (`ProcAsset.track`, the first `track_pick` its `Requires` asks for, the list the reach audit reads too), so that track's `HEAL_GIVEN` source pays it.
- A proc's `Hit` group is the conditional stats' (`ConditionalStatAsset.Hit`, read by `HitReads`), never a copy; `OverTime` reads false unless written, and a hit taken is read as its attacker dealt it. `Ability` asks the move: `Roots` against `ChainRoots` (a player's chain starts, recorded by type only while a live proc asks, a hit reading its own type's latest), `Names` against a consumer's `ProcAbilities` namer (the MMO names its abilities' hits and casts).
- An entry's `Area` aims its target groups at every foe in reach (`Nearby.foesAround`, hostile or locked on), or with `Allies` every ally (zc-core's `entity/Allies`: the party's members, else other players while PvP is off; zc-instance fills its party half), nearest first, held to `Max`; its `OnSelf` groups run once, and its `Counter` once too, but a `PerTarget` counter's, which changes the stacks about each one reached. Bosses and elites stay out through zc-entity's `Zig_Boss_Or_Elite` NPC group asked with `Inverse` (`{ "Id": "ZigFoeFamily", "Groups": [ "Zig_Boss_Or_Elite" ], "Inverse": true }`). `Push` is one Add on the engine's `Velocity`, never the knockback component (a null config multiplies by 25). `Taunt` is zc-effects' `AggroControl.tauntOne`, never a second taunt. `BreakGuard` clears the current wielding and cancels the guard chain, never `Stamina_Broken` (infinite; only the stamina system lifts it). A guard that is up is the engine's own `Wielding` condition.
- Waits are `ProcCooldowns` (per player by entity UUID, per target by ref, dropped when the target leaves and at disconnect); counters are `Counters` (by holder ref, never saved; their modifiers keyed `zigtrack:@counter:<id>`, which the stat grant writer leaves alone and the 1.7.x rollback sweep clears, R755; a holder's first pass sweeps what an earlier session left; a `PerTarget` counter keeps stacks per foe and gives the holder nothing, `counter_stacks` reads it about the context's target, and its `OnFoe` marks go on each foe instead (`FoeMarks`: a look by count and a `Weakness`, timed and topped up each pass while the stacks last, the weakness one shared runtime effect per counter and whole percent through zc-effects' `RuntimeEffectGrid`, the strongest on a foe winning, held at `CounterAsset.Weakness.MAX_PERCENT` and never put on a boss or an elite, `ZigFoeFamilyCondition.inGroup` of `Zig_Boss_Or_Elite`). zc-cast's `ArmedStateStore` is next-hit slots, not a cooldown map; never build a third store.
- No proc runs inside another's actions (a per-thread guard); a heal those actions gave is held and its HealGiven runs once that proc is done (`Procs.runsAfter`; what a held moment's own actions raise is dropped, so nothing feeds itself); and a hit a proc dealt itself is no moment for any proc or counter clear (`ProcMarks`: an effect a proc applied, its instance held weakly; a hit whose `Damage.INTERACTION_TYPE` is the procs' chain label). A proc's chain runs as `InteractionType.EntityStatEffect`, the engine's own server-run label (blocked by no input, no default cooldown), never an input type the triggers listen to.
- A counter's `ClearedBy` skips a damage-over-time tick unless it writes `ClearOverTime`; a counter empties when its own `Requires` fails on the holder pass (a respec, a build swap) and when its holder dies (`ProcSystems.Died`). A held effect's `Hold` and `Linger` are times per holder and file (`HeldEffects.step`), never saved. A held effect's `Reach` makes it an aura: the holder's allies (zc-core's `Allies`) or the creatures their taunt holds (zc-effects' `AggroControl.tauntedBy`) in reach wear it a moment at a time, owned by the holder and topped up each pass while they stay, so nothing records who wears it and nothing is left on anyone once they step away, the hold ends or the holder goes; one already wearing it with no end is left alone.
- Threads: a target leaving one world walks every holder's counters, watched foes and per-foe stacks (`Counters.targetGone`, `EffectWatch.forget`) while another world's thread adds to its own, so those maps are concurrent and a state's target fields volatile; every other per-holder field is touched by that holder's world thread alone. Keep any new cross-world walk on concurrent maps.
- `ProcValidator` is the boot audit pass: an unknown effect, stat, RootInteraction, effect family, counter or trigger is an ERROR (the brief's rule for this store: each would silently do nothing), a proc that cannot run as written, a group that does nothing as written, or a file no live pick reaches a WARNING.

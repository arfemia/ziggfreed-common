# almanac/view/

- Days are counted in the event's own `zone`, the last day included (the day holding a run's last instant, `endMs - 1`, so a run ending at a time of day ends that day). The dates line (`AlmanacLines.window`) says "Every year" only when `datesMove` is false; otherwise it names the framing run's year (`window.year`). A one-day event (start and end on one month-day) names its day once (`window.day`, `window.year.day`) and reads "Today only" on it, never "Last day". A monthly or weekly event (a `Recurrence` in its `Dates`) says how it comes round in place of the year line (`recur.*`), and its next run's days on a line of their own under it (`next.*`, `AlmanacLines.next`, the kit hero's `#HeroNext`), so a long rule never cuts the dates off; the ordinals, weekdays and month lists nest as keys, never English suffixes in Java.

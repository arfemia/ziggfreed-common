# zc-almanac

- No library module may depend on this one: it sits beside zc-objectives, and a consumer reaches it through `page/AlmanacPages` and the `Almanac` destination. The book's Seasons statistics come the other way: `stats/AlmanacStatistics` is contributed to the kit's `LedgerContributions.STATISTICS` from `AlmanacBootstrap.registerVocabulary`, and its page opens the Almanac through that destination.
- The calendar is read only through zc-core's `occurrence/Occurrences` slot (`isEnabled`, `live`, `next`, `history`, `firstYear`, `zone`), through `OccurrenceAlmanacCalendar`; never import zc-calendar. A season the calendar does not answer for is absent: not listed, not counted. Days are counted in the event's own `zone`, the last day included (a run's `endMs` is the midnight after it).
- A season's page is `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Almanac/<Owner>/<EventId>.json`, keyed by the calendar event id. Its achievements are the ones filed under category `Seasons` with the event id as subcategory; its keepsakes are the catalogue's yearly copies of its `Keepsake`, found by `Achievement.occurrence()` (the `<Keepsake>_<yyyy>` id as the fallback); an achievement filed under `Seasons` with no subcategory is cross-season and shows as the banner.
- Tallies live in `AlmanacComponent` (save key `ZiggfreedCommon:Almanac`): `<event>/<stat>` for every season and `<event>@<year>/<stat>` for one, built only through `AlmanacKeys`. The format only grows: append, never rename a key, since a renamed stat starts over.
- The server's own totals (`ServerTallies`) count what every player's record gains, under the same keys, in `<dataDir>/almanac-server-tallies.json` (debounced atomic write with a `.bak`; counts only, no player ids); the moment listener adds a record's growth, so attendance counts once per player per season.
- A stat line ADDS each moment's amount, counted from the registered `MomentListener`; never add an ECS system for a moment the shared producers already fire, and never write progression from here.
- The kill switch is `$Enabled` in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/almanac.json`, mirrored as the feature `ziggfreedcommon:feature` Param `Almanac`. Off means absent: no page, no command answer, no menu tab, no hub tile, no counting. The menu tab's two knobs are `$MenuTab.Show` and `$MenuTab.OnlyWhileLive` in the same file (`AlmanacOwnerLayers` reads and writes them), and `AlmanacPages.menuTabVisible` is the one rule a tab or a consumer's tile asks.
- The page paints and delegates: what a season shows is decided in `view/AlmanacView` (records) and what it says in `view/AlmanacLines` (which countdown line, the dates line); `page/AlmanacPagePlan` maps them to the kit's records, and `page/AlmanacPage` only paints a plan through the kit. `AlmanacPagePlanTest` holds the plan, and `AlmanacPageDocumentTest` drives the paint against the documents, so every id the page sets exists (a missing one drops the player). Nothing clips a child to the hero plate: every layer the page places stays inside 962 x 240.
- A season page's `Hero` is a shipped `Art` (shown while `ShowArt`) or a `Composition` of item pictures on the 962 x 240 plate; the `Composition` is one piece under `Parent` and the owner file (`WholeLeafCodec`), and a malformed one keeps the inherited one. A `Links` destination whose `Type` no installed mod registers is left out, never a failed read.

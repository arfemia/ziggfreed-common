# zc-almanac

- No library module may depend on this one: it sits beside zc-objectives, and a consumer reaches it through `page/AlmanacPages` and the `Almanac` destination. The book's Seasons statistics come the other way: `stats/AlmanacStatistics` is contributed to the kit's `LedgerContributions.STATISTICS` from `AlmanacBootstrap.registerVocabulary`, and its page opens the Almanac through that destination. It reaches zc-stats one way, for its pages' stat lines (`PlayerStats.season`, `importOnce`, `view`).
- The calendar is read only through zc-core's `occurrence/Occurrences` slot (`OccurrenceAlmanacCalendar`); a season the calendar does not answer for is absent: not listed, not counted.
- A season's page is `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Almanac/<Owner>/<EventId>.json`, keyed by the calendar event id. Its achievements are the ones filed under category `Seasons` with the event id as subcategory; its keepsakes are the catalogue's yearly copies of its `Keepsake`, found by `Achievement.occurrence()` (the `<Keepsake>_<yyyy>` id as the fallback); an achievement filed under `Seasons` with no subcategory is cross-season: the banner shows the lowest-ordered one not yet earned (by `SortOrder`, then id), else the highest earned, counted by the engine's tally (seasons, never copies); a year chip counts the runs attended in that year (`AlmanacKeys.runsAttended`, shown at two or more, `year.runs`).
- A page's `Keepsake` is the one source naming its season's keepsake. `AlmanacKeepsakeCheck` warns where it and an `AnyYear` ladder's `MetaSelector` disagree, either way. It reads the loaded files only (never the calendar or the folded pool), and runs once per boot from `ProgressionRuntime.onBuilt`; on a boot that asks for zc's boot audit, `AlmanacValidator.audit()` carries and counts its findings instead, so each prints once.
- Tallies are keyed `<event>/<stat>` for every season and `<event>@<year>/<stat>` for one, built only through `AlmanacKeys`. A page's stat lines live in the player stat store under those keys (`AlmanacStatLines.publish` hands every page's lines to `PlayerStats.season` at the end of each fold; they count while `AlmanacSwitch` is on). `AlmanacComponent` (save key `ZiggfreedCommon:Almanac`) keeps the Almanac's own records, every `$` key. Its old line keys froze at the one-time move (`AlmanacStatsImport`, claim `ziggfreedcommon:almanac@1`, at PlayerReady FIRST whatever either switch says), which copies them into the store and never writes this record; the page and the book read `AlmanacStatsImport.read` (the `$` keys, plus the store's tallies of the lines now loaded; the whole Almanac record while the claim is absent). The format only grows: append, never rename a key, since a renamed stat starts over. `<event>@<year>/$runs` counts the runs attended in a season-year (one `CALENDAR_ATTENDED` moment per player per run); `$attended` stays that year's mark and the lifetime `$attended` counts years (a year marked before `$runs` existed is seeded to its one run at its next); read runs through `AlmanacKeys.runsAttended`.
- The server's own totals (`ServerTallies`) count what every player's record gains, under the same keys, in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/almanac-server-tallies.json` beside the owner files (`AlmanacOwnerLayers.directory()`; counts only, no player ids); the stat store's growth sink adds what each line count adds and the moment listener a record's attendance growth, so `$attended` counts once per player per season and `$runs` once per player per run; the one-time move adds nothing. A count is written by a 3-second debounce and again by the library's stop (`AlmanacBootstrap.shutdown`, from the wiring root's `shutdown()`), which writes only what changed since the last write, one write at a time (the engine's atomic writer shares one `.tmp` per file).
- A stat line ADDS each moment's amount, counted off the shared moment stream by zc-stats' `SeasonLines`; the Almanac's own `MomentListener` counts attendance only. Never add an ECS system for a moment the shared producers already fire, and never write progression from here.
- The kill switch is `$Enabled` in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/almanac.json`, mirrored as the feature `ziggfreedcommon:feature` Param `Almanac`. Off means absent: no page, no command answer, no menu tab, no hub tile, no counting.
- A player's owned marks are `$owned/<item>` in the same record (`AlmanacKeys.owned`, `AlmanacCollection`), only for items some page's `Collection` lists, set for good by `AlmanacInventorySystem` when one lands in any inventory section and by the page's look at the bag on open; never in the server's totals. It is the module's one ECS system: an item reaching a bag is no shared moment.

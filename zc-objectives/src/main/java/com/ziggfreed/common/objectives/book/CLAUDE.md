# objectives/book/ - the objective book

- `canDeliverTurnInAt(subject, quest, null)` is always false, so never gate the Hand in button on it: the book uses `firstActiveTurnIn(subject, quest, null)` and then `attemptTurnIn`, which re-checks everything. A hand-in locked to a character can never complete from the book.
- A quest taken at its giver (`BookVerbs.takenAtGiver`: a giver and no `Flow.AutoAccept`) is never accepted from the book; `QuestEngine.canAccept` stays open, because the NPC quest page is where that quest is taken. A quest that arms itself is taken from the book like one with no giver, its giver recorded as the accept site the way the engine's auto-accept pass records it; `giverBound` only says a quest names a giver, never whether the book may take it.
- Any String-only sink (an item slot's hover name, a dropdown label, the search haystacks, a sort key) goes through `UiText.flatten`; everything else stays a client-resolved `Message` on `.TextSpans`.
- A partial update keeps the scroll: clear the repainted hosts, re-append, bind only the fresh elements in the update's own event builder, then `sendUpdate(cmd, events, false)`. Reopen only where a partial cannot tell the truth (a filter, search, sort or tab change, or a change that re-ranks every row).
- The Achievements tab is `achievement/AchievementsTab`; what it shows is read by the pure `achievement/AchievementReader` (rows, pages, browse sections, the overview) and painted through zc-presentation's kit. Quests read the same way through `journal/QuestReader`.
- The achievement readers list through `achievement/AchievementShelves`: an earned achievement is listed whatever its circulation (its feat flag picks the shelf), and circulation and `isVisible` gate only what the player has not earned. Never filter on `available()` before asking whether it was earned.
- A grouped capstone (`Achievement.metaGroups()`) is counted by `AchievementEngine.tally` (groups, never the copies listed under it), in its row and its Needs block; its Needs lines still list the children a player may see.
- A row and the page offer Pin only through `achievement/AchievementPinOffer`: never on a feat, otherwise where a pin is held or `AchievementEngine.pinnable` says yes. Never decide the offer from `featOfStrength()` alone: the toggle's refusal toast names the cap, which is true only when the engine refuses for nothing else.
- The book sits in the shared menu frame (`$F.@ZigMenuFrame`): its Quests | Achievements switch is the rail's two tabs, its frame paint is the menu's (`ZigMenu.appendThemed`), and a rail click is answered by `MenuRail.handle` before the action is read. Its geometry comes from `LedgerLayout` over `MenuFrame.BODY_WIDTH`, never a literal.
- `ObjectiveBookPage` is the shell (frame, rail, header band, `#TabBody`, toasts); a `BookTab` appends its own document into `#TabBody` and hears every event first through `BookActions`. A tab takes only what it can show better than a reopen, answers through `BookContext` (`sendPartial` plus `keep` for the state it changed), and acts through `BookVerbs`; the shell answers everything a tab hands back with a reopen on `BookState`.

# objectives/trainer/ - the trainer primitive (R593, R608, R617, R623)

- A trainer is an NPC placement carrying a `Trainer` block (zc-dialogue's `NpcPlacementAsset.Trainer`: `Tracks`, `Categories`, `Sections`), loaded and switched on (its own `Enabled` and the owner's switch); `Trainers` is the one read of that, of which trainer a moment means (`resolve`: the authored `Placement`, the placement the moment came from, the character's first trainer placement) and of the tracks it teaches. Its season and `Requires` say where it stands, never whether it teaches.
- What it teaches rides the picks: `PickService.trainerPicks` lists every pick bought outright whose `Where` names the placement. `TrainerBootstrap` fills zc-track's `PickTrainers` seam with `Trainers::teaches`, so a pick whose trainers are all off is learned on its track's page; never answer that question a second way. `teaches` throws on a store it cannot read (the seam then keeps the pick at its trainer); `trainer` is the guarded read for an opener. A trainer standing only in a season still teaches all year, so off season its picks are unlearnable until it stands again: intended.
- A conversation hands its placement to the screens it opens (`DialogueContext.placementId`, from the press F), so two trainer placements of one character stay apart; one opened without a placement takes the character's first trainer placement (`SHARED_TRAINER_CHARACTER` warns).
- Learn and every service act only within the block's `Reach`: `TrainerPage.press` asks `TrainerReach` once, server-side, before any entry arms or acts (a mod's part or service included), unless the entry is `fromAnywhere` (Quests, which only opens the quest list). Near means the NPC the page was opened at, when its stamp is the trainer's own placement, else the placement's cached spots in the player's world.
- `TrainerAudit` (once per boot at the first player ready or in zc's boot audit): WARNINGs `UNKNOWN_TRAINER_TRACK`, `UNKNOWN_TRAINER_CATEGORY`, `SHARED_TRAINER_CHARACTER`, and `WHERE_NAMES_NO_TRAINER` for a `Where` naming a loaded placement with no `Trainer` block or an id no placement can carry; a `Where` naming a trainer that does not teach here is R608's supported state (learned on the track's page), so one INFO a trainer id with its pick count, never a warning: `TRAINER_SWITCHED_OFF` for a trainer placement switched off (its `Enabled` or the owner's switch), `TRAINER_NOT_PLACED` for one no placement file defines as yet; a choice naming trainers is zc-track's `WHERE_ON_CHOICE`.
- The page opens only through `TrainerPages.open`: the `Trainer` destination (a conversation's Train answer, a placement's `Interact`) and the native `ZigOpenTrainer` interaction Type (registered unguarded, R700) both land there.
- Each kind of offer is a page-kit part (`TrainerParts`: Learn, Quests, Services) with a `TrainerSource`; a mod adds a kind with `TrainerParts.register(TrainerParts.part(...), source)` at setup, and a service for a taught track with `TrainerServices.register` (`PRESTIGE`, `RECHOOSE` and `SUGGESTED_BUILD` are reserved and show nothing until filled). Never a hard-coded list of kinds.
- The page paints `TrainerPagePlan` and answers every click from the plan its last build painted; every action goes through the service that owns it (`PickService.take`, `buyRespec`, the character's quest list), which asks every rule again. An entry with a `confirm` charges only on a second press (`ConfirmArm`), a Learn whose pick has a price among them, and a select forgets the arm. Every `handleDataEvent` exit reopens, opens another page, closes, or answers empty.
- `TrainerPageDocumentTest` holds every id the page's Java spells to `Pages/ZigTrainerPage.ui` and `Pages/ZigTrainerSection.ui`.

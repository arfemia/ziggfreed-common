# pvp/ - the PvP ledger and its per-world rules

- Whether players may hurt each other is the engine's own switch (`WorldConfig.IsPvpEnabled`, read live through `hytale:world_pvp` and `Where.Pvp`): a hit it refuses never reaches the ledger, so no rule here ever re-decides it.
- `PvpLedger` is the one credit path: `PvpSystems.Struck` (Inspect group, the hit as applied) fills the victim's `PvpTally`, and `PvpSystems.Died` decides through the pure `PvpCredit` and `PvpBounty`, then writes `PvpRecord`, says the bounty lines (`PvpText`) and fires `ZigPlayerKilledEvent`. zc-objectives' `ZigPvpProducer` is that event's one producer of `PLAYER_KILL`, `PLAYER_ASSIST` and `PLAYER_BOUNTY` (kind files here); never fire those kinds elsewhere, nor a `DEAL_DAMAGE` or `KILL_ENTITY` with a player target for PvP.
- What credits nothing (an idle victim through zc-core's `PlayerActivity`, a fresh respawn, the victim's allies through zc-core's `Allies`, the repeat guard) is decided once, before any record or moment, so XP, quests, achievements, statistics and streaks agree. The repeat guard is a `DiminishSpec` counted in zc-loot's `DiminishLedger` per player and victim, never a counter of its own.
- `PvpRecord` (save key `ZiggfreedCommon:Pvp`) is written only by the ledger on the player's own world thread, each write followed by `PlayerRecords.changed`; append a field, never rename one. Its lifetime numbers reach the stat store as `Kind: Native` stats (`PvpStatReaders`), never counted a second time.
- The rules are `Plugin.ZigPvp` (`PvpRules`), a world's own gameplay config winning whole over the `PvpRules` files' most specific `Where` (`PvpWorldRules`, cached per world and re-read when the world's PvP switch moves); a new knob is a nullable leaf on `PvpRules` with its default constant, its field on `OwnerForms/Pvp_Rules.json` and its words in `ziggfreedcommon.pvp.lang`. The form's binding is `PvpRuleForm` (the repeat group is written whole).
- A carried thing whose bearer cannot attack is data, never code here: an effect tagged `ZigDisarm` (zc-entity's `damage/pvp/Disarm`) held through a held effect (`ZigHolding` the item), a second held effect with `Reach.Allies` for the party's buff, and an effect tagged `ZigPvpAssist` so a kill under the aura credits its owner.

## Added

- **One "who placed this" answer** on `PlacedBlockLedger` (zc-world, `world/placed/`): `placedBy(world, x, y, z)` and `isPlacedBy(player, world, x, y, z)` name the placer, `wasPlaced(world, x, y, z)` says yes or no, and the static `placerAt(chunks, sectionRef, block, x, y, z)` answers for a chunk-store system already holding the refs. The game's own `PlacedByInteractionComponent` answers where the block keeps a block entity (a crop, a chest, a bench), the ledger's own per-section record elsewhere (stone, soil, planks). It reads every real placement whatever the policy says. `trackPlacement(placer, world, x, y, z, guarded)` records a placement with whether the anti-exploit guard holds it.
- **The gathering stat lines** (zc-world, `world/gather/`): `GatherStatSources` takes the stat a consumer counts toward a line (`SPEED` or `YIELD`) for a moment (`On.BREAK`, `On.HARVEST`) and a block or item id; `GatherYields.percentFor(store, player, on, id)` reads the yield line, `GatherYield` plus each source's stat. The library's bonus rows add a break's or hand harvest's yield to a row's written `Chance` (unless the row sets `AddsYield` false, below), and a `ZigLootable` in a broken block's drop list adds it to each written roll `Chance`; a chance nobody wrote is certain and takes nothing.
- **Stats** (zc-entity): `GatherYield`, with its tooltip label; the growth step's cap (200) is `GrowthSpeed`'s `ZigCeiling` tag. A yield, speed or growth bonus while the holder is underground, in water, under open sky or at night is the base stat granted bound `While` that condition, so it costs no stat type.
- **`GatherRules`** store (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/GatherRules/Default.json`, owner file `mods/ziggfreedcommon/gather-rules.json`, `GatherRulesAsset`, `GatherRulesConfig.rules()`): when a tree is tall (`TallTree.Logs`), the break chain's window and step cap (`Rhythm`), and how long a shaped placement keeps every placement shaped (`Shaped.FlowSeconds`).
- **`Families`** on a block family's `Match` and `Exclude` (`BlockFamilyAsset.Selector`): a block in any family named there is named, so a family is written once; a family reaching itself names nothing through the loop. The library's `Soft_Wood` is `Log` minus `Hard_Wood`, `Wood` leaves out `Light`, and `Thrift` names `Light`. Added the `Building` family: stone, soil and wood, and the `Light` family.
- **Thrift never spares a block that can pop off or fall**: one whose type needs support (`BlockType.hasSupport`) and breaks or falls on losing it (`SupportDropType` `BREAK` or `FALL`: sand, a torch, a lantern on a floor), so placing a spared block on another and breaking the one beneath never hands out a free item. A block the game marks as decoration when a player places it (`BlockType.canBePlacedAsDeco`, the placer not overriding the placement rules: a log, gravel) stays out of the game's physics, never falls, and may be spared.
- **The `Light` family holds every placed light by id**: torches, lanterns, lamps and braziers, which the `Thrift` and `Building` families take; thrift spares only those that stand on their own.
- **A moment's chance bonus** (zc-loot): `FactorLookup.chanceBonusPercent()` and `FactorLookup.withChanceBonus(base, percent)` add percent points to every written roll `Chance`, read only in `RollEvaluator.effectiveChancePercent`; `DropContext.Who` carries one as `chanceBonus` (its 4-argument constructor stays, with none); `BonusChance.fraction` takes one for a written chance in a new overload.
- **Every living entity carries the library's stats** (zc-entity `stats/StatCarriers`, zc-effects `effect/sync/`): a creature's `DamageBonus_<Type>`, `DamageResist_<Type>`, `MoveSpeed`, `KnockbackResist`, conditional stats and effect-length stats now count as a player's do. A player is read every tick, a creature every 8 world ticks, staggered by entity index; a creature holding none of a family's stats costs a few stat reads. The route step (`TypedDamageBonusSystem`), the conditional first-hit record and the effect-length watch read any attacker.
- **One stat-to-effect pass** (zc-effects `effect/sync/StatEffectSync`, registered by `StatSyncBootstrap`): every registered `StatEffectChannel` runs on every carrier. `DamageBonusReconciler.CHANNEL`, `DamageResistReconciler.CHANNEL` and `MovementReconciler.CHANNEL` are the library's; each reconciler's `sync`, `removeAll` and `resynthesizeAll` stay.
- **Crits** (zc-entity `damage/onhit/`): the `CritChance` and `CritDamage` stats (whole percent points; `CritDamage` ships at 50, a crit deals 1.5 times) and `CritSystem`, in the Filter group after `WeakSpotSystem`, which rolls once per direct hit of any attacker. The chance adds `Crits.registerChanceSource` stats (each on the hits its `Route` names) and every conditional stat whose file sets the new `Pays.CritChance`; the crit damage adds `Crits.registerDamageSource` stats. A crit marks the hit (`Crits.hitKey`, `isCrit`), fires `ZigCritEvent` (store, command buffer, attacker, target, damage, multiplier) and shows the `CritText` word to the attacker alone, a new group in `WeakSpotSettings` (key `ziggfreedcommon.crit.combattext`) whose `Enabled` is the switch for everyone. zc-track's Crit procs answer the event; a consumer never calls `Procs.crit` for a roll of its own.
- **Each player's own switch for the crit word** (zc-presentation `settings/`): a Combat card on the Settings tab (`SettingsSlot.COMBAT`, `page/CombatSettings`) with one switch, shown while `CritText.Enabled` is on. The record writes `Combat { CritWord }` (null shows the word); `PlayerSettings.critWord` reads it, `setCritWord` writes it and fires `ZigPlayerSettingChangedEvent` with `CRIT_WORD` (`Combat.CritWord`). The crit step asks it through `Crits.installWordRule`, which the library fills; a consumer with its own combat text toggle points it here instead of keeping a second one.
- **Lifesteal** (zc-entity `damage/onhit/`): the `Lifesteal` stat (whole percent points) and `LifestealSystem`, in the Inspect group, which heals any attacker that share of each direct hit it lands through `HealthUtil.heal` with no healer (a drain) and fires `ZigLifestealEvent`. `Lifesteal.registerSource` adds routed stats.
- **`HitRule`** (`Crits.installHitRule`, `Lifesteal.installHitRule`): a consumer's one rule that refuses a hit the crit or the lifesteal, asked with the damage step's store.
- **Crit by damage type** (zc-entity `damage/onhit/`): `CritChance_<Type>` and `CritDamage_<Type>` (whole percent points) add to a hit's chance and crit damage when its damage cause is that type or inherits from it, the nearest cause with such a stat winning, so a fire talent crits only fire hits. Shipped for Fire, Ice, Lightning and Arcane with their tooltip labels; a pack's own type ships its own pair as JSON. `DamageTypes.generation()` moves on every stat or cause load, for a reader keeping its own answer per cause.
- **Bound grants** (zc-entity, `stats/condition/BoundGrants`): a Stat grant's `While` and a gear set tier's `While` take a list of entity conditions (the engine's `Condition.CODEC`: its own kinds, the library's `ZigHolding` or `ZigFoesNear`, or one a mod registers), every one of which must hold on the holder for the stat to count, read every tick for a player (so a weapon swap counts on the next swing) and on a creature's carrier cadence; unauthored, the stat always counts. `StatGrant.whileConditions()` and `bound()`, and `GearSetAsset.Tier.whileConditions()` and `bound()`, read it. A writer hands its bound modifiers per owner to `BoundGrants.set(stats, ref, owner, bound)` (`BoundGrants.Bound(id, statId, modifier, conditions)`, `Bound.additive` for a flat amount on the stat's maximum), and the conditional holder pass puts each on the stat or takes it off only when its conditions flip, under `BoundGrants.MODIFIER_PREFIX` (`zigtrack:@bound:`, `writesKey`), so a hit reads the stat as it reads gear's. A `While` grant naming a stat this server does not have warns once, as an unbound one does. `has(ref)` says whether a creature holds any; `forget` and `clear` drop the memory. A gear set's bound tier hands its stats over under `GearSetPlan.BOUND_OWNER` (`GearSetPlan.bound(TierRef, Tier)`), while its look, badge and notice follow its pieces alone, and it never merges with a joined layer's tier. `HoldSeconds` beside `While` (on a Stat grant and a gear set tier, `StatGrant.holdSeconds()`, `GearSetAsset.Tier.holdSeconds()`; 0 when unwritten) asks that the conditions have held without a break that long before the stat counts, the same knob a conditional stat file's `HoldSeconds` is, so a "while still" talent waits for its holder to have stood still a moment; the hold is measured over the list's conditions on the holder's own state, never a hit-aware one, so a weapon swap never restarts it, and a hit pays an off modifier only once it has held. `BoundGrants.Bound` carries it as its fifth component (`holdSeconds`), its four-argument constructor and `additive(id, statId, amount, conditions)` kept beside `additive(id, statId, amount, conditions, holdSeconds)`. A bound `ZigFoesNear` or `ZigPlayersNear` answers from a memo per entity read again four times a second (`ConditionalStats.EVALUATE_EVERY_NANOS`), the hit term reading the same memo, so a crowd or ally talent walks who is near no more often than a conditional stat's pass does. A bonus about the holder's own state (sprinting, low health, standing still, a crowd near, at night, underground) is no stat of its own: the grant raises the plain stat (`DamageBonus`, `DamageResist` or one of its types, `HealingPower`, `GatherSpeed`, `GatherYield`, `GrowthSpeed`, `KnockbackResist`) bound `While` that state, which costs every entity no stat slot.
- **Bound grants count on a hit** (zc-entity, `stats/condition/HitAwareCondition`): a condition kind a consumer registers into `Condition.CODEC` may also implement `HitAwareCondition.evalOnHit(accessor, attacker, damage, now)`, its answer for one hit its holder dealt (its `Inverse` applied), so a weapon talent bound `While` such a condition also counts on a hit that earns that weapon's credit with something else in hand (a damage-over-time tick or a thrown weapon landing after a swap, a technique cast with another item). A bound modifier that is off pays on a hit where every one of its conditions holds on that hit, a hit-aware one asked of the hit and every other as the holder pass reads it; one that is on is already on the stat and is never paid twice, a list with no hit-aware condition is never asked, and only an additive amount on the stat's maximum is paid on a hit. `BoundGrants.hitPoints(accessor, attacker, stat, damage)` (a stat index or a stat id) reads it, `BoundGrants.hasHitTerms(ref)` says whether an entity holds any, and `HitAwareCondition.allHold(accessor, ref, conditions, damage, now)` and `anyIn(conditions)` are the shared reads. The library adds it wherever a hit reads a stat: the per-type damage bonus (any stat whose damage line counts on the hit, joining the hit's engine sum under `DamageBonusMax` as a route term does), the crit chance and crit damage with the hit type's own pair, the weak-spot stats, lifesteal, the conditional stats' hit payouts, and the procs' `ProcChance`, `ProcStrength`, `ProcCooldownCut` and `CounterSize`. A consumer's own routed source (`RoutedStats`) takes none. A writer handing `BoundGrants.set` a changed amount under the same condition array has a modifier that is on written over at once.
- **`ZigDirectHit`** (zc-entity, `stats/condition/ZigDirectHitCondition`): an entity condition that holds only on a direct hit its holder deals (any hit but a damage-over-time tick) and never outside a hit, whatever its `Inverse` says; with `Inverse` it holds on a damage-over-time tick only. It is hit-aware, so a grant bound `While` it never sits on its stat: the bound hit term pays it hit by hit where its other conditions hold, and a bonus "on your hits while you sprint" lifts a sprinting swing and never the bleed it leaves ticking. A proc whose `Holder` names it fires on a direct hit alone. `ZigDirectHitCondition.ensureKnown()` registers it for a file read before setup (a unit-test JVM).
- **Bound grants read away from the act** (zc-entity, `BoundGrants.offPoints(accessor, ref, stat, standsIn)`, a stat index or a stat id): what an entity's bound modifiers that are off add to a stat when each condition the consumer's predicate accepts is taken as holding, for a roll made away from what a grant binds to (a reward rolled when a player collects it, a payout gathered from a station nobody worked). It sums each off additive amount on the stat's maximum whose list names a condition the predicate accepts and whose every other condition holds as the holder pass reads it; one that is on is already on the stat and is never counted again. Its twin `BoundGrants.onPoints(ref, stat, names)` sums the bound modifiers that are on (additive on the maximum) whose list names a condition its predicate accepts, so a consumer reading a stat as one owner's grants give it takes another owner's share back out; a modifier is counted by at most one of the two. A consumer composes them over the stat's own reading, which `HytaleFactors.resolveStat(ctx)` (now public) reads exactly as `hytale:stat` does.
- **Working at a station** (zc-entity, `stats/condition/ZigWorkingAtCondition`, `StationWork`): the entity condition `ZigWorkingAt` holds while the entity is working at a station, and with `Stations` only at one of these (a station's own id as its mod names it, matched without regard to case). Its other knobs each ask one more fact of the work, unauthored asking nothing: `CyclesMin` (the session has reached that cycle, the first being 1), and of the piece being worked `ItemLevelMin`, `QualityMin` (its quality tier value), `DurabilityBelow` (a durability percent) and `Stamped` (it carries stamped enhancement points, or with false none), each read as `hytale:item_level`, `hytale:item_quality`, `hytale:item_durability_percent` and `ziggfreedcommon:item_stamp_points` read the same stack, once per piece (`StationWork.Piece`, kept per entity until the piece changes or the work ends); a piece knob fails with no piece. The library knows no station: a station mod answers through `StationWork.install(Source)` at its setup (`Source.workAt(accessor, ref)`, a `StationWork.Work` of the station, the cycle being worked and the piece, or null; `StationWork.workAt` and `stationAt` read it, asking the station mod once per entity per world tick however many conditions ask), and calls `StationWork.workChanged(accessor, ref)` on the world thread as a work begins and as it ends, which reads that entity's bound grants at once, so the work's first read (a ritual's pace from a talent bound to the station) never waits for the holder pass's next tick, and with none installed the condition never holds, logged once, so a file naming it loads on any server. `ZigWorkingAtCondition.at(stations...)` builds one in code and `ensureKnown()` registers it for a file decoded before setup (a unit-test JVM); `names`, `countsAt` and `bindsElsewhereThan` read its list. It is not hit-aware.
- **A stat read at a station** (zc-entity, `StationStatReading`): the factor `ziggfreedcommon:station_stat`, Param a stat and a station joined by `@` (`StationYield@Sawmill`), reads the stat as it would read at that station whether or not its holder works there: `hytale:stat`'s reading, less each bound share that is on for another station's `ZigWorkingAt`, plus each off share whose `ZigWorkingAt` counts at this station, its knobs taken as holding (`BoundGrants.onPoints` and `offPoints`). While its holder works at a station the share's condition names, the holder pass has read the knobs live, so such a share is never added and the reading is exactly the stat; it is for a roll made away from the station, an unattended pile paid to whoever gathers it or an order rolled when it is collected. Its sibling `ziggfreedcommon:station_share` (same Param) reads only the shares bound to working at that station, on or off, none of the stat's gear or effects: a roll that pays for the talents alone. `parse`, `read` and `share` are public for a consumer composing its own reading. Both requirement-line names ship as `Factors` overlays.
- **Procs read a hit-aware `Holder` condition on the hit** (zc-track): on a hit the holder dealt (Hit, Crit, SprintHit, Finisher), a `Holder` condition implementing `HitAwareCondition` answers for that hit, so a proc that counts on a weapon's hits names the consumer's condition in `Holder`; every other condition and moment reads the holder as it stands.
- **A counter's `PerStack` row takes `While`** (zc-track, `CounterAsset.PerStack.whileConditions()` and `bound()`): its stacks raise the stat only while every condition holds (and on a hit that earns a hit-aware one), handed to `BoundGrants` under `Counters.BOUND_OWNER_PREFIX` (`counter:<id>`, keyed `zigtrack:@bound:counter:<id>:<stat>:<n>`, which the 1.7.x rollback sweep of `zigtrack:` keys clears); a row without one is written as before. The stacks are kept either way.
- **`ZigHolding` `Items`**: the condition holds only while the held item matches one of the listed match keys: the game's item Family (`ItemTag:Family=Sword`), any item tag, or a name pattern read over the item's id and each `Parent`'s, so a copy of a vanilla sword holds as the sword does. Each item id's answer is kept until an item or tag reload (`MatchSets.generation()`). `ZigHoldingCondition.holdingAny(items...)` builds one in code.
- **Stats** (zc-entity): `CritChance_VsControlled`, a conditional crit chance on a slowed, rooted, frozen or withered foe (`Pays.CritChance`), and six shared conditional damage stats, each with its conditional file and tooltip label: `Damage_VsDot` (a burning, poisoned or bleeding foe), `Damage_VsHealthy` (a foe above 90% of its health), `Damage_VsUnaware` (a foe not targeting the holder), `Damage_ChargedHit` (an attack whose interaction is tagged `Charged`), `Damage_ProjectileHit` (a projectile's hit) and `Damage_AtReach` (a foe at least 2.5 blocks away).
- **`Damage_LaterTarget`** (zc-entity): a shared conditional damage stat on every foe past the first one swing strikes (`Hit.LaterTarget` true, `OverTime` false), with its conditional file and tooltip label.
- **The `ZigHitClass` effect tag** (zc-entity, `HitReads.CLASS_TAG`): an effect tagged `"Tags": { "ZigHitClass": ["Charged"] }` (its first value a damage class name) gives its wearer's classless hits that class to every `Hit.Classes` read, a conditional stat's and a proc's alike, so a mod whose attack's damage file names no class (the game's held axe swing, which the engine classes `UNKNOWN`, read here as no class) can have it heard as one without overriding that file. A hit whose own damage names a class keeps it, a damage-over-time tick is never given one, and the engine's own damage class (what armor's class enhancement reads) is untouched.
- **A missed throw can come back** (zc-track): the proc action `ReturnThrown` (`Delay`, a formula in seconds counted from the landing, never under half a second) answers ProjectileMiss by taking the projectile still lying where it landed out of the world and handing the holder back one of the item it was thrown from, the stack as it left their hand (its durability and metadata kept, quantity 1), through `WorldTasks.later` and the library's one hand-over, `LootRewardKinds.handToPlayer` (where their pickup settings send it, with the game's notice, the rest to the overflow policy, by default their feet); nothing to a holder who died, left or changed worlds before the wait ends, and nothing for a hit after all. The game's projectile names no item, so `ProjectileMisses` notes the stack and slot each player chain begins with (`ProcSystems.ChainStarts`, `InteractionContext.getHeldItem`, `getHeldItemContainer`, `getHeldItemSlot`; a chain on another slot never replaces the note of a chain still running that has launched nothing and is under 3 seconds old) and the player's next projectile takes that note. Only an item the throw spent comes back: the new `ProjectileMisses.Spent` hears the engine's `InventoryChangeEvent` and credits a throw with its item leaving that slot within 200 ms of the launch (the game's spear throw takes one from the stack in a fork a tick behind it), and the new `ProjectileMisses.Dropped` hears `DropItemEvent.PlayerRequest` so nothing taken from that slot within 200 ms of the player asking to drop from it counts; a throw that keeps its item in hand gives nothing back. `ProcMoment` carries the item as its new last component `thrown` (an `ItemStack`, null on every other moment, reported whether or not the throw spent it; the 14-argument constructor stays). The proc audit's `MOMENT_ONLY` warns on a `ReturnThrown` off ProjectileMiss.
- **`ThrowSpeed`** (zc-entity stat, zc-effects `effect/movement/ThrowSpeeds`): whole percent points on how fast a thrown projectile leaves its thrower, the game's kind a `LaunchProjectile` step launches at its asset's `MuzzleVelocity` (a thrown spear, a thrown potion), held to [-90, 300], with its tooltip label. `ThrowSpeeds.Launched` notes each one a thrower with the stat spawns and `ThrowSpeeds.Speeding` multiplies its speed on its `Velocity` component once its first physics step has written the launch speed there (the spawn hides it), which every later step reads back first; a projectile config's own launch (an arrow, a bomb) is left alone. Registered by `MovementBootstrap`; nothing to wire. `ThrowSpeeds.factorOf(EntityStatMap, String)` (public) is the one rule as a multiplier for any speed stat by id, so a consumer launching its own projectile moves it by the same points, floor and ceiling.
- **`Damage_VsStunned`** (zc-entity): a conditional damage stat on a foe wearing a `Stunned`-family effect (a stun, a stagger or a knockdown, `ZigStun`'s included), with its conditional file and tooltip label.
- **Every-type stats**: `DamageBonus` (every damage type at once, the library's own `EVERY_TYPE` source, `TypedDamageBonuses.EVERY_TYPE_STAT_ID`) and `DamageResist` (every damage type's resistance, never `Fall`, `DamageResistances.EVERY_TYPE_STAT_ID`); `DamageResistances.registerSource` takes `EVERY_TYPE`. The six `DamageResist_All_*` conditional stats fold onto `DamageResist`.
- **`DamageTypes`** (zc-entity `stats/damage/`): the damage types are every loaded cause with a loaded `DamageBonus_<Type>` stat, the resistance types every loaded cause with a `DamageResist_<Type>` stat, read off the asset maps and read again on every stat or cause load. A pack's cause or Element ships its own stat file and gets the bonus or the resistance with no code. `DamageResistances.types()` reads it.
- **`Nearby.alliesAround`**: the one ally read (zc-core's `Allies`); `Nearby.players` and the `ZigPlayersNear` condition now count allies only (party members, else other players while PvP is off; a creature has none). `Allies.pvpOn(ComponentAccessor)` beside the `Store` form.
- **`RoutedStats`** (zc-entity `stats/damage/`): the one routed-source registry the weak-spot, crit and lifesteal sources share. `TypedDamageBonuses.readMax` is public. `HitWords` (`damage/onhit/`) is the one combat-word helper.
- **`HytaleFactors.percentOf(current, min, max)`**: `hytale:stat_percent` and `ZigStatPercent` read one range, the engine's min-to-max, so a stat with a negative minimum reads alike in both.
- **Mirrored stats** (zc-entity, `stats/MirroredStats`): a consumer that mirrors a number it keeps onto a stat (`StatMirror`, a level) claims that stat once at setup (`claim(owner, stats, reading, why)`: which stat ids, how a live entity's value reads off the consumer's own record, and a raw-English reason), so no modifier on it counts anywhere the library reads it as a value. `hytale:stat` (`HytaleFactors.resolveStat`, every registry that registers the portable readings, a consumer's own composed readings included) answers the claim's reading for a claimed stat, never the stat's fold, so an item's, an effect's or a grant's line on it moves no gate, formula or threshold step; `claimOf(statId)` (matched on the stat's current id) and `readAt(claim, ref, statId)` serve a consumer's own reader of an authored stat id. `ziggfreedcommon:station_stat` reads a claimed stat as exactly the claim's reading, with no bound share added or taken out, and `ziggfreedcommon:station_share` reads 0 on one (`StationStatReading.read` and `share`). `hytale:stat_percent` is unchanged. A claimed id is a reading that needs no stat file: none of these looks it up as a stat first, so a consumer may claim ids no stat type carries, which costs no living entity a slot, and every audit that would call an id unknown asks the claim first (a gear set tier's line, a held effect's stat, a track's `Mirror`). The gear set audit (`GearSetValidator`) WARNs `MIRRORED_STAT_ON_GEAR` on a tier line naming a claimed stat, with the claim's reason.
- **A track's `Mirror` stat reads the track's climb** (zc-track, `TrackMirrors`, claimed through `MirroredStats` at setup): a mirrored stat answers the level its mirror writes (the best level, or the current climb's under `Mirror.Current`, several tracks on one stat summed), so a gear, effect or grant line on a mirrored track or reputation level moves no reading, and the gear set audit warns on a tier line naming one. A proc's stat read (`Procs.statPoints`, a held effect's "stat above 0" included) asks the claim too. A `Mirror` may name a reading no stat file ships: `hytale:stat` on it still answers the track's level, the mirror write is skipped quietly where no stat of that id loads (`TrackService.mirror`), and the track audit accepts it.
- **Track aliases** (zc-track, `track/TrackAliases`): a consumer that retires a track registers a lookup (`registerSource(owner, id -> category)`, asked at every read) naming the old id and the category its successor tracks sit in, so content still asking for the old id reads the best of that category's usable tracks. Only the gate readings `ziggfreedcommon:track_level` and `ziggfreedcommon:track_climb` read an alias (an item's `ZigLevel` tag included, whose usable check is now `TrackAliases.readable`), each alias logged once on its first read; nothing writes, lists, pays or prestiges one, and a usable track of the same id always wins. The library registers none.
- **An owner word on a track's `Cap`** (zc-track, `TrackConfig.ownerCap(owner, caps)`), the curve owner word's twin: a consumer hands the Cap the server owner's settings in its own older files give each track id, per owner, an empty map withdrawing them. Each sits in the owner layer as if `tracks.json` wrote that Cap alone, so every read of the track's Cap answers it (a gain's stop, the ceiling, `pointsCap`, the validator); an entry in `tracks.json` for the same id decodes against it (`TrackConfig.resolveBelowOwner`), keeping it unless the entry writes a `Cap` of its own, which wins (`TrackConfig.ownerFileCap(id)` answers that Cap, else null, so a consumer's page can say the owner file decides). A word that moves re-reads `tracks.json` once the packs have landed. Unwritten, as on a server running the library alone, a track's own Cap stands. **`TrackDef.capLevel()`** reads the one cap as a level: the Cap held to the curve's top where the curve stops, the top with no Cap, `Integer.MAX_VALUE` on a curve that runs on.
- **Retired settings come out of owner files** (zc-core `util/RetiredSettings`): a mod lists each setting a release stops reading as a `RetiredSettings.Entry` (the owner file, the setting's dotted path, `*` for every entry of an object or element of an array, and one sentence on where it went) and runs `strip(ownerDir, release, entries)` over its own folder at setup. Each file that still writes one loses it, a copy of the file as it was is kept beside it as `<file>.pre-<release>` (never overwritten), and the caller logs each `Outcome.line()` through its own logger; a later start finds nothing and writes and logs nothing, and a retired setting an owner writes back in comes out again on the next start, with a fresh copy. A file that does not parse, or declares a newer `$SchemaVersion`, is left alone. The library's own list is `RetiredLibrarySettings`, run first in setup over `mods/ziggfreedcommon/`: today `reward-chests.json`'s `View` on every chest. A consumer lists only its own files.
- **The server content pack** (zc-core, `content/`): one folder content pack per server, `mods/ServerContent` (`Server:ServerContent`), where every in-game editor saves. The library makes it the way the Asset Editor makes a pack (its manifest, switched on in the server config, registered at `start()` for the session that made it); from the next start the engine's own scan loads it, mutable, so the file watcher and the Asset Editor write into it. An owner may rename it in its manifest, and a pack the server config turns off stays off. A server running the library alone has all of it; `ServerContentBootstrap.install(plugin)`, `start()` and `shutdown()` wire it from the library's own setup, start and shutdown, and a consumer calls none of them.
- **Its load order**: at each boot every other loaded pack that does not load after it is merged into its manifest's `OptionalDependencies` (the owner's entries and their version ranges kept, an entry for a pack this server does not load kept, the config's `ModLoadOrder` respected), so it loads last from the next start and its assets may override or `Parent` a mod's. Only an entry naming a pack that has come to load after it is taken out, since naming it would be a cycle the engine refuses to start on; a start that already sees such a loop (a mod updated while the server was stopped) takes the pack out of its sort, registers it at `start()` instead, after the asset pass, and fixes the manifest for the next start. That start leaves an empty `Common` folder in the pack, which the engine's watcher needs and any pack may carry.

- **`ServerContent`**, the one writer every editor calls: `write(store, id, json)` checks the document the way the Asset Editor does (the store decodes it), writes `Server/<store path>/<id>.json` (or the pack's existing file for the id) and loads it as the file watcher would; `remove(store, id)` deletes the pack's file and its copy in the store (another pack's copy of the id comes back). ## Changed

- **Every read and write of an authored stat id follows a retired id to its successor** (zc-entity, zc-effects, zc-track), so a gate, a factor, a pick, a proc or a cost naming a renamed stat keeps working, with one warning naming the old id. A read goes through `resolveCurrent` (one successor at scale 1): the `hytale:stat`, `hytale:stat_percent` and `hytale:item_stat` factors (and `ItemReadings.statTotal`), a conditional stat's own reading, a bound grant's hit, on and off readings, a cost's pool (`ResourceCosts`, one stat read and spent together), a proc's stat readings and a soak's `Onto` (`Procs`, `ProcActions`), an effect family's `AddedBy` and `CutBy` stats (`EffectDurations`, each stat still counted once) and a track's mirror stat (`StatMirror`). A write lands on every successor at the alias's scale, as a saved roll reads: a stat grant (`StatGrantWriter`), a bound grant (`BoundGrants.set`, each successor under a key of its own), a counter's row (`Counters`), a proc's `ChangeStat`, a conditional stat's `Onto` and a gear set tier's stats (an additive amount scaled). `GrantFamilies.ofStat` answers a retired id's first loaded successor's families. The audits pass a retired id with that warning rather than reporting it unknown: the track audit (a mirror stat on one successor, a grant once every successor loads), and the gear set, conditional stat and proc audits (a write once every successor loads); an id that resolves to nothing stays an ERROR in the proc audit. The library's own constants and derived families, `StatChannelAudit` and a writer's own ids still resolve exactly as written.
- **A proc's `Push` moves as a weapon hit's knockback does** (zc-track, `proc/ProcActions`): its velocity instruction now carries the game's own weapon-knockback `VelocityConfig` (air resistance 0.99 easing to 0.98, ground resistance 0.94 easing to 0.3 below 3 blocks a second, Linear: the settings nearly every weapon hit's knockback writes), so its `Force` means what a knockback's Force means in the game's files. It carried none, which the engine takes as a player's own push: the horizontal part reached a creature at about 0.044 of it and braked hard, so a `Force` of 8 moved one about 0.03 blocks. On flat ground a creature of knockback scale 1 now slides about 0.29 blocks for each block a second past 3, and about 0.1 more as it stops (5.5 about 1 block, 9 about 2, 12.5 about 3); `Up` is as before. A consumer that tuned a large `Force` to make up for the old braking retunes it. And a push on the one a moment's own hit lands on (a `Hit`'s target, or the holder on a hit they take) is added to that hit's knockback when the hit carries one with velocity settings, since the hit's own instruction comes after the push in the same tick and a `Set` (most weapons write one) wiped it.
- **The library's own crowd control asks the foe's rules** (zc-track, zc-effects, `BossRules`): a proc's `Control` effect (the kinds its families name, `SpareBossOrElite` as a talent's, a boss or an elite unowned by `isBossOrElite`), a proc's `Push` on anyone but the holder (a `Pull` when a negative `Force` draws the target in, off the holder's look, else a `Push`), `BreakGuard` (an `Interrupt`), `Taunt` (through `AggroControl.tauntOne`), a counter's `OnFoe` look and weakness, `CreatureHold.canMove` (a `Carry`) and every pin and taunt window `AggroSweepSystem` sweeps. A step with no setting of its own (`Push`, `Taunt`, `BreakGuard`) asks as a talent's: the action entry's new `ByTalent` (true unless written, `ProcAction.byTalent()`) says whose it is, so a file that forgets it keeps bosses safe, and content that means the move's own control writes `ByTalent` false; an `ApplyEffect`'s `Control` keeps its own `SpareBossOrElite`. Every refusal names the holder, so a player holder sees the shrug word. Under the family default everything lands as it did, but three things: a counter's weakness that leaves `SpareBossOrElite` unwritten now lands on a boss or an elite (the library ships no such counter), a proc's pull never moves one, and a proc's push or guard break with no `ByTalent` written never lands on one (a consumer whose proc means its ability's own push writes `ByTalent` false). A kind a foe's rules shrug is shed at once: `StunImmunitySystem` also takes off every effect whose families name a kind its wearer's rules shrug, whoever put it on, the game's own included, and `AggroSweepSystem` ends a pin whose creature comes to shrug it and passes over, in a taunt window, a creature that shrugs a taunt; a kind going back to usual leaves what landed to run out. zc-effects' edge to zc-entity is `api` (`tauntOne` takes a `ControlKind`).

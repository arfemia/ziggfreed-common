## Fixed

- **A dungeon area's paste sends no block particles** (zc-instance, `instance/dungeon/Areas`): the paste passes `SetBlockSettings.NO_SEND_PARTICLES`, so a party walking into a dungeon no longer floods every player near the copy with one particle packet per pasted block. Block states, block entities, fillers, lighting and physics land exactly as before.

## Deprecated

- **`ObserverRegistry`** (zc-cast, `cast/`), deprecated since 2.6.0 and going in a later release: a moment another mod may hook is a native event. Make the event an `IEvent<Void>` POJO, fire it through zc-core's `event/NativeEventSeam` (built only when somebody listens), and register each listener on the engine bus with its plugin's `getEventRegistry().register(Event.class, listener)`; the bus runs every listener whatever another throws. `ObserverRegistryTest` goes with it.
- **`QuestMarkers`** (zc-objectives, `objectives/marker/`): `addListener(String, QuestMarkerListener)`, deprecated since 2.6.0 and going in a later release. A quest-mark surface listens on the engine bus: `ZigQuestMarksEvaluatedEvent` carries the `QuestMarkerScope` a surface was handed, on the world thread at the same moments (the six quest events, player ready, the 5 s sweep), and the engine's `PlayerDisconnectEvent` is where its `forget` ran. The new `QuestMarkers.registerSurface(PluginBase, QuestMarkerListener)` hangs both on a plugin's event registry, each guarded so a surface that throws costs only itself and one warning; the library's overhead marks and quest waypoints now register that way. A surface still added through `addListener` runs as before, just ahead of the event. `QuestMarkerListener` stays, as the shape `registerSurface` takes.

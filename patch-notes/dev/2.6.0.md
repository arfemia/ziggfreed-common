## Added

- **One "who placed this" answer** on `PlacedBlockLedger` (zc-world, `world/placed/`): `placedBy(world, x, y, z)` and `isPlacedBy(player, world, x, y, z)` name the placer, `wasPlaced(world, x, y, z)` says yes or no, and the static `placerAt(chunks, sectionRef, block, x, y, z)` answers for a chunk-store system already holding the refs. The game's own `PlacedByInteractionComponent` answers where the block keeps a block entity (a crop, a chest, a bench), the ledger's own per-section record elsewhere (stone, soil, planks). It reads every real placement whatever the policy says. `trackPlacement(placer, world, x, y, z, guarded)` records a placement with whether the anti-exploit guard holds it.
- **The gathering stat lines** (zc-world, `world/gather/`): `GatherStatSources` takes the stat a consumer counts toward a line (`SPEED` or `YIELD`) for a moment (`On.BREAK`, `On.HARVEST`) and a block or item id; `GatherYields.percentFor(store, player, on, id)` reads the yield line, `GatherYield` plus each source's stat. The library's bonus rows add a break's or hand harvest's yield to a row's written `Chance`, and a `ZigLootable` in a broken block's drop list adds it to each written roll `Chance`; a chance nobody wrote is certain and takes nothing.
- **Stats** (zc-entity): `GatherYield`, `GatherYield_Fishing`, `GatherYield_Harvesting`, and `GatherYield_WhileInWater`, `_WhileNight` and `_WhileUnderground` with their conditional files and tooltip labels; `GrowthSpeedMax`, the growth step's ceiling (200).
- **`GatherRules`** store (`Server/ZiggfreedCommon/GatherRules/Default.json`, owner file `mods/ziggfreedcommon/gather-rules.json`, `GatherRulesAsset`, `GatherRulesConfig.rules()`): when a tree is tall (`TallTree.Logs`), the break chain's window and step cap (`Rhythm`), and how long a shaped placement keeps every placement shaped (`Shaped.FlowSeconds`).
- **`Families`** on a block family's `Match` and `Exclude` (`BlockFamilyAsset.Selector`): a block in any family named there is named, so a family is written once; a family reaching itself names nothing through the loop. The library's `Soft_Wood` is `Log` minus `Hard_Wood`, `Wood` leaves out `Light`, and `Thrift` names `Light`. Added the `Building` family: stone, soil and wood, and the `Light` family.
- **Thrift never spares a block that can pop off or fall**: one whose type needs support (`BlockType.hasSupport`) and breaks or falls on losing it (`SupportDropType` `BREAK` or `FALL`: sand, a torch, a lantern on a floor), so placing a spared block on another and breaking the one beneath never hands out a free item. A block the game marks as decoration when a player places it (`BlockType.canBePlacedAsDeco`, the placer not overriding the placement rules: a log, gravel) stays out of the game's physics, never falls, and may be spared.
- **The `Light` family holds every placed light by id**: torches, lanterns, lamps and braziers, which the `Thrift` and `Building` families take; thrift spares only those that stand on their own.
- **A moment's chance bonus** (zc-loot): `FactorLookup.chanceBonusPercent()` and `FactorLookup.withChanceBonus(base, percent)` add percent points to every written roll `Chance`, read only in `RollEvaluator.effectiveChancePercent`; `DropContext.Who` carries one as `chanceBonus` (its 4-argument constructor stays, with none); `BonusChance.fraction` takes one for a written chance in a new overload.
- **Every living entity carries the library's stats** (zc-entity `stats/StatCarriers`, zc-effects `effect/sync/`): a creature's `DamageBonus_<Type>`, `DamageResist_<Type>`, `MoveSpeed`, `KnockbackResist`, conditional stats and effect-length stats now count as a player's do. A player is read every tick, a creature every 8 world ticks, staggered by entity index; a creature holding none of a family's stats costs a few stat reads. The route step (`TypedDamageBonusSystem`), the conditional first-hit record and the effect-length watch read any attacker.
- **One stat-to-effect pass** (zc-effects `effect/sync/StatEffectSync`, registered by `StatSyncBootstrap`): every registered `StatEffectChannel` runs on every carrier. `DamageBonusReconciler.CHANNEL`, `DamageResistReconciler.CHANNEL` and `MovementReconciler.CHANNEL` are the library's; each reconciler's `sync`, `removeAll` and `resynthesizeAll` stay.
- **Crits** (zc-entity `damage/onhit/`): the `CritChance` and `CritDamage` stats (whole percent points; `CritDamage` ships at 50, a crit deals 1.5 times) and `CritSystem`, in the Filter group after `WeakSpotSystem`, which rolls once per direct hit of any attacker. The chance adds `Crits.registerChanceSource` stats (each on the hits its `Route` names) and every conditional stat whose file sets the new `Pays.CritChance`; the crit damage adds `Crits.registerDamageSource` stats. A crit marks the hit (`Crits.hitKey`, `isCrit`), fires `ZigCritEvent` (store, command buffer, attacker, target, damage, multiplier) and shows the `CritText` word to the attacker alone, a new group in `WeakSpotSettings` (key `ziggfreedcommon.crit.combattext`) whose `Enabled` is the switch for everyone. zc-track's Crit procs answer the event; a consumer never calls `Procs.crit` for a roll of its own.
- **Each player's own switch for the crit word** (zc-presentation `settings/`): a Combat card on the Settings tab (`SettingsSlot.COMBAT`, `page/CombatSettings`) with one switch, shown while `CritText.Enabled` is on. The record writes `Combat { CritWord }` (null shows the word); `PlayerSettings.critWord` reads it, `setCritWord` writes it and fires `ZigPlayerSettingChangedEvent` with `CRIT_WORD` (`Combat.CritWord`). The crit step asks it through `Crits.installWordRule`, which the library fills; a consumer with its own combat text toggle points it here instead of keeping a second one.
- **Lifesteal** (zc-entity `damage/onhit/`): the `Lifesteal` stat (whole percent points) and `LifestealSystem`, in the Inspect group, which heals any attacker that share of each direct hit it lands through `HealthUtil.heal` with no healer (a drain) and fires `ZigLifestealEvent`. `Lifesteal.registerSource` adds routed stats.
- **`HitRule`** (`Crits.installHitRule`, `Lifesteal.installHitRule`): a consumer's one rule that refuses a hit the crit or the lifesteal, asked with the damage step's store.
- **Every-type stats**: `DamageBonus` (every damage type at once, the library's own `EVERY_TYPE` source, `TypedDamageBonuses.EVERY_TYPE_STAT_ID`) and `DamageResist` (every damage type's resistance, never `Fall`, `DamageResistances.EVERY_TYPE_STAT_ID`); `DamageResistances.registerSource` takes `EVERY_TYPE`. The six `DamageResist_All_*` conditional stats fold onto `DamageResist`.
- **`DamageTypes`** (zc-entity `stats/damage/`): the damage types are every loaded cause with a loaded `DamageBonus_<Type>` stat, the resistance types every loaded cause with a `DamageResist_<Type>` stat, read off the asset maps and read again on every stat or cause load. A pack's cause or Element ships its own stat file and gets the bonus or the resistance with no code. `DamageResistances.types()` reads it.
- **`Nearby.alliesAround`**: the one ally read (zc-core's `Allies`); `Nearby.players` and the `ZigPlayersNear` condition now count allies only (party members, else other players while PvP is off; a creature has none). `Allies.pvpOn(ComponentAccessor)` beside the `Store` form.
- **`RoutedStats`** (zc-entity `stats/damage/`): the one routed-source registry the weak-spot, crit and lifesteal sources share. `TypedDamageBonuses.readMax` is public. `HitWords` (`damage/onhit/`) is the one combat-word helper.
- **`HytaleFactors.percentOf(current, min, max)`**: `hytale:stat_percent` and `ZigStatPercent` read one range, the engine's min-to-max, so a stat with a negative minimum reads alike in both.
- **Track aliases** (zc-track, `track/TrackAliases`): a consumer that retires a track registers a lookup (`registerSource(owner, id -> category)`, asked at every read) naming the old id and the category its successor tracks sit in, so content still asking for the old id reads the best of that category's usable tracks. Only the gate readings `ziggfreedcommon:track_level` and `ziggfreedcommon:track_climb` read an alias (an item's `ZigLevel` tag included, whose usable check is now `TrackAliases.readable`), each alias logged once on its first read; nothing writes, lists, pays or prestiges one, and a usable track of the same id always wins. The library registers none.
- **Retired settings come out of owner files** (zc-core `util/RetiredSettings`): a mod lists each setting a release stops reading as a `RetiredSettings.Entry` (the owner file, the setting's dotted path, `*` for every entry of an object or element of an array, and one sentence on where it went) and runs `strip(ownerDir, release, entries)` over its own folder at setup. Each file that still writes one loses it, a copy of the file as it was is kept beside it as `<file>.pre-<release>` (never overwritten), and the caller logs each `Outcome.line()` through its own logger; a later start finds nothing and writes and logs nothing, and a retired setting an owner writes back in comes out again on the next start, with a fresh copy. A file that does not parse, or declares a newer `$SchemaVersion`, is left alone. The library's own list is `RetiredLibrarySettings`, run first in setup over `mods/ziggfreedcommon/`: today `reward-chests.json`'s `View` on every chest. A consumer lists only its own files.
- **The server content pack** (zc-core, `content/`): one folder content pack per server, `mods/ServerContent` (`Server:ServerContent`), where every in-game editor saves. The library makes it the way the Asset Editor makes a pack (its manifest, switched on in the server config, registered at `start()` for the session that made it); from the next start the engine's own scan loads it, mutable, so the file watcher and the Asset Editor write into it. An owner may rename it in its manifest, and a pack the server config turns off stays off. A server running the library alone has all of it; `ServerContentBootstrap.install(plugin)`, `start()` and `shutdown()` wire it from the library's own setup, start and shutdown, and a consumer calls none of them.
- **Its load order**: at each boot every other loaded pack that does not load after it is merged into its manifest's `OptionalDependencies` (the owner's entries and their version ranges kept, an entry for a pack this server does not load kept, the config's `ModLoadOrder` respected), so it loads last from the next start and its assets may override or `Parent` a mod's. Only an entry naming a pack that has come to load after it is taken out, since naming it would be a cycle the engine refuses to start on; a start that already sees such a loop (a mod updated while the server was stopped) takes the pack out of its sort, registers it at `start()` instead, after the asset pass, and fixes the manifest for the next start. That start leaves an empty `Common` folder in the pack, which the engine's watcher needs and any pack may carry.
- **Its file watcher**: the engine watches a store folder of the pack only if it is there when the pack loads. A store folder a save makes later gets the engine's own watcher at once (`AssetStore.addFileMonitor`, as the engine does for a store registered late); one made by hand or in the Asset Editor is watched from the next start. A save loads itself either way.
- **`ServerContent`**, the one writer every editor calls: `write(store, id, json)` checks the document the way the Asset Editor does (the store decodes it), writes `Server/<store path>/<id>.json` (or the pack's existing file for the id) and loads it as the file watcher would; `remove(store, id)` deletes the pack's file and its copy in the store (another pack's copy of the id comes back). Both run whole on the asset writer thread and never throw: a `ServerContent.Result(id, appliesWhen, completion)` hands back the completion to publish on (`refused()` once it is known to have failed, `problem()` why), which fails with a `ContentRefusedException` (`problem()`, and the static `problemOf(Throwable)` that unwraps a chained stage's failure) whose `ContentProblem` (`PACK_UNAVAILABLE`, `UNSUPPORTED_STORE`, `BAD_ID`, `BAD_JSON`, `REJECTED`, `NOT_FOUND`, `BAD_TEXT`, `TEXT_TAKEN`, `IO`) carries the sentence to show (`message()`).
- **Where the pack stands**: `ServerContent.packId()`, `writable()`, and `status()`, a `ServerContent.Status(state, packId, folder, assetFiles, texts, fileWatcher, watchedFolders)` whose `ServerContent.State` is `READY`, `READ_ONLY`, `DISABLED`, `STARTING`, `FAILED` or `UNAVAILABLE`.
- **`ServerContent.mintId(store, typedName)`**: an `Is_Like_This` id from the typed name (accents and apostrophes dropped), free in the store under any casing, in the pack's files and among ids minted this session, with the first free `_2`, `_3` suffix. An id is minted once and never renamed.
- **Typed words as language keys**: `ServerContent.setText(key, english)` and `setTexts(map)` keep en-US `server.lang` entries in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/server-content-text/`, served as a generated language pack (`Server:ServerContentText`, an immutable zip, registered at setup and again after each change), never a `.lang` in the watched pack. Words holding a backslash are refused as `BAD_TEXT`: the engine's lang grammar cannot show one, and one at the end of a line would join the next entry onto it. `textKey(store, id, field)` (or `textKey(storeName, id, field)`) builds `<store>.<id>.<field>` (the store's last folder, lower-cased, so an item's name is the native `items.<Id>.name`), `storedKey(key)` the registered `server.<key>` an asset names; `removeText(key)` takes one out. Each result's `AppliesWhen` (`NOW`, `NEXT_JOIN`, `RESTART`, with `notice()` the sentence an editor shows and `orLater(other)` the later of two): a new key is `NOW` (pushed to every player online as the engine pushes a key an asset load adds), new words on a key the server already loaded `RESTART` (the engine keeps a key's first definition), a key another pack defines refused as `TEXT_TAKEN`.
- **`ServerContent.export()`**: zips the pack and its text into `mods/ziggfreedcommon/exports/<Name>_<yyyyMMdd_HHmmss>.zip`, a content pack under a name of its own (the live id would collide with another server's own pack), after every save queued before it; it completes with a `ServerContent.Export(zip, packId, files, texts)`, or exceptionally with a `ContentRefusedException`.
- **`/zigpack status`** (where the pack is, whether a save can land, its file and text counts, how many of its folders the file watcher covers) and **`/zigpack export`** (the export above), the family `ZigPackCommand` with its verb names in `PackCommandLine` (`FAMILY`, `EXPORT`, `STATUS`).
- **`AssetStoreWriter.writePaths(what, store, packKey, paths)`** (files on disk loaded as the file watcher loads them; a file the store refuses fails the write) and **`AssetStoreWriter.removeAssets(what, store, packKey, keys)`** (only that pack's copy goes). **`GeneratedLangPack.readLocaleFile(root, filename, locale)`** reads back a file `writeLocaleFiles` wrote, each value as the engine's parser reads it (for a value with no backslash, exactly as written).
- **Scaled stat aliases** (zc-core, `stats/StatAliases`): `registerScaled(oldId, scale, newIds...)` retires a stat whose successor counts in another unit (a percent onto seconds), each successor taking the old value times `scale`; `scale(id)` answers it (1 for a plain alias or a live id), and `normalize` applies it, so a saved stack's roll lands in the new unit. The library registers its own retired ids too (the flat damage stats, under Removed).
- **A boost item** (zc-track `boost/BoostToken`): a stack carrying `ZigBoost` metadata (`Boost`, `Percent`, `Seconds`, `Track`) starts that boost through the `ZigBoost` interaction, its leaves winning over the interaction's own, so one item id carries any boost. `BoostToken.mint(itemId, token, quantity)` makes the stack with its description line (`token.description` in `ziggfreedcommon.boost.lang`); `BoostToken.of(stack)` reads one back. The library ships its own token item, `Zig_Boost_Token` (a vanilla potion's art), and `/zigboost give [--player=<name>] --boost=<id> [--percent] [--seconds] [--track] [--quantity]` hands an online player that many, up to one stack, through the one pickup hand-over; a consumer mints onto its own item instead.
- **A boost start command** (`BoostSettings.OnStart`: `Enabled`, `Command`, `Personal`, `Server`, `ReplacesAnnouncement`; off as shipped): a server owner's command run as the console through `CommandRunner` whenever any boost starts, a player's, a reward's, an admin's or a console's, never a seeded run, with the placeholders `{player}`, `{multiplier}`, `{target}`, `{duration}` (minutes), `{scope}` (`Global` or `Personal`) and `{boost}`. `Personal` and `Server` say which starts run it; with `ReplacesAnnouncement` a server boost's start tells only its starter, for a command that announces it to everyone instead, unless the command could not be handed over, when the library's line goes out as usual. Each value a placeholder takes from outside (a name, a boost's words or id) is one line of plain text, whitespace collapsed and control characters dropped. The shipped record writes a `say` line for server boosts, ready to switch on. Read through `BoostSettingsAsset.startCommandOn`, `startCommand`, `startCommandOnPersonal`, `startCommandOnServer` and `startCommandReplacesAnnouncement`.
- **A start may name one track** out of those its boost lifts: `Boosts.start(..., track, by)`, the `Track` leaf on the `ZigBoost` interaction and the `ZigBoostReward` type, the `track` param on the `Boost` reward kind and `/zigboost start --track`. The run keeps it (`BoostRun.track`, written as `Track` on the player's `Boosts` leaf and in `server-boosts.json`) and lifts that track alone, so one boost file covers each track a consumer adds, with no file per track. A boost file's `Text.FlavorKey` words what it lifts in its start and reward lines (`BoostAsset.liftsKey`).
- **Reusable boosts**: a Boosts file's `Reusable` group lets a player start the boost themselves (`/zigboost own --boost=<id>`, which every player may use and which, bare, lists what they may start with any wait left, or a consumer's page), again after its `Stack.Cooldown`; its own `Requires` (a permission, say) is asked only at that start, so a server boost one player starts lifts everyone, and a running one keeps running when its starter loses the gate. `BoostConfig.reusable()` lists the switched-on ones, `Boosts.mayStartOwn` asks, `startOwn` starts and `waitMs` reads the wait left. A standing boost is never reusable.
- **A server boost started by no player**: `Boosts.startServer(boostId, percent, seconds, track, by)` (a console, a schedule), announced to everyone online; a personal boost refuses it as `BoostOutcome.NEEDS_PLAYER`. `ZigBoostStartedEvent.playerId()` is null for one.
- **Runs moved from a consumer's own save**: `Boosts.seed(player, runs, waits)` puts personal runs and cooldown waits on the player's record and `seedServer(runs)` server runs in `server-boosts.json`, silently, skipping a run already held (equal in every leaf) and keeping the later of two waits; a `seedServer` before the boot read waits for it. `seedServer(runs, written)` tells `written` once whether `server-boosts.json` now holds them, so a consumer retires its older file only then. Reads for a page: `Boosts.standing(player)` (the standing boosts lifting now), `personalRunning(player)`, `multiplier(player, ref, trackId, categoryId)` (the boosts' share of one gain, under the cap) and `BoostOwnerLayers.file()` and `settingsFile()`.

## Changed

- **`GeneratedLangPack.writeLocaleFiles` writes the engine's own lang grammar**, and writes each file to a temporary file beside it that is moved into place, so a failed write leaves the last good file whole. A backslash is written as it is (the engine's parser never undoes a doubled one, so a doubled backslash showed twice), and a value that ends in a backslash, or starts and ends with a quote, is wrapped in one more pair of quotes, which the parser strips: before, the first joined the next entry onto it and the second lost its quotes.
- **The placed-block ledger records every real placement with its placer, whatever the policy says**, exempt and creative builders included; the policy now decides only credit. `consumePlacement` spends a block's record on every break whatever the policy, and answers true only for a placement the guard held while the policy is on; `isPlaced` answers the same without spending. A placement made while the guard was off stays unguarded when it comes back on. A placed item is still remembered only for a guarded placement.
- An endless wave's damage step is a keyed modifier (`WaveDamage.MODIFIER_KEY`) on the creature's `DamageBonus`, which the outgoing pass carries; the `Zig_Wave_Damage_All_<pct>` effects are no longer written.
- A consumer's `TypedDamageBonuses.installAttackerGate` and `DamageResistances.installGate` are now asked of creatures too.
- **`CommandRunner.substitute` fills placeholders in one pass** (zc-core), left to right: a value is written as it is and never read again, so a value holding a `{key}` of its own (a player named `{item}`) is no longer filled a second time, and the order a map lists its keys in no longer matters. A line with no such value runs exactly as before. `CommandRunner` dispatches from any thread, as the engine's command manager runs a line on its own pool; its doc said world thread.
- `JsonOverrideWriter.setLeaves` (zc-core) writes a `Map` value as a JSON object, a list of them as an array of objects (a `Requires` block's `Factors` row), where it wrote each as its `toString`.

## Fixed

- **A dungeon area's paste sends no block particles** (zc-instance, `instance/dungeon/Areas`): the paste passes `SetBlockSettings.NO_SEND_PARTICLES`, so a party walking into a dungeon no longer floods every player near the copy with one particle packet per pasted block. Block states, block entities, fillers, lighting and physics land exactly as before.

## Deprecated

- **`ObserverRegistry`** (zc-cast, `cast/`), deprecated since 2.6.0 and going in a later release: a moment another mod may hook is a native event. Make the event an `IEvent<Void>` POJO, fire it through zc-core's `event/NativeEventSeam` (built only when somebody listens), and register each listener on the engine bus with its plugin's `getEventRegistry().register(Event.class, listener)`; the bus runs every listener whatever another throws. `ObserverRegistryTest` goes with it.
- **`QuestMarkers`** (zc-objectives, `objectives/marker/`): `addListener(String, QuestMarkerListener)`, deprecated since 2.6.0 and going in a later release. A quest-mark surface listens on the engine bus: `ZigQuestMarksEvaluatedEvent` carries the `QuestMarkerScope` a surface was handed, on the world thread at the same moments (the six quest events, player ready, the 5 s sweep), and the engine's `PlayerDisconnectEvent` is where its `forget` ran. The new `QuestMarkers.registerSurface(PluginBase, QuestMarkerListener)` hangs both on a plugin's event registry, each guarded so a surface that throws costs only itself and one warning; the library's overhead marks and quest waypoints now register that way. A surface still added through `addListener` runs as before, just ahead of the event. `QuestMarkerListener` stays, as the shape `registerSurface` takes.

## Removed

These 2.5.1 public members are gone, so a consumer calling one rebuilds against this release. Each names what takes its place.

- **`BookVerbs`** (zc-objectives): `acceptedToast(Quest, GrantOutcome, Source)` and `more(int)`. Use `announceAccepted(ObjectiveBookDeps, Quest, QuestVerbs.Accepted, Store, Ref, Player, Consumer)`, the one accept announcement the book and a conversation share: nothing for an accept that finished the quest at once, else the book's `ActionFeedback`, and the library's own accepted line only where no consumer filled that seam. `more` has no replacement.
- **`ObjectiveBookDeps`** (zc-objectives): `QuestClaimPreCheck`, `NO_CLAIM_PRECHECK`, `claimPreCheck()` and `claimPreCheckGuarded(Quest, Store, Ref, Player)`. No replacement: a Collect is the engine's own `attemptClaim`, which refuses rewards that will not fit as `NO_ROOM` through the quest gate's `canReceiveRewards`, and every surface answers a refusal with `ClaimToasts.refusedToast`.
- **`ObjectiveBookDeps.Builder`** (zc-objectives): `claimPreCheck(QuestClaimPreCheck)`. No replacement, as above.
- **`ObjectiveBookDeps.ActionFeedback`** (zc-objectives): the five-argument `accepted(Quest, Store, Ref, Player, GrantOutcome)`. Implement the four-argument `accepted(Quest, Store, Ref, Player)`: an accept that finished the quest at once is announced by the engine's completion notice alone, so the seam is never asked about it and has no payout to carry.
- **`QuestPresentation`** (zc-objectives): `claimPreCheck(Quest, Store, Ref, Player)`. No replacement, as for `ObjectiveBookDeps`.
- **`QuestPresentation.BookPresentation`** (zc-objectives): `claimPreCheck(Quest, Store, Ref, Player)`. No replacement, as above.
- **`QuestVerbs.Accepted`** (zc-objectives): the record's canonical constructor `(boolean, GrantOutcome)` and `settled()`. Use the constructor `(boolean accepted, boolean finished)` and `finished()`, true when the settle right behind the accept paid the quest out or parked it for collecting.
- **`NpcQuestPageDeps`** (zc-objectives): `CompletionToast`, `NO_COMPLETION_TOAST`, `completionToast()`, `handInToast(Quest, GrantOutcome, Message, Message, IntFunction)` and `resolveCompletionToast(Quest, List, Message, IntFunction)`. No replacement: a quest finishing, waiting to be collected or collected at a character is announced by the engine's quest notices alone (`Quest_Completed`, `Quest_Parked`, `Quest_Claimed`), drawn into the page as they fire.
- **`NpcQuestPageDeps.Builder`** (zc-objectives): `completionToast(CompletionToast)`. No replacement, as above.
- **`RewardChestAsset`** (zc-instance): `itemsWindow()` and the nested `View`. The reward chest's window chain, which no chest opened since 2.5.0, is gone: every chest opens `RewardChestPage`. The first start takes `View` out of an owner's `mods/ziggfreedcommon/reward-chests.json` (`RetiredLibrarySettings`, a copy kept beside it), and a pack file that still writes `View.ItemsWindow` loads with the engine's one unused-key warning.
- **`ClaimsPage`** (zc-instance): the constructors `(PlayerRef, ClaimsPageDeps, String, String)` and `(PlayerRef, ClaimsPageDeps, String, String, boolean)`. The fight and chest modes went with the window chain; the three-argument constructor, the Claims tab, stays.
- **`ClaimsView`** (zc-instance): `ACTION_ITEMS`, `detail(Claim, ClaimsText, boolean)`, `spoilsEmpty(ClaimsText)` and `spoilsItemsLeft(ClaimsText)`. Use `detail(Claim, ClaimsText)`; the en-US keys `claims.spoils.paid`, `spoils.detail.lead`, `spoils.detail.hint`, `spoils.items_left` and `spoils.action.items` go from every locale.
- **`ClaimGrants`** (zc-instance): `claimFightExceptItems(...)`, `claimExceptItems(...)`, `hasNonItem(Claim)` and the nested `Paid`. A reward chest's presses are `claimReward` and `claimFight`.
- **`SpoilsChestSystems`** (zc-instance): the nested `NoDrop`. No chest opens the game's chest window, so nothing takes its place.
- **`ClaimRolls`** (zc-instance): `roll(LootableAsset, UUID, String, Consumer)`. Use `award(ClaimRolls.Payout, Consumer<Claim>)`, which rolls a payout's tables (`ClaimRolls.Draw`) as the player while they are online, on their world's thread, writes the Claim through `ClaimStore.award` and hands back the Claim as held.
- **`DungeonValidator`** (zc-instance): `audit(Collection, Predicate, Function, Predicate, Predicate, Predicate, Predicate, int, Predicate)`. Use the ten-argument form, which takes `lootableGated` after `lootableKnown`; the eight-argument form stays.
- **`DropRarity`** (zc-loot): `LEGENDARY`, `EPIC`, `RARE`, `UNCOMMON` and `COMMON`. The bands are data now (`DropRarityAsset`), and a quality is the game's own id, as `ItemQuality` names it.
- **`AlmanacEntryAsset`** (zc-almanac): `FINDING_COLLECTION_SLOT_CONDITION_IGNORED`. A slot's whole `Requires` is read now, so nothing is ignored.
- **`AlmanacView.CollectionItem`** (zc-almanac): the constructor `(String, String, boolean, boolean, String)`. It takes a sixth component, `lock`: the slot's requirement line, null when it lets the player collect it.
- **`TypedDamageBonuses`** (zc-entity): `TYPE_IDS`. The damage types are read off the loaded files: `DamageTypes.bonusTypes()`, `bonusType(String)`, and `TypedDamageBonuses.registeredTypes()` for every type a source names.
- **`TypedDamageBonuses`** (zc-entity): `FLAT_PREFIX`, `registerSource(String, String, String, Gate)`, and with them the twelve `DamageBonusFlat_<Type>` stat files and their tooltip keys, `flatFor` and the four-argument `registerRouteSource`. No stat grants flat damage: a flat amount is an ability's or a talent's own modifier. Register a percent stat with `registerSource(String, String, Gate)` or `registerRouteSource(String, String, Route)`. A stack's saved `DamageBonusFlat_<Type>` roll reads as `DamageBonus_<Type>` at 4 points a flat point (`RETIRED_FLAT_PERCENT`, registered by the library at setup; `RETIRED_FLAT_PREFIX` names the old ids); an item's own `StatModifiers` must name the percent stat. `TypedDamageBonusSystem` adds only route lines now, and `apply(amount, percent, flat)` stays for an ability's flat.
- **`TypedDamageBonuses.Source`** (zc-entity): the constructor `(String, String, Gate)` and `flatStatId()`. A source is one percent stat: the constructor `(String, Gate)`; `RouteSource` likewise drops its flat.
- **`TypedDamageBonuses.Sums`** (zc-entity): the constructor `(double, double)` and `flat()`. A sum is whole percent points alone: the constructor `(double)`.
- **`DamageResistances`** (zc-entity): `TYPE_IDS`, `STAT_IDS`. The resistance types are `DamageResistances.types()` (`DamageTypes.resistTypes()`), a type's stat `statId(String)`; a source for every damage type at once registers under `EVERY_TYPE`.
- **`DamageResistSyncSystem`** (zc-effects): `DamageResistSyncSystem`, the whole class. Its pass is `DamageResistReconciler.CHANNEL` on the one `StatEffectSync`; `DamageResistReconciler.sync` stays the consumer's entry point.
- **`BonusTable.Row`** (zc-loot): the constructor `(NamePattern, String, BonusEntry)` and `pattern()`. A bonus row's key is a zc-core `match/MatchKey` now, a name pattern or a set key (`ItemTag:`, `BlockFamily:`, `NpcGroup:`): the constructor takes one in place of the `NamePattern` and `pattern()` returns one, while `Row.of`, `BonusTable.fold` and `BonusRowAudit.audit` keep their signatures.
- **`BonusTable`** (zc-loot): `patterned(BonusMoment)`. It returns `List<Map.Entry<MatchKey, E>>` in place of `List<Map.Entry<NamePattern, E>>`: the same erased signature, so it still links, but code naming the entry's key type must read a `MatchKey` (its `raw` is the authored key).
- **`NameMatchRank`** (zc-core): `ANY_BAND`. Its value moves from 3 to 4, the new set keys taking band 3 (`SET_BAND`), so the bare `*` catch-all still ranks below every set; zc-world's `MatchRank.ANY_BAND` re-exports it. A constant is copied into the code compiled against it, so a consumer that reads either one rebuilds against 3.0.0.

> Built on Hytale Update 7 (0.7.0), like 2.3.0: the manifest's `ServerVersion` is unchanged, and its `Dependencies` gain three of the engine's own plugins that every server ships: `Hytale:Instances`, `Hytale:TriggerVolumes` and `Hytale:Rewards`. Additive over 2.3.0 except the encounter module's offline reward queue and the old nine-argument `EncounterLoot.grantShares`, listed under Removed: a consumer that never filled that queue or called that method links unchanged. New: zc-loot's `loot/claim/` (`Claim`, `ClaimStore`, `ClaimAddedEvent`) and `loot/diminish/` (`DiminishSpec`, `DiminishLedger`); zc-instance's `instance/claims/` (the Claims tab, its grants and notices, `/zigclaims`), `instance/world/` (`InstanceWorlds`, `CopyKey`), `instance/dungeon/` (the Dungeon store and its audit, `CopyRouter`, `DungeonEntry`, `Areas`, `AreaLayout`, `AreaMembers`, `AreaSpawnProvider`, `DungeonRuns`, `DungeonObjective`, `DungeonResults`, `DungeonResultsActions`, `DungeonResultsMessages`, `ZigEnterDungeonInteraction`, `DungeonDestinations`, `DungeonRefusals`, `/zigdungeon`), `party/ZcParty` with `ZcPartyMessages`, `ZcPartyScreenMessages`, `ZcPartyText` and `/zigparty`, `metadata/InstanceRoundStartedEvent` and `InstanceRounds.fireStarted`, `ResultsActions.offersPlayAgain` (a default), `EncounterLeaderboardListener.fightInRun`, `runOf` and `fightOf`, and `InstanceBootstrap.installClaims`, `registerParty` and `registerDungeons`; zc-presentation's `MenuSlot.CLAIMS`; zc-objectives' `objectives/interaction/ZigRequiresInteraction`, `objectives/gate/ZigRequiresTriggerCondition`, `RequiresCheck` and `RequiresVolumeAudit`, `objectives/dungeon/DungeonObjectives`, `producer/InstanceRoundStartedPayload`, and `ZigInstanceRoundProducer.KIND_STARTED` and `KIND_LOST`; zc-progression's seeded kinds `INSTANCE_ROUND_STARTED` and `INSTANCE_ROUND_LOST` (thirty built-ins); zc-stats' `Dungeon_Runs_Started`, `_Completed` and `_Failed`; zc-encounter's `payout/ZigClaimReward` and `payout/EncounterClaims`, `seam/EncounterRunTag` and `seam/RunTag` with `EncounterSeams.fillRunTag`, `isRunTagFilled` and `runTagFor`, `EncounterDefeatedEvent`'s fourteen-argument constructor and `runTag()`, `ZigEncounterRun.settledShares` and `keepSettledShares`, `EncounterRuns.untrack(UUID, UUID, Ref)` and `runRostering`, and `EncounterBindingAsset.Loot.retiredLeaves`. zc-instance gains two module edges: zc-world (`TickingSections.wake`) and zc-cast (`InteractionTypes`).

- **New: the engine instance seam, `world/InstanceWorlds` and `world/CopyKey` (zc-instance).** zc-instance is the one launcher of the engine's instance worlds. `InstanceWorlds.spawn(template, worldName, forWorld, returnPoint, emptyCloseSeconds, extraConfig)` spawns a copy through `InstancesPlugin`'s five-argument `spawnInstance` under zc's own name, never an `InstanceKey`, and in the spawn's config step sets the removal conditions (native `WorldEmptyCondition` AND `IdleTimeoutCondition`, the latter `emptyCloseSeconds`), `DeleteOnUniverseStart` and `DeleteOnRemove`, then runs the caller's `extraConfig`. It refuses a name that is live or still spawning. `teleportIn(ref, accessor, worldFuture, overrideReturn, arrival)` is the five-argument `teleportPlayerToLoadingInstance`, `exit(ref, accessor)` the engine's `exitInstance`, `safeRemove(world)` its safe remove, and `removeOffThread(world)` removes a copy on a daemon remover thread, only while its name still holds that world object. `InstanceBootstrap.shutdown()` stops the remover (`InstanceWorlds.shutdown()`); it starts on first use. `CopyKey(world, area)` keys an area of a copy (`SOLE_AREA` 0, `sole(world)`). A copy left by a hard stop is deleted at the next start; a world zc did not spawn is never touched. No test (smoke).
- **New: Claims, `loot/claim/` (zc-loot).** Rewards are collected, never handed over unasked, so whatever pays a player a Claim writes it here and the player presses Claim when their bags have room. `Claim(id, source, titleKey, titleArgs, difficulty, rewards, createdAt)` is one payout a player has earned and not taken (`withRewards`, `is`; the id is matched without regard to case). `ClaimStore` (`shared()`, `init(dataDir)`) keeps every player's Claims in one versioned file, `claims.json` in the library's data folder, written atomically through the engine's `FileUtil` on every change; a file declaring a newer version than the build reads is left unread. `award(player, claim, spec, diminishKey, nowMs)` is the one way a Claim is paid: it answers false and counts nothing for a Claim with no rewards or one whose id the player still holds, so a roll that lands nothing never spends a player's first clear of the window; else it counts one clear of `diminishKey` under `spec`, scales each reward's quantity by that clear's multiplier (a fraction paid whole through `StochasticCount`, a reward scaled to nothing dropped), writes the Claim unless nothing is left, and fires the native `ClaimAddedEvent(player, claim)`. `list`, `has`, `count`, `take`, `takeAll` and `putBack` read and hand over. A Claim never expires. `PendingRewardStore` stays the retry queue. Test: `ClaimStoreTest` (the file round trip, tagged `engine-items`).
- **New: diminishing returns, `loot/diminish/` (zc-loot).** `DiminishSpec` is authored as `"Diminish": { "Steps": [1, 0.5, 0], "Window": "Daily" }`: `Steps` is the multiplier for each clear in a window, the last repeating, and `Window` is `Daily` or `Weekly` (midnight UTC, weeks from Monday, through zc-core's `PeriodMath`). Read it with `window()`, `steps()` and `multiplier(priorClears)`, or build one with `of(window, steps...)`; `Window.periodIndex` and `byWord` are the window's own reads. `DiminishLedger` (`shared()`, `init(dataDir)`, its file `claims-diminish.json`) counts clears per player and key: `count(player, key, spec, nowMs)` answers the multiplier the clear pays at, starting over in a new window, pruned to current windows. Test: `DiminishSpecCodecTest`.
- **New: the Claims tab, `instance/claims/` (zc-instance, zc-presentation).** `MenuSlot.CLAIMS` sits after `REPUTATION`. `ClaimsDestinations` registers the destination `Claims` (owner `ziggfreedcommon`; `"Open": "Claims"`; `CLAIMS`, `ICON` `Furniture_Dungeon_Chest_Epic`), whose menu `entry()` shows only while the viewer holds a Claim (`ClaimStore.has`), so the tab appears with the toast that names it, and `open(store, ref, player, selectedId)` opens the page. `ClaimsPage` (a `ToastablePage<ClaimsEventData>` over `Pages/ZigClaimsPage.ui`) lists Claims newest first with reward chips and a detail, built by `ClaimsView` (`ACTION_CLAIM`, `ACTION_CLAIM_ALL`, `newestFirst`, `pick`, `ledger`, `detail`, `empty`, `title`, and `chip`, public so a results screen draws a Claim's rewards with the same chip) from `ClaimsPageDeps(claims, sink, text)`, whose `library()` is the library's own store, `ZcRewardSink` and `ClaimsText`. `ClaimGrants.claim` and `claimAll` hand a Claim over through the bag guard and put back what does not fit, answering a `Result(taken, emptied, granted, kept, failed)`; this is Kweebec Nightmare's `claimPending` and `KweebecRewardSink` lifted. `ClaimNotices` raises a toast naming the tab on `ClaimAddedEvent`, and once a session, at the first ready after login, a notice counting what waits; both work on a zc-only server. `/zigclaims give --player=<name|uuid> --lootable=<id>` (`ZigClaimsCommand`, `ZigClaimsGiveCommand`, `ClaimTargets`) rolls a loot table into a player's Claims online or not (source `admin:<lootable>`, title `title.admin`); `--player` asks the profile service directly (the `ServerAuthManager` session and `ProfileServiceClient.getProfileByUsernameAsync`) and tries an online starts-with match last, so a full name typed for an offline player never lands on an online player whose name merely starts with it. `InstanceBootstrap.installClaims(plugin)` opens the store and ledger and registers the rest. New `ziggfreedcommon.claims.lang`. Tests: `RailIconsTest` and `RailLabelLengthTest` take the tab; `ZigMenuTest.theAlmanacSitsAboveQuestsAndAchievements`, an exact slot-order test, is deleted.
- **New: the boss Claim, `payout/ZigClaimReward` and `payout/EncounterClaims` (zc-encounter, zc root).** A native reward type, `"Type": "ZigClaim"` under `Server/Rewards/<Id>.json` (`Lootable`, one table id; `Diminish`; `ShareScaled`, true by default; `TitleKey`), registered in setup from `EncounterBootstrap` on `RewardAsset.CODEC` through `getCodecRegistry`, so an encounter script's native `{"Type": "GrantReward", "Reward": "<Id>"}` names it and the engine checks the id at script load. Accessors: `lootable()`, `diminish()`, `diminishOrDefault()`, `shareScaled()`, `titleKey()`, `grant(PlayerRef)`. `EncounterClaims` is the one boss Claim path. A grant finds the player's run (the member index, else `EncounterRuns.runRostering`) and pays only a defeated run, from the shares its defeat settled (`ZigEncounterRun.settledShares`, kept until the reset's roll-over), each won roll kept at `rand < share` (`ShareScaled` false pays at share 1). It writes through `ClaimStore.award` with the Claim id `boss:<script>:<run>:<reward>:<player>` and the Diminish key `boss:<script>:<reward>:<difficulty>`; with no `Diminish`, the first win each day pays (`FIRST_EACH_DAY`, `[1, 0]` Daily). An uncredited member gets nothing, and so does a wiped, leashed or fresh run. Only a player no run indexes or rosters (a world event's grant) is paid at share 1, under `reward:<id>` (Claim id `reward:<id>:<player>:<ms>`). A `ZigClaim` with no `Lootable` decodes and pays nothing, with an INFO line. A Claim's title is the reward's `TitleKey`, else the binding's `NameKey`; loot cues play when a Claim is written for a player in the same world. Known limit: the native `GrantReward` pays only the encounter's live roster (`getMemberTtl`), so a credited player who is offline or out of range at the grant gets none. Test: `ZigClaimRewardCodecTest`.
- **Changed: a boss's payout is a Claim, never the bag (zc-encounter).** `EncounterLoot.grantDefeat` (a binding's `Loot.OnDefeat`) and `grantShares` write one Claim per credited participant through `EncounterClaims` (reward name `OnDefeat`, `EncounterClaims.ON_DEFEAT`, first win each day), never `RewardGrants.grantAll`. A credited participant who is offline still gets one where the rule's `CreditDisconnected` allows. The defeat receipt is one row, `claim.waits` (`EncounterClaims.CLAIM_WAITS_KEY`: "Your reward is waiting under Claims in your menu."). The `ZigGrant` script action redirects to the same path with one WARNING per script. A row's `Loot.ToKiller` and `QueueIfOffline` are read and ignored with one WARNING at the row's first payout (`EncounterBindingAsset.Loot.retiredLeaves()`), so the killer is paid at the share they earned. zc's example binding drops `QueueIfOffline`. The offline reward queue is gone (Removed, below), and the wiring root no longer fills it. Test: `EncounterSeamsTest`'s two methods on the queue are deleted.
- **Changed: a continuing reset keeps its members on the next run (zc-encounter).** `EncounterLifecycle.reset` rolls the run over first, then calls the new `EncounterRuns.untrack(ended, next, encounterRef)`, which points the members at the next run and remembers its encounter until that run's first tick. `EncounterRuns.runRostering(store, member)` answers a live run's roster, then a successor's. So a `GrantReward` after a leash, a `zc:reset` or the tick timeout in the same action list refuses instead of paying every roster member a full share. `untrack(UUID)` is unchanged. Test: `EncounterRunsMembersTest`.
- **New: zc's default party, `party/ZcParty` (zc-instance).** `ZcParty.service()` builds the library's `PartyService` on first use (game and settings id `ZcParty.GAME_ID`, `ziggfreedcommon`; settings `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Party/ZiggfreedCommon.json` through `PartySettingsConfig`: `MaxSize` 5, `InviteTimeoutSeconds` 60, `OwnerOnlyInvite` false), `pageDeps()` hands it to `PartyInvitePage` with no queue handler, and `shutdown()` stops its invite timers, last in `InstanceBootstrap.shutdown()`. `ZcPartyMessages`, `ZcPartyScreenMessages` and `ZcPartyText` read the new `ziggfreedcommon.party.lang`; `count = {0}/{1}` keeps bare slots, since the page paints it on `.Text` and the server fills it first through `UiText.flatten`, whose `PlainText` fills only a bare slot. `/zigparty` (`ZigPartyCommand`, in the adventurer group) opens the page bare; `invite --player=<name>`, `accept` and `leave` act from the command line. `InstanceBootstrap.registerParty(plugin)` registers it. A consumer registers only what it adds over this party. Known limits: the page does not repaint on another member's change, and an edited settings file takes effect after a restart. No test (smoke).
- **Changed: the party page shows its privacy toggle only beside a queue handler (zc-instance).** Privacy only decides how a party queues, so `PartyInvitePage` hides it when its deps carry no queue handler. A consumer with one, such as Kweebec Nightmare, sees no change.
- **New: `ZigRequires`, a gate for interaction chains and trigger volumes (zc-objectives).** `objectives/interaction/ZigRequiresInteraction` (`{"Type": "ZigRequires", "Requires": {...}, "Next": ..., "Failed": ...}`) runs `Next` for a player who meets its `Requires` block (the shared one: `Factors`, `Permission`, `Quests`, `Custom`, `AllOf`, `AnyOf`, `Not`). For one who does not, it runs `Failed` and shows the lock line naming the first thing they lack (`LockReasons`) as an ERROR toast. An unauthored block passes everyone, and a chain with no nameable player takes `Failed` silently. `objectives/gate/ZigRequiresTriggerCondition` (a `Conditions` entry `{"Type": "ZigRequires", "Event": "ENTER", "Requires": {...}}`) lets a trigger volume act only for a player who meets it, silently, and answers false for anything that is not a player. Both read through `objectives/gate/RequiresCheck` (`playerSubject`, `passes`, and `refusal`, which answers null for a player who passes or the lock line from the same single walk of the block, so the interaction reads a refused player's block once), the evaluator a quest's gate uses. `ProgressInteractionsBootstrap.registerProgressInteractions` registers the condition on the TriggerVolumes plugin (`TriggerVolumesPlugin.registerConditionType`, guarded), and the type joins the progress interaction specs. A new `server.lang` key names it in the trigger-volume inspector: `customUI.triggerVolumeEffectEditor.conditionType.ZigRequires` = "Requires". Test: `ProgressInteractionsBootstrapTest`, which held one exact-list test, is deleted.
- **New: the `ZigRequires` volume audit, `objectives/gate/RequiresVolumeAudit` (zc-objectives).** The engine decodes a trigger-volume effect's `Conditions` through the tolerant `TriggerVolumeCodecs.TOLERANT_CONDITIONS`, which drops a condition that throws with one WARNING naming no file and keeps the volume, so its gate admits everyone. A misspelt `Requires` key does not throw at all: it decodes asking for nothing. So every boot, on `BootEvent` and never behind `BootAudit.ENV`, the audit reads each loaded pack's `Server/TriggerVolumes/Effects/**.json` raw (folder or zip) and logs one SEVERE line per finding (label `[gate] audit`, domain `gate`), naming the pack, the file and the condition's index: `ZIG_REQUIRES_UNREGISTERED` (no `ZigRequires` type is registered), `ZIG_REQUIRES_MALFORMED` (the condition throws on decode), `ZIG_REQUIRES_UNKNOWN_KEY` (it decodes past a key no codec read) and `ZIG_REQUIRES_NEAR_MISS` (a `Type` that is `ZigRequires` only up to case or spaces). It reports and changes nothing: the engine still loads the volume. Prefab-carried volumes and a world's own volume data are not read. No test (smoke).
- **New: dungeons, `instance/dungeon/` (zc-instance, zc root).** A dungeon is one file, `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Dungeons/<Id>.json` (`DungeonAsset`, store `ZiggfreedCommon/Dungeons`, registered in `FrameworkAssetRegistrar`), every leaf inherited under `Parent`: `Instance` (the template, default `ZiggfreedCommon_Dungeon_Void`), `Enabled` (true), `Share` {`PartyLocked` true, `MaxPlayers` 5 per area, `AreasPerCopy` 4}, `Area` {`Prefab`, a path under `Server/Prefabs/` with its extension; `Spacing` 2048 blocks; `EntryOffset`}, `Run` {`TimeLimitSeconds`, absent for none; `FailOnWipe` false}, `Complete` and `Rewards` (runs and the completion Claim, below), `Copy` {`EmptyCloseSeconds` 60}, `Difficulty` (a label, empty for normal), `Season` (zc's `SeasonLeaf`: open only while that season is live) and `Text` {`NameKey`, `DescriptionKey`}. `DungeonConfig` folds defaults, then packs, then the owner file `mods/ziggfreedcommon/dungeons.json` (`DungeonOwnerLayers`); `open(id)` answers an open dungeon. `DungeonValidator` (domain `dungeon`, label `[dungeon] audit`) joins the boot audit with these codes: `UNKNOWN_DUNGEON_TEMPLATE`, `UNKNOWN_DUNGEON_PREFAB` and `NO_DUNGEON_PREFAB` (warnings); `TEMPLATE_INSTANCE_KEY`, `SPACING_TOO_SMALL` (under twice the server's view radius in blocks while areas are shared), `PACKED_PORTAL_TIMER` (the template's written `TimeLimitSeconds` or its `PortalType`'s own, read as the engine's `PortalKey` reads it, a JSON null as unwritten) and `PACKED_WORLD_EVENTS` (errors, the last two only while `AreasPerCopy` is above 1). `CopyRouter` (pure, `shared()`) keeps one area per party per dungeon. It fills a live copy's free area before it opens `<dungeon>-<n>` (`copyName`), numbered past every name the server still holds, and skips a copy whose tick average (`TickingThread.getBufferedTickLengthMetricSet`) runs over `LAG_BUDGET_MS` (25), counting parties still on their way in. It reserves before the teleport, never into a copy within `CLOSE_MARGIN_MS` (10 s) of its close, and forgets a copy only once its name is free. Its parts: `Rules`, `Placement`, `Kind`, `CopyView`, `WorldProbe` (`LiveWorlds` the live one), `partyKey`, `dungeonOf`, `reserve`, `arrived`, `failed`, `left`, `closing`, `closed`, `copies`. zc ships the void template `Server/Instances/ZiggfreedCommon_Dungeon_Void/instance.bson` (Void world generation, `IsSavingChunks` and `SaveNewChunks` false, `DeleteOnRemove` true, no `InstanceKey`, its gameplay config `ZiggfreedCommon_Dungeon`). `/zigdungeon list`, `status` and `close <copy>` are the admin verbs (`ZigDungeonCommand`, `FAMILY` `zigdungeon`). New `ziggfreedcommon.dungeon.lang`. Tests: `DungeonAssetCodecTest` (the codec round trip), `AssetCodecInitTest.dungeonCodecsInitialize`; `BootAuditWiringTest`'s pass count is deleted.
- **New: areas and membership (zc-instance).** `DungeonEntry` is the one way in: `enter(ref, store, player, from, dungeonId, partyKey)`, `enterAlone` (the admin's: an empty area even where areas are shared) and `enterWithParty` (the party's way in, below) reserve through `CopyRouter`, spawn or join the copy through `InstanceWorlds` with `AreaSpawnProvider` installed in the spawn's config step, paste the area while the player sits on the loading screen, and teleport them to the area's anchor plus `EntryOffset`, their own return point where they stood. Each answers an `Outcome` whose `Result` is `SENT`, `UNKNOWN`, `CLOSED`, `NO_PREFAB`, `MISSING_PREFAB`, `AUDIT_ERROR`, `ON_THE_WAY`, `INSIDE`, `FULL`, `COPY_GONE` or `FAILED`; `PROBE` counts a copy still spawning as live, so a party waits on it rather than opening another. `Areas` (`shared()`, `prepare`, `released`) wakes the prefab's chunk sections first (zc-world's `TickingSections.wake`: an entity added to a sleeping section is parked out of the world), clears a used area (`TargetUtil.getAllEntitiesInBox`, players skipped, plus a sweep for the Intangible markers, drops and projectiles it never returns), then pastes with FORCE through `PrefabUtil.loadPasteRegionAsync` and `paste`, entities and block states included, logging each paste's world-thread time at INFO under `[dungeon]`. Pastes are not sliced across ticks until one is measured over `PASTE_BUDGET_MS` (5). `AreaLayout` fixes a copy's areas when it opens: anchors at `AREA_Y` 64, two to a row on X, then rows on Z, a cell being the column within half a `Spacing` of its anchor. `AreaMembers` (`shared()`) runs a 1 Hz daemon ticker off the world thread (`zc-dungeon-members`, stopped in `InstanceBootstrap.shutdown()`) that drops a member who left the world, their cell or the server and frees an emptied area; a player still on their way after `ON_THE_WAY_LIMIT_MS` (120 s) is let go. `AreaSpawnProvider` (`ZigDungeonArea`, on `ISpawnProvider.CODEC`, so a copy's config saves) answers each player's own area. zc's gameplay config `Server/GameplayConfigs/ZiggfreedCommon_Dungeon.json` (`Parent` `Default_Instance`) respawns the dead through `WorldSpawnPoint`, keeps world events off, and writes `World.AllowBlockBreaking`, `AllowBlockGathering` and `AllowBlockPlacement` all false, since a child's `World` group replaces the parent's whole group. Entry refuses a dungeon whose audit finds an ERROR (`DungeonValidator.entryErrors`, the boot audit's engine walk for one dungeon, cached per loaded asset, each ERROR logged once; an audit that throws is not cached). `/zigdungeon enter <dungeon>` joins the command. Known limits: a block item whose `BlockType` carries a prefab list still pastes that prefab before the placement gate; a member who strays out of their cell is dropped and, dying, respawns at area 0's anchor; parked entities outside the prefab's sections survive a clear. No test (smoke).
- **New: `ZigEnterDungeon` and the `Dungeon` destination (zc-instance, zc-cast).** `ZigEnterDungeonInteraction` (`{"Type": "ZigEnterDungeon", "Dungeon": "<id>"}`) ends a pack's entry chain, `[ZigRequires, ZigEnterDungeon]`, so zc-instance never reads progression. It runs `DungeonEntry.enterWithParty`, which takes the player's party from `ZcParty` (none: a party of one) and counts only members on their way or inside, so an offline member neither enters nor holds a place. The chain finishes only when the player is sent. A refusal takes `Failed` with its line, and a player who is dead, still arriving or mid-teleport fails silently, as the engine's own instance teleporter declines them. It writes through the chain's command buffer and waits on the server (`WaitForDataFrom.Server`). `InstanceBootstrap.registerDungeons` registers it through zc-cast's `InteractionTypes`, beside the spawn provider's codec, the destination, `/zigdungeon`, the runs' listener and the run-tag fill. `DungeonDestinations` adds the destination `{"Type": "Dungeon", "Dungeon": "<id>"}` (owner `ziggfreedcommon`; `Dungeon.of`, `getDungeon`), so a placement's press-F or a conversation's open action enters: it queues the entry on a world task, answers false, and closes the open custom page once the player is sent. `CopyRouter.reserve` lets go of an area held in a copy that is stopping (registered, no longer live) and places the party afresh. `DungeonRefusals` (`tell`, `line`, `dungeonName`) maps each `Outcome` to one toast line: `entry.off`, `entry.out_of_season` (the season named through `Occurrences.source().titleKey`, else `entry.out_of_season.unnamed`), `entry.full`, `entry.on_the_way`, `entry.inside`, `entry.copy_gone`, and `entry.unavailable` for an author's error. Known limits: a `Dungeon` destination naming an unknown id is caught only at its first press (one WARNING); `ZigEnterDungeon` fires no `EnterInstanceTeleporterEvent`. No test (smoke).
- **New: dungeon runs and their counting (zc-instance, zc-objectives, zc-progression, zc-stats).** `DungeonRuns` (`shared()`, `MOD_ID` `dungeon`) holds one run per area over `AreaMembers`. A run begins at the area's first arrival, its start naming that player and everyone already on the way. It fails when `Run.TimeLimitSeconds` passes, when its area frees, or, with `Run.FailOnWipe`, when every member is dead at once (one `DeathComponent` check per such run on the copy's world thread). An ended run takes nobody until its area frees and is pasted afresh. A run leaves as rounds: the new `InstanceRoundStartedEvent(metadata, participants)` (`InstanceRounds.fireStarted`) and the existing `InstanceRoundCompletedEvent`, both queued under the locks and posted on the copy's world thread. The contract is mod id `dungeon`, mode id the `Difficulty`, preset the dungeon id, so a step targets `dungeon:` or `dungeon:hard`, qualified by the dungeon: the winners are every member present at a clear, none on a failure, and every player the start named is counted at the end. `ZigInstanceRoundProducer` gains `KIND_STARTED` (`INSTANCE_ROUND_STARTED`, once per player a start names, with `InstanceRoundStartedPayload`) and `KIND_LOST` (`INSTANCE_ROUND_LOST`, once per participant of a round nobody won). Both are seeded in zc-progression's `ObjectiveKindRegistry`, with the kind files `ObjectiveKinds/Instance_Round_Started.json` and `Instance_Round_Lost.json` and the lines `objective.instance_round_started` and `objective.instance_round_lost` (each with `.any`). zc-stats ships `Dungeon_Runs_Started`, `Dungeon_Runs_Completed` and `Dungeon_Runs_Failed` (Sum, from `INSTANCE_ROUND_STARTED`, `_WON` and `_LOST` on `Target` `dungeon:` by PREFIX, `Breakdown` `TargetAtQualifier`, so each keeps a line `dungeon:<difficulty>@<dungeon id>`), and `Rounds_Won` counts clears too. zc-instance ships the Records section `StatSections/Dungeons.json` (`Order` 35, the three totals; per-dungeon lines in `/zigstats show`). Tests: `ZigInstanceRoundProducerTest` covers the start and loss fan-outs; `ObjectiveKindRegistryTest.theBuiltInVocabularyIsPresentAndEveryKindIsProducible` and the producer test pinning two endings on a loss are deleted.
- **New: what clears a run, `Complete` (zc-instance, zc-objectives).** A run clears the moment every leaf its dungeon writes holds (`DungeonAsset.hasClear()`): `Bosses` (encounter script ids, read off `EncounterDefeatedEvent` for the run of each participant's area, `DungeonRuns.onEncounterDefeated`; any one of them, or every one with `AllBosses` true), `Objective` (`Kind`, `Target`, `MatchMode`, `Qualifier` and `Amount`, written as a quest step is; every member's moments in the area count toward one party total; `completeObjective()`, a `DungeonObjective`) and `SurviveTimer` (seconds from the run's first arrival; `surviveTimerSeconds()`). A dungeon that writes none has no clear. zc-instance cannot see the moment stream, so zc-objectives' `dungeon/DungeonObjectives`, a moment listener registered in `ProgressionDefaults`, matches each produced moment of a player in such a run the way a quest step reads it (a registered kind alias included) and counts it through `DungeonRuns.countObjective`, with `anyObjective` (one volatile read a moment on a server with no such run) and `objectiveOf` its reads. `DungeonValidator` warns `NO_DUNGEON_CLEAR` (nothing in `Complete` can clear a run, or `SurviveTimer` outlasts `Run.TimeLimitSeconds`) and `UNKNOWN_DUNGEON_BOSS` (a `Complete` boss naming no loaded encounter script). Test: `DungeonAssetCodecTest` covers `Complete`.
- **New: the completion Claim and the results screen, `Rewards` (zc-instance).** A dungeon's `Rewards` {`Lootable`, `Diminish`} pays each member present at a clear one Claim: the table rolled once for them and written through `ClaimStore.award` (Claim id `dungeon:<id>:<run>:<player>`, source `dungeon:<id>`, Diminish key `dungeon:<id>:<difficulty>`; with no `Diminish`, every clear pays in full; `rewardsLootable()`, `rewardsDiminish()`). `DungeonResults` then opens a normal `ResultsPage` on each such member's own world thread, built from `DungeonResultsMessages` and a `DungeonResultsActions` holding their Claim, whose Claim button hands it over through `ClaimGrants`; what they leave, or a full bag keeps, waits on the Claims tab. A member who is dead or offline at the clear gets no screen (the respawn screen is a page of its own), and their Claim waits all the same. The run's time is one key, `results.time` = `Time {0}`, filled on the reader's client, since the page paints `#Duration` on `.TextSpans`. `ResultsActions.offersPlayAgain()`, a default answering true, so a consumer with a queue such as Kweebec Nightmare keeps its button, answers false for a dungeon, and `ResultsPage` then shows no Play Again. A run failed by its time limit or a wipe tells each member still in the area why in a toast (`run.failed.time_limit`, `run.failed.wipe`). `DungeonValidator` warns `UNKNOWN_DUNGEON_LOOTABLE` for a `Rewards.Lootable` no loaded table defines. No test (smoke).
- **New: a fight's run, the run-tag seam (zc-encounter, zc-instance, zc-stats).** `seam/EncounterRunTag` (`runOf(worldUuid, players)`, `NONE`) answers which zc run a fight belongs to, as a `seam/RunTag(source, run)` (`source` reads `<site>:<id>`, such as `dungeon:Crypt`). `EncounterSeams.fillRunTag`, `isRunTagFilled` and `runTagFor` fill and ask it, and consulting it unfilled reports once. `InstanceBootstrap.registerDungeons` fills it from `DungeonRuns.runOf`, so on a zc-only server a fight inside an area belongs to the area's run. `EncounterDefeatedEvent` carries it: a fourteen-argument constructor taking the tag, and `runTag()` (the thirteen-argument constructor still links and tags none). zc-stats' `EncounterStats` files such a fight's records under `<bucket>@dungeon:<id>` (`EncounterLeaderboardListener.fightInRun`, read back with `runOf` and `fightOf`), and `EncounterBoards.fightTabs` gives it a Boss Fights tab of its own, named `{fight} in {dungeon}` (`records.fight`, through `DungeonRuns.fightIn` and `nameOfSource`), a `withSpans` tab. No test (smoke).
- **Changed: a round nobody won also counts as lost (zc-objectives).** `ZigInstanceRoundProducer` fires `INSTANCE_ROUND_LOST` after `INSTANCE_ROUND_ENDED` for every participant of any round nobody won, a consumer's own rounds included (a lost Kweebec Nightmare round now counts toward a "lose a round" step). `INSTANCE_ROUND_STARTED` fires only for a round that posts `InstanceRoundStartedEvent`.

## Removed

These 2.3.0 public members are gone in 2.4.0, so a consumer calling one rebuilds against 2.4.0. Each names what takes its place.

- **`EncounterRewardQueue`** (zc-encounter, `encounter/seam/`): the interface `EncounterRewardQueue`, whole. No replacement: every encounter payout is a Claim (`ClaimStore`), which waits for a player who is offline.
- **`EncounterSeams`** (zc-encounter): `fillRewardQueue(EncounterRewardQueue)`, `isRewardQueueFilled()` and `rewardQueue()`. No replacement, for the same reason; the library's wiring root no longer fills it.
- **`EncounterLoot`** (zc-encounter): `grantShares(Store, ZigEncounterRun, String encounterId, LootRef, ParticipationSpec, ParticipationShares, boolean toKiller, boolean queueIfOffline, String label)`. Use `grantShares(Store, ZigEncounterRun, String encounterId, EncounterBindingAsset row, LootRef, ParticipationSpec, ParticipationShares, String reward, String label)`, which writes one Claim per credited participant under the reward name `reward` and titles it from the nullable `row`; the killer is paid at the share they earned.

Changed but still linking: `EncounterLoot.grantDefeat` keeps its descriptor and writes Claims; the `ZigGrant` script action (`ActionZigGrant`) keeps its shape and redirects to the Claim path, not deprecated; `EncounterBindingAsset.Loot.toKiller()` and `queueIfOffline()` and `BuilderActionZigGrant.getToKiller()` and `getQueueIfOffline()` still read their leaves, which no longer change a payout; `EncounterDefeatedEvent`'s thirteen-argument constructor is kept beside the new one; `ResultsActions` gains `offersPlayAgain()` as a default, so an implementor compiles unchanged; `MenuSlot` gains `CLAIMS` between `REPUTATION` and `SETTINGS`, so `SETTINGS`' ordinal moves by one; `PartyInvitePage` and `ZigInstanceRoundProducer` behave as the Changed entries above say.

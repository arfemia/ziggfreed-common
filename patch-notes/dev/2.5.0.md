> Built on Hytale Update 7, like 2.4.1: the manifest's `ServerVersion` and `Dependencies` are unchanged, and no module gains an edge. Additive over 2.4.1: nothing is removed or changes shape, so a consumer built against 2.4.0 or 2.4.1 links unchanged. New: zc-instance's `claims/RewardChestAsset`, `RewardChestConfig`, `RewardChestOwnerLayers`, `ClaimRolls` and `/zigclaims chest`, `claims/RewardChestPage`, `ClaimGrants.claimReward` and `rewardKey`, `ClaimsEventData.reward`, and `dungeon/RunMembers`; zc-presentation's `DetailLine.withSelect`; zc-loot's `loot/reward/ChestSite`; zc-encounter's macros `Zc_Wave_Chain` and `Zc_Wave_Rise`, `EncounterFeedback.beatArgs`, a binding row's `Scale.Companion` and `EncounterRuntime.companionScale` and `companionScaleOf`; zc-calendar's `moon/` (`MoonSource`, `MoonPhases`, `MoonPhaseSystems`, `MoonRange`), `LiveRule` and `CalendarConditionHerald`; zc-progression's `ObjectiveTargetNames`, `progress/ClaimAttempt`, `QuestEngine.attemptClaim` and `AchievementEngine.attemptClaim`; zc-objectives' `ClaimToasts.refusedToast`, `noRoomToast` and `nothingToCollectToast`; zc-entity's `GearSetEvents.nameOf`; zc-world's `PlacementWheres` and `WorldSelector`'s `Placement`; zc-reputation's `Kills` row `Season`; zc-instance's `InstanceRewardGranter.grantAllLanded` and `ClaimGrants.Collected`; zc-core's `PresenceRequiresCodec.Block.liveFactors` and `PresenceRequiresCodec.liveFactors`; zc-loot's `LootableAsset.payingRolls` and `payingPool`, `RewardSpec.withConditions` and `conditions` and `RewardGrants.firstRefusal`; and, now public, `RequiresVolumeAudit.auditAll`, `DungeonResults.nameOf`, `RewardToastLines.row` and `LootRewardKinds.isItemKind`.

- **New: reward chests, `claims/RewardChestAsset` (zc-instance, zc-loot, zc-encounter, zc-objectives).** A reward chest is one file, `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/RewardChests/<Id>.json` (store `ZiggfreedCommon/RewardChests`, registered in `FrameworkAssetRegistrar`, every leaf inherited under `Parent`, the owner file `mods/ziggfreedcommon/reward-chests.json` through `RewardChestOwnerLayers`), whose nullable groups say what the chest is: `Look` (`Prop`, `HintKey`), `Open` (`Credited`), `Contents` (`Lootable`, `Diminish`, `TitleKey`), `View` (`ItemsWindow`, read but no longer changing the view: every chest opens one page, below), `Banner` (`TitleKey`, `SubtitleKey`, `Major`), `Text` (`LeadKey`, `NoticeKey`) and `Despawn` (`Lifetime`, `WhenEmptied`). A file that writes nothing is the library's boss chest, which the shipped `Zc_Spoils_Chest` says out loud; `Zc_Reward_Chest` is the neutral chest, with a banner, that a kill or a break stands when its reward names none. `SpoilsChests.stand` is the one entry every source calls: a won bound fight, keyed by its run id, through a binding row's new `Spoils.Chest` (default `Zc_Spoils_Chest`; an id no file defines is the audit's `ENCOUNTER_UNKNOWN_REWARD_CHEST` warning and falls back to the boss chest with one warning); the new `Reward_Chest` reward kind on the library's KillMob and BreakBlock bonus rows (`{"Kind": "Reward_Chest", "Params": {"Chest": "<id>"}}`, collected by zc-loot's `ChestSite`, keyed `chest:<uuid>`, crediting the one player the moment credits; a chest whose file names no `Contents` warns once); and the admin's `/zigclaims chest [--player=<name>] [--chest=<id>] [--lootable=<id>]`, which stands one two blocks east of an online player, `--lootable` standing in for the file's `Contents.Lootable`. A chest's `Contents` roll once per credited player through `ClaimRolls`, the one Lootable-to-Claim roll, which a dungeon clear and `/zigclaims give` now share; a roll that lands nothing stands no chest. Nothing saved moves: the `SpoilsChest` component, `Zig_Open_Spoils`, Claim ids, Diminish keys and the `claims.spoils.*` keys read as in 2.4.1. New en-US keys in `ziggfreedcommon.claims.lang`: `chest.lead`, `chest.banner.title`, `chest.banner`, `notice.added.chest` and `.named`, `command.desc.chest`, `command.desc.arg.chest`, `command.desc.arg.player.online` and `command.chest.unknown`, `.done`, `.nothing` and `.failed`. Known limit: a `Reward_Chest` row in a consumer's own bonus table is warned and counted lost. Tests: `RewardChestAssetCodecTest`, `AssetCodecInitTest`.
- **Changed: a Claim press says what each reward landed as (zc-instance, zc-loot, zc-presentation).** `InstanceRewardGranter.grantAllLanded` hands each row over with its kind's receipt (`Landed`; null for a row that paid by itself), `ClaimGrants.Result.collected()` lists it per Claim (`ClaimGrants.Collected`), and the Claims page's press toast names each reward at that amount (`ClaimsView.landedLines`: the kind's receipt, else the row at its own quantity, a section per Claim through `RewardToastLines.linesBy` when a press pays several), so a reputation row reads what moved after the worn gain and the Cap. The toast stays unranked, the page's answer to the click, so a quest-completed or Claim-arrived toast raised during the press is requeued after it. A press lists the items too, since it adds them straight to the bag and the game's pickup notice never fires for that, and a reward chest's page names what each line or its Take All landed the same way (`ClaimsView.pressToast`, below). `RewardToastLines.row(RewardChip)` is public. Test: `ClaimToastLinesTest`.
- **Changed: a reward chest opens one page (zc-instance, zc-presentation).** Every reward chest (a boss's, a kill's or a break's, `/zigclaims chest`'s) opens `claims/RewardChestPage`, a `ToastablePage<ClaimsEventData>` over the new `Pages/ZigRewardChestPage.ui` (the kit's `@ZigDetailPage` from `ZigKit.ui`, inside `ZigFrames.ui`'s `@ZigDecoratedFrame`, 680 x 780, no menu rail), where it opened the Claims page's chest mode for what is not an item and then the game's chest window for the items. `ClaimsView.chestDetail` lays it out: the chest's picture, `spoils.title` and how many rewards wait, the difficulty of the first Claim that names one as a pill, the file's `Text.LeadKey` line, one block per Claim (oldest first, under its title) holding what is not an item first and then the items, each item a one-slot `ItemGrid` with its own tooltip (`DetailLine.reward`), and a gold Take All. A line's click (its `#LineSelect`, shown through the new `DetailLine.withSelect`) takes that one reward through `ClaimGrants.claimReward(claims, player, claimId, rewardKey, ref, store, sink)`, an item only when all of it fits, the Claim's other rewards going back where they stood; `ClaimGrants.rewardKey(reward)` names a reward on its line by a short token of its kind, id, quantity and display key, so no command reward's console line reaches the client, and the event carries it as `ClaimsEventData.reward` (`Reward`). Take All is `ClaimGrants.claimFight`. Each press raises one toast naming what landed and how much (`ClaimsView.pressToast`, the Claims page's rule) and repaints, a stale line taking nothing; what does not fit stays listed and waits under Claims. With nothing left the page reads `spoils.all_taken` and stays open, so the toast is seen, and the chest goes at its next upkeep; a player with nothing in a chest reads `spoils.empty`, and no page opens. Every event is answered exactly once through `RewardChestActions.event`. Nothing saved moves: the run-id and `chest:` keys, the `SpoilsChest` mark, `Zig_Open_Spoils`, Claim ids, Diminish keys, both shipped chest files and every `claims.spoils.*` key. `ClaimsPage`'s chest mode (its five-argument constructor), `ClaimGrants.claimExceptItems` and `claimFightExceptItems`, `SpoilsView.openItems` and `SpoilsWindow` still link, though no chest opens them now; `claimExceptItems` and `claimReward` share one split, which puts a Claim's untouched rewards back in their own order. New en-US keys in `ziggfreedcommon.claims.lang`: `spoils.page.hint` and `spoils.all_taken`. Test: `RewardChestPageExitsTest`.
- **Fixed: a Claim's registered-kind row pays through its kind (zc-instance, zc-loot).** A Claim's deferred row that kept its spec (`InstanceReward.spec`) paid through its console line, so reputation from a spoils chest or a dungeon clear skipped everything `ReputationService.change` does: the worn `ReputationGain`, the Cap, the bar and the change event, Beyond parking and the rank notice. `InstanceRewardGranter` now pays such a row through its kind's handler on `RewardKinds.shared()` at the press, at the row's scaled quantity (a `Diminish` step's `{amount}` arrives unchanged), and keeps the line for when the kind cannot pay: no live claimer, a throw, an item kind, or a line rewritten after deferral. Currency, Title, Flair, Command and authored command kinds ride the same seam, so a `Command` reward's `RunAs` and `DelayTicks` are honoured from a Claim. A new `grantAll` overload takes a `sourceId`, and `LootRewardKinds.isItemKind` is public. Test: `DeferredRewardsTest`.
- **Fixed: a re-pasted dungeon area's containers start empty (zc-instance).** The engine's `ItemContainerSystems.OnReplaced` moves a replaced container's items into its replacement, so a used area's chest kept what the last party left, and a leftover in a slot the stash wanted failed that roll's add ("Could not add Item to Stash"). `Areas` now empties every container block inside the prefab's box (live ones through a chunk-store query, parked holders through the sections) before `PrefabUtil.paste` on a used area, so the move carries nothing and the prefab's `Droplist` fills a clean container. Every dungeon, no new field; the `[dungeon]` paste line counts the containers emptied. The boss's spoils chest is an entity prop and never carried. No test (smoke).
- **Fixed: a death in a dungeon drops nothing (zc-instance).** `ZiggfreedCommon_Dungeon` (`Server/GameplayConfigs/`) writes `Death.ItemsLossMode` `None`, as vanilla's `TheDeepworks` does. It wrote only `Death.RespawnController`, and `DeathConfig` inherits each leaf through `Parent`, so the library's dungeons took `Default`'s `Configured` loss (half of each `DropOnDeath` stack) through `Default_Instance`. The durability loss still comes from `Default` (10%). A dungeon whose template names another gameplay config keeps that one's rule. No test (smoke).
- **New: target names for an objective kind, `ObjectiveTargetNames` (zc-progression, zc-entity, zc-objectives).** A module whose objective kind targets ids from its own registry registers a `Namer` for that kind; the neutral composer (`NeutralObjectiveComposer.targetName`) asks it for a step's `{1}` before the engine's catalogues, and a consumer's renderer asks `nameOf(kindId, target)`. zc-entity's `GearSetEvents.nameOf(setId)` names a set by its `TitleKey` (null for an unknown set or an unshipped title) and `ZigGearSetProducer.install` registers it for `GEAR_SET_TIER` on every server, so a gear-set step with no text of its own names the set, where it showed the prettified id. `objective.gear_set_tier` (`ziggfreedcommon.gearset.lang`) reads "Wear {0} pieces of the {1}", retranslated in every locale.
- **New: calendar conditions, the moon and events built from events (zc-calendar).** A calendar event may write `Moon` (`MoonPhaseRange` [first, last] by the engine's own wrap rule, `WorldTimeResource.isMoonPhaseWithinRange`, phase 0 full; `World`, blank for the default world) or `Live` (`AnyOf`, `AllOf`, `NoneOf` event ids) in place of a `Window`, with no `FirstYear`; `Live` beats `Moon`, and either beats a `Window`. It declares `<Id>` and `<Id>_Live` and answers `calendar_live` like any event, so every `Season` leaf reads it unchanged. A condition is no run: every occurrence answer reads it as absent (live, history, next, first year, attendance, the Almanac, zc-stats seasons), and a calendar spawn riding one warns. Each world's phase is kept by two new ECS systems (`MoonPhaseSystems`: seeded once on the world's thread, then `MoonPhaseChangeEvent`). A `Live` that leads round a loop is refused, and so is every event whose `Live` reaches the loop. `Herald.OncePerDay` (default false) shows a condition's start banner once a day per player instead of at every rise; who saw it is kept in memory, so a restart may show it again. A rise or lapse fires no native event, only a log line. `/zigcalendar status` and `force on|off|clear` cover conditions (22 new en-US keys in `ziggfreedcommon.calendar.admin.lang`). New audit codes: `MOON_RANGE_INVALID` and `LIVE_EMPTY` (ERROR); `MOON_UNKNOWN_WORLD`, `MOON_RANGE_PAST_PHASES`, `LIVE_UNKNOWN_EVENT`, `LIVE_CYCLE` and `SPAWN_RIDES_CONDITION` (WARNING). Tests: `CalendarConditionsTest`, `LiveRuleTest`, `MoonRangeTest`, `CalendarConditionEditorSchemaTest`.
- **New: waves of adds, `Zc_Wave_Chain` and `Zc_Wave_Rise` (zc-encounter).** Two data macros under `Server/EncounterManager/Macros/`. `Zc_Wave_Chain` is a chain of waves players clear one after another: it holds a `PendingFlag` a boss's rise can wait on until the run has counted its waves, counted by the `ziggfreedcommon:encounter_waves` reading, and fails open with no run. `Zc_Wave_Rise` is one wave of two fanned rows from the game's own `Spawn` action, so a companion mod's scaling sees its adds, with a signal and an optional moment. Every encounter feedback beat and `ZigFeedback` carries `wave`, the run's wave count (`EncounterFeedback.WAVE_ARG`), through the new `EncounterFeedback.beatArgs`. Test: `EncounterFeedbackTest`.
- **New: a boss a companion mod scales, `Scale.Companion` (zc-encounter).** A binding row's `Scale` takes a `Companion` group (`Health`, `Damage`, both false by default in the editor schema). The library scales nothing through it: a companion mod reads it with `EncounterRuntime.companionScale(store, runId)` or `companionScaleOf(store, subjectRef)` and applies its own scale. `zigencounter validate` warns `ENCOUNTER_COMPANION_HEALTH_STACKS` when `Health` is on beside the row's own health growth, since the engine adds health multipliers rather than multiplying them; a spawn call's own health multiplier (`SpawnOptions`) would stack the same way, so a companion-scaled boss is spawned without one. Test: `EncounterBindingCodecTest`.
- **New: a `Where` that names NPC placements, `Placement` (zc-world, zc-dialogue).** `WorldSelector` gains `Placement`, a list of placement ids matched in any case, a positive axis beside `Match` and `GameplayConfig`: a world matches wherever a named placement's own `Where` (an owner's override included) may stand it, at that match's rank, whatever its NPC is doing, so a disabled or out-of-season placement still answers. zc-world declares the lookup as the seam `world/PlacementWheres`, which `NpcBootstrap` fills from `PlacementWorlds.placementWheres` on every server. A placement's own `Where` cannot use the axis (no chains): one whose `Where` holds only `Placement` stands nowhere and warns `PLACEMENT_CHAIN`, and an unknown id warns `UNKNOWN_PLACEMENT` once placements have folded. A `Once` or `Memories` scope reached through `Placement` keys as that placement's `Where` would inline, so no saved key moves. Every consumer of `Where` (storefronts, boards, dialogue conditions and memories, calendar spawns, waypoints, encounter participation) gets it unchanged, so a storefront, board or dialogue line follows its NPC when a server moves it. Test: `WorldSelectorMatchTest`.
- **New: a reputation `Kills` row's `Season` (zc-reputation).** A `Kills` row can name a `Season`, so it counts kills only while that calendar event is live, the same leaf shops, boards, quests and dungeons use (`SeasonLeaf.append` gains an overload taking a row's own editor sentence; `Kill.season()` and `live()`). A `Season` no calendar event defines is an `UNKNOWN_SEASON` warning in the reputation audit, which now runs with the boot audit beside the commerce, dungeon and conversation passes; its own boot hook stands down then, so an error prints once. The Reputation page's line about defeating creatures shows only while some row is live. Test: `ReputationKillsTest`.
- **Fixed: a loot table, a bonus row, a gear set and a reward row honour their whole `Requires` (zc-core, zc-loot, zc-entity, zc-objectives, zc-progression, zc-commerce, zc-instance).** The four decode `PresenceRequiresCodec.Block`, whose plain `hytale:mod_installed` `Min: 1` gate the mod-gate fold reads; every other `Factors` condition was kept and never asked. `Block.liveFactors()` (null-safe: `PresenceRequiresCodec.liveFactors(Block)`) is `Factors` less each presence gate, asked where each kind pays or applies through the vocabulary it already reads. `LootableAsset.payingRolls()` and `payingPool()` put the table's conditions in front of each roll's and entry's own, so a failed one takes no chance sample (copies made once per instance; the authored instances when nothing is left to ask), and `LootEngine.resolve` and zc-instance's `ClaimRolls` roll them. `LootableConfig`'s enriched and sourced views keep the base file's block, which `LootableAsset.of` dropped, and a contributor's rolls and entries carry its own in front. `BonusRowReactions` asks a row's before its chance through the loot vocabulary. `GearSets.recompute` skips a set whose conditions refuse the wearer (`FactorContext.about`, over the `hytale:` readings plus every contributed and asset-defined factor); a change shows at the wearer's next recompute. `RewardEntryAsset.toSpec` sets `RewardSpec.withConditions` (read back by `conditions()`, never serialized, kept by `with`, `withOrigin` and `asPickupNoticed`), and `RewardGrants` pays a refused row nothing: not granted, queued or failed. `ShopEngine.canPurchase` asks `RewardGrants.firstRefusal` after the offer's own gate and refuses with that factor's `factor:<id>` gate token, so the price is never taken for a row the buyer cannot get. A condition about a player fails closed for an offline one and in `ClaimRolls`, whose lookup answers the run's score and win alone, so a boss chest, a dungeon clear or `/zigclaims give` pays nothing from a table gated beyond mod presence. The shorthand leaves (`Permission`, `Quests`, `Custom`, `AllOf`, `AnyOf`, `Not`) stay unread on these four: write `hytale:permission` or `ziggfreedcommon:quest_completed`. The editor sentences say so, and zc-progression's and RPG Stations' `SCHEMA.md` are regenerated. Every shipped `Requires` on these kinds is the presence gate alone, so nothing shipped pays differently. Test: `LootableRequiresTest`.
- **Fixed: a Collect refused for room says so (zc-progression, zc-objectives).** `QuestEngine.attemptClaim(subject, quest[, atId])` and `AchievementEngine.attemptClaim(subject, achievement)` answer a collect as the new `progress/ClaimAttempt`: what it `paid`, or its `Refusal` (`NOT_WAITING`, `ELSEWHERE`, `NO_ROOM`), off the same checks in the same order the payout asks, a false `canReceiveRewards` being `NO_ROOM`; each engine's `tryClaim` is that attempt's `paid()`, unchanged. The book's quest and achievement Collect and the NPC quest page's Collect read the refusal through `render/ClaimToasts.refusedToast`, so rewards that will not fit show `book.achievements.inventory_full` ("Make room to collect your rewards.", ERROR) where they showed `book.toast.claim_failed` ("Nothing to collect right now."), which stays, as a WARNING, for a press with nothing waiting; the NPC page names a site-bound quest's collection place as the book does (`book.quests.claim_at_site`), and the book's milestone outcomes speak through the same helper (`noRoomToast`, `nothingToCollectToast`). A refused press pays, spends and records nothing. No new keys. No test (smoke).
- **Changed: three reads go public, with no behaviour change (zc-objectives, zc-instance).** `RequiresVolumeAudit.auditAll()` is public and returns the trigger-volume `ZigRequires` findings the boot walk logs (`run()` logs from it), so a consumer's audit can report them; `DungeonResults.nameOf` is public, and `StatBoardDungeons` calls it; a dungeon run's members are one tracker, `dungeon/RunMembers`, for walk-in and portal dungeons alike (`DungeonRuns.Run`, `PortalDungeonRuns.Run`).
- **Changed: every locale carries 2.5.0 (zc-calendar, zc-instance, zc-entity).** Every key new since 2.4.1 (22 in `ziggfreedcommon.calendar.admin.lang`, 14 in `ziggfreedcommon.claims.lang`) ships in all nine locales, and `objective.gear_set_tier` is retranslated.

CommonLib 2.5.0 is out now! Any mod or pack can leave a reward chest, so a rare kill or a rare block can drop a chest with a share only you can open. Claiming a reward now tells you what you got.

Built for Update 7, like 2.4.1, and nothing built against 2.4.0 or 2.4.1 needs a change.

## New

- Added reward chests that any mod or pack can hand out. A pack can give a creature you kill, or a block you break, a chance to drop a chest with a share only you can open, just like the chest a boss leaves where it falls. A banner tells you when one goes up, and whatever you leave in it waits on the Claims tab.

## Changes

- A boss's chest, like every reward chest, now opens on one page with your whole share laid out as cards: currency, reputation and experience first on gold cards, the same reward from each source added into one, then your items on their own rarity colours, best first. Click a card to take it, or Take All for everything. Anything that doesn't fit in your bags stays listed and waits on the Claims tab.
- Claiming from the Claims page or a boss's chest now lists every reward you got and how much, with your gear's favor bonus counted in.
- Quest steps and achievements that ask you to wear a gear set now call the set by its name, not its file id.
- Dying in a dungeon no longer drops any of your items. Your gear still loses some durability, as it does when you die anywhere else.

## Fixes

- Fixed reputation from a boss's chest or a dungeon clear ignoring gear that speeds your reputation gains and the reputation's cap, and holding back its bar and rank-up notice until your next gain.
- Fixed chests inside a dungeon keeping whatever the last party left in them, which could also cost the next party some of the chest's own loot. Each run's chests now start with only their own loot.
- Fixed collecting a quest's or an achievement's rewards with a full bag saying there was nothing to collect. It now asks you to make room, and your rewards keep waiting until you do.
- Fixed items you take from a reward chest or the Claims tab landing in an empty armor or utility slot when your hotbar is full, where a shield could sit unseen. They now go into your hotbar, storage or backpack, just like an item you pick up.

## For server owners

- `/zigclaims chest` stands a reward chest next to an online player, holding a loot table's roll for them, so you can see one on any server.
- `mods/ziggfreedcommon/reward-chests.json` changes any reward chest on your server, such as what it holds, how it looks and how long it stays, without touching the mod that ships it.
- Every time the server starts, the log now names problems in reputation files too, beside shops, dungeons and conversations.

## For mod and pack authors

- A reward chest is one file in your asset pack: how it looks, who can open it, what it holds, its banner and when it goes. A boss fight can name the chest it leaves, and a bonus row on a kill or a block break can pay one.
- Added two boss fight pieces for waves of adds: a chain of waves players clear one after another, which a boss can wait on before he rises, and a single wave that rises in two rows. Every fight notice can say which wave it is.
- A calendar event can follow the moon, such as running only on the full moon, or run while other events are live, in place of dates. Its start banner can show once a day for each player, and `/zigcalendar status` and `force` work on it like any event.
- A reputation's kill row can name a seasonal event, so a kill counts toward it only while that event runs.
- A shop, a board or a conversation line can name a placed character in place of a world, and then follows that character wherever a server moves them.
- A boss fight can let a companion mod scale its boss's health and damage. The library itself leaves both alone.
- Fixed a loot table, a bonus row, a gear set or a single reward line ignoring what it asks of the player beyond which mods are installed. A rank, a finished quest or a permission it asks for now decides who it pays, and a shop turns a buyer away before taking the price for a reward they can't get.

We'll see you in Orbis!

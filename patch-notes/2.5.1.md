CommonLib 2.5.1 dresses up the reward chest: every card says where its reward came from, gold cards show their rarity the way items do, and opening a chest flashes and plays a sound to match the best thing inside. It also stops Essence of Life and other item currencies from vanishing when your bag is full.

Built for Update 7, like 2.5.0, and nothing built against 2.5.0 needs a change.

## New

- Added a word on every reward chest card that says where its reward came from: Boss Kill, Dungeon Clear, Bonus, Quest, Event or Gift.
- Added a flash behind the chest and a sound when you open a reward chest, grander with the rarest reward inside. Epic and Legendary cards shimmer.

## Changes

- Rewards that aren't items now sit on smaller gold cards, five to a row, in smaller text so a long name like a reputation's fits whole, and show their rarity the way items do: the rarity's word in its colour and its slot behind the picture.
- The same reward from two places now shows as two cards, so a currency paid by the boss and by the dungeon clear gets a card for each.
- The Objective Book now opens on right click.
- Turning in a quest step no longer shows an extra Objective complete notice after the hand-in's own notice and your rewards.
- Claiming your reward on a dungeon's results screen now shows what you got, reputation included, the same way claiming it on the Claims tab does.

## Fixes

- Fixed Essence of Life and other item currencies vanishing when they were paid or refunded into a full bag. What fits goes into your bag with the usual pickup notice, and the rest drops at your feet.
- Fixed a reward that hits a currency's cap showing its full amount instead of the amount you actually got.

## For server owners

- If you move your server back to this version from a newer one, the newer version's stat bonuses no longer stay stacked on your players' characters.

## For mod and pack authors

- An Almanac page can list an item from another mod and hide that slot on servers without the mod.
- A mod can name the word a reward chest's cards show for its own kind of reward, and a pack can change the sound a chest plays when it opens.

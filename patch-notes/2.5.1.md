CommonLib 2.5.1 is a hotfix on 2.5.0 that stops Essence of Life and other item currencies from vanishing when your bag is full. Built for Update 7, like 2.5.0, and nothing built against 2.5.0 needs a change.

## Fixes

- Fixed Essence of Life and other item currencies vanishing when they were paid or refunded into a full bag. What fits goes into your bag with the usual pickup notice, and the rest drops at your feet.
- Fixed a reward that hits a currency's cap showing its full amount instead of the amount you actually got.

## For server owners

- If you move your server back to this version from a newer one, the newer version's stat bonuses no longer stay stacked on your players' characters.

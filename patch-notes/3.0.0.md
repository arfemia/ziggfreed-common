CommonLib 3.0.0 is out now! Step into the Temple Hub, a new wing off the Forgotten Temple, build quests, achievements and reputations in game, and run your server from one owner menu, `/zigadmin`. Every menu takes a new look too.

Built for Hytale Update 7. This is a major version: some parts of the library that older mods used are gone, so update the mods you run on CommonLib along with it.

## The Temple Hub

Talk to the **Doorkeeper** near the Forgotten Temple's landing to step into the Temple Hub. You arrive in an open temple court, the Concourse, with the hub map and the Porter just ahead. Step on the pad in the marble alcove to go back to the temple, or the one in the goblins' iron alcove to head straight out to the overworld.

Goblin builders are at work on the lots around it, and the notes and boards tell you what the place is. Mods you run can open halls of their own on its lots.

## Make it in game

Admins who build content get an **Authoring Tools** tab in the creative library. The Inspect Tool tells you what a block, a creature or an item on the ground is called, and the block families, NPC groups and item tags it belongs to: the names you write in quests and other content.

Click a character, creature, block or item with the **Quest Tool** and the quest editor opens with it filled in. Name the quest, choose its giver, steps and rewards, and save. It's live at once. The Achievement Editor and the Reputation Editor work the same way, and everything you make lands in one server content pack in your mods folder that opens in the Asset Editor.

## One menu for every owner screen

`/zigadmin` opens one menu for every owner screen, with boosts, world rules, level curves, quests and the HUD layout, on a server running CommonLib alone too. The **Kill Drops** screen lists every extra drop or command from a kill, from every mod and from your own file, to switch off or retune.

## A new look

Every menu and screen has a new look, modelled on the game's own Chapter screen: a wide band over a blurred world, with gold titles.

We'll see you in Orbis!

## The full changelog

### The Temple Hub

- Added the Temple Hub, a new wing off the Forgotten Temple. Talk to the Doorkeeper near the temple's landing to step inside, and the Porter on the Concourse sends you back to the temple or out to the overworld.
- Added the Concourse, where you arrive: an open temple court with the hub map and the Porter just ahead, a pad in the marble alcove back to the temple and one in the goblins' iron alcove out to the overworld. Goblin builders are at work on the lots around it.

### Holidays and gifts

- Added holiday changes for a mod's villagers: the same villager comes back for the next holiday with new clothes, new things to say and a new spot to stand, and the change happens on its own when a holiday starts or ends, with no restart.
- Added gifts you hand to a friend: point a mod's gift item at another player to give it to them. They see who it came from, and it works with PvP off and in the Forgotten Temple.
- Added gifts for villagers: pointing a gift at a villager counts toward a quest that asks you to bring them one.
- Added a way for mods to leave out a reward or a job that is only there for servers without another mod, so you never see it greyed out where that mod is installed.

### Making content in game

- Added the Authoring Tools tab to the creative library, for admins who build content in game. Each tool works in creative mode for players with its permission, and anyone who may use the Asset Editor may use them.
- Added the Inspect Tool, which shows what a block, a creature or an item lying on the ground is called, and the block families, NPC groups and item tags it belongs to. Its permission is `ziggfreedcommon.author.inspect`.
- Added the Quest Tool. In creative, click a character, creature, block or item and the quest editor opens with it filled in: name the quest, choose its giver, steps and rewards, and save. It's live at once and players collect its rewards. You can also stand a new giver with their own name. The progression admin page has a Quest editor button, and `/zigauthor open Quest` opens it too.
- Added the Achievement Editor: make an achievement in game with its category, what it asks for, its points, a title and rewards players collect, or change one a mod ships. Open it with the Achievement Tool or from the progression admin page.
- Added the Reputation Tool and the Reputation Editor. Make a reputation with a name, a picture, the creatures whose defeat earns or costs it, ranks of its own above Exalted and rewards past its top rank, then click a character with the tool to make it treat players by their standing. The tool needs the `ziggfreedcommon.author.reputation` permission, and `/zigauthor open Reputation` opens the editor too.
- Added `/zigauthor list` and `/zigauthor open`, to list the in-game editors and open one.
- Added a server content pack: everything you make in game is saved in one content pack in your mods folder that opens in the Asset Editor. `/zigpack status` shows where it is, and `/zigpack export` zips it for another server.
- Added Asset Editor forms for quests, achievements, reputations, tracks and titles. Pick a quest step's or a reward's kind from a list and its fields change to match, with the game's own pickers for items and effects and suggestions for blocks and creatures. A file you save into the server content pack loads without a restart.

### Combat and stats

- Added critical chance and critical damage for Fire, Ice, Lightning and Arcane hits, and critical chance against slowed, rooted or frozen foes.
- Added situational damage bonuses gear and effects can carry: against burning, poisoned or bleeding foes, barely hurt or unaware foes, when surrounded, at high health, moving, sprinting or unhurt, on charged attacks, thrown and shot hits, and at the far end of your reach.
- Added weapon-bound bonuses: a talent or a set bonus can count only while you hold a certain weapon.
- Added Combat Luck: gear and effects can raise how often monsters leave bonus drops.
- You can turn the critical hit word off for yourself on the Settings tab's Combat card.
- Items that gave flat elemental damage now give a percent bonus to the same element.
- Gear that speeds reputation keeps working.
- Talents and abilities from mods built on CommonLib can make a stun shorter each time it lands on the same foe, before the foe shrugs it off for a moment.
- Item tooltips say plainly what a gathering or building stat does, such as Durability Saved and Blocks Saved.

### Gathering and building

- Added a "Lucky find!" note for every bonus drop from any mod, with its own switch on the Settings tab.
- Gathering Yield on your gear raises the chance of bonus finds when you break blocks or harvest by hand.
- Thrift no longer spares sand, torches or any block that drops as an item when the block it rests on goes. Logs and gravel you place, which stay put, still can be spared.
- A block you place is remembered as yours even on servers that turn off placed-block protection, or when you build in creative, so tools that take back your own blocks keep working.
- Vein mining and felling pay a block's special drops once, on the block you mined.

### Boosts

- Added the Boost Token: hold right-click to start the boost it carries. Admins hand them out with `/zigboost give`, and `/zigboost own` lists and starts the boosts you may start yourself.

### Quests, achievements and rewards

- Finishing a quest, having it wait to be collected, and collecting it each show one notice everywhere. The quest book, a character's quest list and conversations no longer stack their own toast on top.
- A quest you collect where you picked it up now says so when it's done.
- The achievements book shows the points milestone ladder on any server, and a rung that won't fit in your bags asks you to make room.
- An achievement retired by its mod is a Feat of Strength now: if you earned it, you keep it among your feats and can still collect it, nobody can earn it any more, and its points no longer count toward your total.
- Boss chests, dungeon clears and endless-wave rewards check your own progress, so a reward that needs a skill level, a rank or a finished quest pays you when you have it.
- If a dungeon fails to open after you paid to go in, what you paid waits for you under Claims.
- Almanac items that need something, such as a rank, a level or a quest, show a lock and what you still need, and still count toward the season's total.
- A shop choice that asks for something tells you what you lack when you pick it.
- Added the Items Consumed, Sprint Distance and Swim Distance statistics.

### Dungeons

- A cleared run's results show your party's deaths.
- A dungeon's par is now its target time.

### Fixes

- Fixed quest steps and achievements for dying, taking fall damage, eating or drinking, sprinting, swimming and reaching a place never counting on a server running CommonLib on its own.
- Fixed quest steps and achievements for earning and spending currency, finishing bounties and bounty streaks never counting on a server running CommonLib on its own.
- Fixed the Asset Editor's reward kind list, which offered nothing, and its quest step kind list, which missed the kinds a mod adds.

## For server owners

- One menu for every owner screen, `/zigadmin`, with the quest and achievement switches, use gates, NPC placements, the HUD layout and the boost settings, on a server running the library alone too. An operator opens it; grant `ziggfreed.ziggfreedcommon.command.zigadmin` to let someone else in. A mod that keeps its own admin command opens the same menu with its own screens first.
- The boost settings screen writes `mods/ziggfreedcommon/boost-settings.json` as you would by hand, under `Default`, and Reset takes a setting back out of the file. The command a boost start runs as the console shows on the screen only to a server operator.
- Boost Templates lists the boosts players start themselves, each with its switch, percent, length, wait and how many can run at once, saved into `mods/ziggfreedcommon/boosts.json`. Award Boost gives a player online any boost as a Claim they collect when they want it to start, starts one for everyone now, or grants a player online the permission a boost they start themselves asks for.
- The Kill Drops screen lists every row that drops something extra from a kill, from every mod and from your own `mods/ziggfreedcommon/bonus-rows.json`. Switch a row off, or change which mobs it covers, its chance, and whether the killer's Combat Luck adds to it. A row's commands change only in the file, and a kill row's command can name `{tier}`, the tier a mod gives the mob that died, beside `{player}` and `{mob}`.
- World Rules lists every world rule: switch one off, or change what gains in its worlds (on or off, the multiplier, the tracks that gain nothing or alone gain, whether hits on players pay) and what the picks grant there, saved into `mods/ziggfreedcommon/track-world-rules.json`. The switch above the list turns every rule off at once.
- Level Curves lists every curve on the formula and changes its two numbers, showing what levels 10, 25, 50 and 100 would take as you type, saved into `mods/ziggfreedcommon/track-curves.json`.
- The quest limit is yours to set on the Quest Settings screen, or in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/progression.json`. It ships with no limit. A player at the limit is told their log is full, and a board contract never counts.
- The quest tracker's switch and place are on the HUD layout screen, and in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/player-settings.json`. A change shows on every player's screen at once.
- The Almanac Tab screen says when players see the Almanac tab: always, only while a season runs, or never.
- Press Help at the top of `/zigadmin` to read what each screen does, with a button that opens it. The owner screens wear the server's menu theme like the rest of the menus.
- The Doorkeeper in the Forgotten Temple leads into the Temple Hub. Switch the way in off with `{ "Zc_Hub_Doorkeeper": { "Enabled": false } }` in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/npc-placements.json`, and the goblin builders with `Zc_Hub_Builder` (the Concourse's) and `Zc_Hub_Builder_Large` (the empty lots') in the same file.
- The hub's districts are yours to move or switch off in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/hub-districts.json`. A copy already open keeps its layout until it empties and closes.
- On the first start, the reward chest View setting, which no longer does anything, is taken out of `mods/ziggfreedcommon/reward-chests.json`, with a copy of the file kept beside it.
- The NPC placement file stays small now: on its first start it drops the rows of worlds that no longer exist, and logs how many. Nothing standing is placed again. Every data file the library saves keeps a backup copy beside it, ending in `.bak`; leave it in place.
- If you go back to an older CommonLib, it reads every file and save this one writes.

## For mod and pack authors

- This release removes parts of the library that 2.x mods could call. The developer notes list each one.
- Added tracks: a level to climb on its own curve, with rewards waiting on the Claims tab and picks a player chooses, up to whole talent trees with branches, saved builds, a suggested build and resets. A world rule can switch off what a track grants.
- Added boosts on tracks: a boost file lifts every gain on the tracks it names by a percent, timed or standing, personal or for the whole server, under one cap.
- Added crowd control any mod can call: stuns, taunts and dropping aggro, which Kweebec Nightmare's Moonbloom and hunters use. A foe's own rules, and a boss fight's rule for each phase, decide what lands.
- A requirement block works in the game's own condition lists: an effect's, an NPC's decisions, trigger volumes, a world event's stages, and shop and reputation choices.
- A native drop list can roll a CommonLib loot table, and the game's own Objectives can count CommonLib's moments and pay its rewards.
- Added dungeon pieces: an endless wave run with a chest that banks each cleared wave, checkpoints that heal a party and bring it back after a fall, tiers, weekly omens, a daily layout everyone shares, and layouts generated from room files with fresh rooms for each run.
- A placed character can come and go with the moon and other calendar events.
- A placed character can be a trainer: its page teaches a track's picks, points to its quests and sells a reset.
- An owner file of your mod gets an in-game editor on the owner menu from one data file.
- The flat damage stats are gone: an item that names `DamageBonusFlat_<Type>` in its own stats must name `DamageBonus_<Type>`. Renamed stat ids, such as `GatherSpeed_Tall_Tree`, which is `GatherSpeed_TallTree`, still read under their old id in your gates and files.

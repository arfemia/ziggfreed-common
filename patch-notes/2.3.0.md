The library's release for Hytale's Update 7. It is built for Update 7 servers (0.7.x) and does not run on the current game version, so a server that has not updated the game keeps 2.2.0. Nothing built against 2.2.x needs a code change, unless it builds a reputation change record itself.

### New

- **Banners in the game's other looks, with a sound.** A mod can show a banner in any of the game's banner styles (the goblin breach and void eviction banners among them, not only the ordinary and the large one), and have a sound play as it appears. A moment that banners the server, such as a boss fight starting, takes the same two choices in its file: `Style` and `SoundEventId`. A file that writes neither looks and sounds as before.
- **The quest book tells you when more steps are coming.** A quest that keeps its later steps out of sight until you reach them ends its step list with one line, such as "1 more step after this", so a hidden step never reads as the end of the quest.
- **Added a list of targets for one step.** A quest step, a board contract's step or an achievement can name several targets in `Targets`, beside `Target`, and any one of them counts, compared the way the step's `MatchMode` says. A content pack can count three kinds of bear toward one hunt without also counting every creature whose name happens to contain the word. The step's line names the first target in the list.
- **Added a gate for one contract on one board.** A contract's entry in `Boards` can write its own `AcceptRequires`, which stands in for the gate the board puts on that contract's difficulty band: an empty block lets anyone take it there, and leaving it out keeps the band's gate.
- **Added each rank's achievement to a reputation's page.** Under each rank, a reputation's page lists the achievement that rank earns and what it pays, which you collect under Achievements.
- **Added sale dates for shop offers.** A shop offer can go on sale only between two dates each year, with `"Dates": { "Start": "11-20", "End": "01-06" }`: both days count, a range can cross the new year, and dates turn over at midnight UTC. Outside its dates an offer is off the shelf and can't be bought. A server owner changes an offer's dates as they would any other offer field, with an offer file of the same name in their own pack.
- **Added a new title showing itself.** When you earn a title while you show none, it shows beside your name straight away, as if you had picked it yourself. If you took your title off with Show none, a new one stays off until you pick it. A server owner can turn this off with `"$AutoShow": false` in `titles.json`.
- **Added titles for players who are logged out.** An administrator can grant you a title, or take one away, while you are offline: `/zigtitle grant`, `revoke` and `list` take a player who is not online, by name or by player id. The change waits and happens the next time you log in, with the same notice as any new title.
- **Added titles to requirements.** A conversation, a shop offer or a quest can ask whether you have earned a title, or whether you show it, so a mod or a pack can save something for the players who hold one.
- **Added a count of your titles.** The Title tile on the Settings tab and the Earned heading in the title picker show how many titles you have earned of the ones you can see, such as "2 of 5".
- **Added secret titles.** A title can stay a secret until you earn it: it is left off the picker's "Not earned yet" list and out of the count until it is yours. A server owner can make any title secret by giving it `"Hidden": true` in `titles.json`.
- **Added a reminder to collect a title.** When a title comes with collecting an achievement's rewards, and you have earned the achievement but not collected it yet, the picker says to collect it from that achievement. Press the line to open the achievement in your book.
- **Added a way to open the title picker from a pack.** A conversation line, a menu tile or a character can open the title picker with `"Open": "Titles"`.
- **Added titles in chat, for servers that want them.** A server owner can show the title you pick beside your name in chat too, with `"$Chat": true` in `titles.json`. Until now titles never showed in chat, and they still don't unless the owner switches this on. When it is on, the title picker says your title shows in chat. Titles still never show over a player's head.
- **Added your gear set to the buff bar.** While a bonus of a gear set you wear is on, the set shows one icon beside your other effects, with its name when you hover it. A set can give each of its bonuses its own icon (`Badge`), so the icon changes as you put on more of the set; a set that gives none shows the library's own gear set icon. The icon stays after you log back in, change worlds or respawn, and goes when the bonus does.
- **Added a way to add to another pack's gear set.** A gear set file can name the set it adds to in `Joins` instead of making a second set over the same pieces. Its pieces join the set's, and a bonus that asks for the same pieces as one of the set's own joins that bonus, so the full set shows one notice with every line, not one notice per mod. A file whose set is not on the server adds nothing.
- **Added timed bonuses to the buff bar.** A mod can show a timed bonus, such as a boost or a blessing, as an icon beside your other effects, its ring running down with the time the bonus has left. It stays up after you log back in, change worlds or respawn. A bonus with no icon of its own shows one of the library's two, one for your own bonuses and one for bonuses running for the whole server, and up to four of each show side by side.
- **Added Combat, Gathering and Adventure rankings to Records.** The menu's Records tab ranks players by what they do on your server: Combat, Gathering and Adventure each rank their own numbers, such as Mobs Defeated, Blocks Broken, Quests Completed and Playtime. Under each one you can switch between All Time, This Week and This Month, and every seasonal event gets a tab of its own while it is on, named with its year, such as "Hallow's Eve 2026". That tab stays after the event ends, and Records keeps each event's two most recent. An event with an Almanac page also gets a tab after Combat and the others, ranking that page's tallies. A mod can add stats and tabs of its own as JSON, a column nothing on your server counts never shows, and a quest, a conversation or a shop can ask how many of something you have done (`ziggfreedcommon:counter`, such as `Mob_Kills`).
- **Added Renown to Records.** Renown ranks how many of the game's own objectives you have completed and your standing with the server's reputations, one column for each (up to three), over all time only, so its week, month and season tabs stay empty. Your standing there is what you have earned, before anything your gear adds. Your own numbers update when you open Records; everyone else's stand as of their last login or the last time they opened it.
- **Added Records settings for server owners.** `"$Enabled": false` in `mods/ziggfreedcommon/stats.json` turns stats off: Records stops counting and ranking and its tab goes away (the Almanac's own tallies still count), and what players already have is kept. The same file moves the day a week starts and the time zone weeks and months turn over in, Monday and UTC unless you change them (`"Weekly": { "StartsOn": "Sunday", "Zone": "America/New_York" }`, and `Zone` under `"Monthly"`). It also adds date ranges of your own, each a tab that counts from one date to the next and can still be read after it ends (`"Launch": { "Kind": "Range", "From": "2026-10-12T00:00", "To": "2026-10-19T00:00" }`, with a `NameKey` from your own lang file to name the tab, and a `Zone` of its own, UTC unless set). An admin can list every stat an online player holds with `/zigstats show --player=<name>`.
- **Added `Hands` for a reward kind that hands over an item.** A reward kind file whose command hands over an item can name that item in `Hands`, and collecting a reward of that kind then waits until your bag has room for it, like any other item reward, instead of dropping it at your feet.
- **Added Arcane, Void and Life damage.** Three damage types beside Hytale's own Fire, Ice and the rest, for the mods built on the library to hit with. Each shows its damage numbers in a colour of its own, and each has its own line on the death screen: "You were killed by arcane damage!", "You were killed by void damage!" and "You were drained of life!".
- **Added damage bonuses by type on gear.** Gear can raise one damage type, such as Fire or Void, by a percent or by a flat amount, on any server running the library, with or without MMO Skill Tree. The item's tooltip names the bonus, such as "Fire Damage: 10%" or "Flat Fire Damage: 5". A bonus to a type also counts for the types under it, so a bonus to Physical counts for slashing and crushing hits too.
- **Added hit tags.** A mod can tag its own hits, such as a bomb's blast, and tell them apart while they keep the game's damage types, so a creature's fire resistance still counts against a fire bomb. Tag the damage in its file with `"Tags": {"Hit": ["My_Bomb"]}`.

### Changed

- **Banners on Update 7.** Event banners (an achievement, a matchmaking countdown, a party invite, a round title in Kweebec Nightmare) are drawn through Update 7's banner styles, at the same two sizes and with the same timing as before. A banner sent without a second line now shows its headline instead of nothing.
- **Hiding your HUD hides the quest tracker too.** `/zighud hide` with no display named now takes the quest tracker away along with the bars. While everything is hidden, every Show switch on the Settings tab reads off, and turning one back on lifts the hide, so the displays you had showing come back.
- **A contract's own requirements count at the board.** A board contract that writes `Requires` can now be taken only by a player who meets it, checked after the board's own gate and its band's; until now a board never read it. A requirement that only switches a feature or a mod on or off still decides whether the contract is posted at all, as before.
- **The game's own bosses without a boss bar count as you'd expect.** Fights from the base game that show no boss bar, such as the Voidtaken Grizzly's hunt, the Goblin Feastmaster, the Revenant Guardian and a Void Crystal, no longer pop fight notices as if a boss fight had started or been lost. Killing a Goblin Feastmaster, a Voidtaken Grizzly or a Revenant Guardian counts as a boss fight won for whoever lands the killing blow, so quests, contracts and achievements that ask for boss fights count it. A Goblin Feastmaster that a Veilbound Coffer's goblin waves bring along does not count.
- **A new title points you to Settings.** The notice for a new title now tells you to choose it in Settings, under Title, instead of naming a command, and the Title tile there shows only while the server offers a title.
- **The Almanac tab names the event on now.** The second line of the Almanac's tab in the menu names the event that started most recently, with its picture, when that event's calendar file gives it a name and a picture, so a short festival running inside a longer season shows while it lasts. With no such event on, the line names the season on now, as before.
- **Gear set notices count the full set and name the next bonus.** A set's notice now counts against the pieces the full set takes, so a set that lists two helms and two shields for one helm and one shield reads "2/2", not "2/4". When a bonus comes on with another still ahead, the notice names that one too, and putting on a piece that turns on no bonus shows a quiet line with your count. A piece counts only where the set's bonuses want it, so a shield in your main hand no longer counts toward a bonus that wants it in your off hand, and a bonus that's on never shows a count below its own size.
- **Boss fight records moved to Records' Boss Fights.** Each boss fight on record has a tab of its own there (one for each party size or difficulty when the fight keeps those apart) with Wins, Best Time, Best Share and Damage, over All Time, This Week, This Month and each seasonal event's tab. The records kept so far carry over once, when your server first starts on this version, and the fights you win from then on add to them. Open it from the Boss Fights tab on Records or with `/zigleaderboard encounter`, where `--encounter=<id>` still picks the fight. The menu's Records tab now shows whenever your server keeps stats, not only once a boss fight has a record. The old screen's total and damage taken are no longer kept.
- **A reward past a reputation's top rank waits to be collected.** Past a reputation's last rank, each reward it pays now waits for you: a notice says one is waiting, and the gold Collect button on that reputation's page pays up to 20 at a press. If your bags can't hold it, it keeps waiting. Falling below a reward and earning it back no longer pays it twice.
- **Shops have a new look.** A shop opens in the frame of the game's own Memories page. Its wares fill a grid on the left, each a tile with its picture, its name and its price, which turns red when you can't afford it, and a tag such as "3 left", "Sold out" or "None today" when it has a limit. A ware behind a requirement or sold out is dimmed, and hovering any ware you can't buy yet tells you why. Pick one to see it large on the right with its cost, its limits and what you get, and buy it there. Your wallets and the shop's two filters sit under the title.

### Fixed

- **Notices over an open menu, and the Play screen.** On an Update 7 server, the previous version could no longer change an open menu in place: a notice floating over a menu (a purchase going through, a reward landing) could stay up or never appear, and the Play screen stopped updating its queue status. Both update in place again.
- **A weapon's own move gets its key back.** When a mod puts its own action on a key a weapon also uses, it hands the press to the weapon while the weapon's move is ready (a bow with a signature charge banked, say). On an Update 7 server the previous version could not read a weapon that first checks it is not broken, the way bows and crossbows do, so the mod always took the press. It reads them again.
- **Building where the world forbids it earns nothing again.** On the updated game, a block placed in a protected spot (a place the game refuses to let you build in) still counted as placed for quests, achievements, statistics and anything else that pays for building. The library asks the game the same question it asks before it lets a block go down, so a refused placement counts for nothing again.
- **Characters placed at the world's spawn show up again.** On the updated game, a character a pack places at the world's spawn point, such as a hub's quest givers, never appeared. The game can now take a moment to find the spawn point while the ground there loads; the library waits for it without holding up the server and places the character as soon as the spot is known.
- **Characters placed where no player is standing are really there.** On the updated game, a character a pack places somewhere nobody is (a hub's quest giver at the world's spawn while the server starts, say) could be tucked away instead of placed: it only appeared once a player walked over, without the extra toughness some characters are given, and a later check could place a second one beside it. The library now wakes that part of the world before it places anyone and makes sure no copy is already there, so the character stands there from the start, once, with its toughness. A boss a mod raises far from its players, such as the Warden in Kweebec Nightmare, appears the same way, and a fight nobody is standing next to keeps going until it is decided.
- **A round that holds your gear holds your runes too.** A minigame that takes your inventory for a round and hands it back afterwards (Kweebec Nightmare's chase, say) now also holds your slotted rune abilities and your rune bag, the updated game's two new inventories: you go in without them and get both back exactly as they were. A round that was already under way when the server updated gives back what it took and leaves your runes where they are.
- **Titles your achievements already earned you.** An achievement you earned before it came with a title, on an older version of a mod say, never handed the title over. It does the next time you log in. A title that comes with collecting an achievement's rewards waits until you have collected them.
- **A contract outlives its board.** A finished board contract waits to be collected at the board you took it from. When that board is taken down or its season ends, you can now collect the contract from your quest book instead of waiting for the board to come back.
- **A quest that starts itself hands in at its giver.** A quest set to start on its own (`AutoAccept`) and handed in where it was taken (`@accept`), when started by the first thing you did for it, now remembers its giver as the place it was taken, so you can hand it in there. Before, a quest started that way could not be handed in anywhere.
- **A character that was killed stays gone after a restart.** A character a pack places once and never brings back (one not set to respawn) stood again after the server restarted, because the library forgot what it had placed in a world every time the server stopped. It now remembers across a restart, and forgets only a world that is deleted, such as a dungeon instance that closes. A character killed before this update can stand once more at the first restart; from then on it stays gone.

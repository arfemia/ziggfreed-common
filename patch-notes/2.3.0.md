The library's release for Hytale's Update 7. It is built for Update 7 servers (0.7.x) and does not run on the current game version, so a server that has not updated the game keeps 2.2.0. Nothing built against 2.2.x needs a code change.

### New

- **Banners in the game's other looks, with a sound.** A mod can show a banner in any of the game's banner styles (the goblin breach and void eviction banners among them, not only the ordinary and the large one), and have a sound play as it appears. A moment that banners the server, such as a boss fight starting, takes the same two choices in its file: `Style` and `SoundEventId`. A file that writes neither looks and sounds as before.

### Changed

- **Banners on Update 7.** Event banners (an achievement, a matchmaking countdown, a party invite, a round title in Kweebec Nightmare) are drawn through Update 7's banner styles, at the same two sizes and with the same timing as before. A banner sent without a second line now shows its headline instead of nothing.

### Fixed

- **Notices over an open menu, and the Play screen.** On an Update 7 server, the previous version could no longer change an open menu in place: a notice floating over a menu (a purchase going through, a reward landing) could stay up or never appear, and the Play screen stopped updating its queue status. Both update in place again.
- **A weapon's own move gets its key back.** When a mod puts its own action on a key a weapon also uses, it hands the press to the weapon while the weapon's move is ready (a bow with a signature charge banked, say). On an Update 7 server the previous version could not read a weapon that first checks it is not broken, the way bows and crossbows do, so the mod always took the press. It reads them again.
- **Building where the world forbids it earns nothing again.** On the updated game, a block placed in a protected spot (a place the game refuses to let you build in) still counted as placed for quests, achievements, statistics and anything else that pays for building. The library asks the game the same question it asks before it lets a block go down, so a refused placement counts for nothing again.
- **Characters placed at the world's spawn show up again.** On the updated game, a character a pack places at the world's spawn point, such as a hub's quest givers, never appeared. The game can now take a moment to find the spawn point while the ground there loads; the library waits for it without holding up the server and places the character as soon as the spot is known.
- **Characters placed where no player is standing are really there.** On the updated game, a character a pack places somewhere nobody is (a hub's quest giver at the world's spawn while the server starts, say) could be tucked away instead of placed: it only appeared once a player walked over, without the extra toughness some characters are given, and a later check could place a second one beside it. The library now wakes that part of the world before it places anyone and makes sure no copy is already there, so the character stands there from the start, once, with its toughness. A boss a mod raises far from its players, such as the Warden in Kweebec Nightmare, appears the same way, and a fight nobody is standing next to keeps going until it is decided.
- **A round that holds your gear holds your runes too.** A minigame that takes your inventory for a round and hands it back afterwards (Kweebec Nightmare's chase, say) now also holds your slotted rune abilities and your rune bag, the updated game's two new inventories: you go in without them and get both back exactly as they were. A round that was already under way when the server updated gives back what it took and leaves your runes where they are.

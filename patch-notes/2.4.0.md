CommonLib 2.4.0 is out now! It brings dungeons you go into with your party, a party you can start on any server, and a Claims tab where your rewards wait until you take them. Built for Update 7, like 2.3.0.

## INTO THE DUNGEON

Any mod or pack can add a **dungeon**, behind a block you use, a character you press F on or a line in a conversation. Go in and you land in an area set up fresh for your party, and everyone in your party who follows you in lands there too. Another party gets an area of its own.

Do what the dungeon asks, whether that's beating its boss, finishing a task inside or simply lasting long enough, and the **results screen** shows your time, how your party did and your completion reward, ready to claim. Every run you start, clear or fail counts on the new **Dungeons** tab in Records.

## Bring a friend (or four)

Type `/zigparty` to start a **party**, invite up to four players who are online, and take on a dungeon together. It works on any server running CommonLib, with no other mod needed.

## Your loot can wait

Rewards you earn now wait on the new **Claims** tab in your menu until you take them, so a full bag never costs you a thing. Boss rewards go there too, and nothing on the tab ever runs out.

## Work comes to you

A character can hand out a board's jobs right on their own **quest page**: take one, hand it in and collect your pay without walking over to the board. The **bounty boards** open on that same page, with their wallets at the top, a countdown to the next contracts and Reroll on any contract you haven't taken.

We'll see you in Orbis!

## The full changelog

### Dungeons

- Added dungeons, which a mod or pack opens behind a block you use, a character you press F on or a line in a conversation. Going in takes you to an area set up fresh for your party, and each member who goes in after you joins you there. Another party gets an area of its own, unless the dungeon lets parties share.
- You can go into a dungeon on your own too. A party member who is offline doesn't go in or take up a place.
- If you die in a dungeon, you get back up in your own area. When you leave, you're back where you stood when you went in.
- You can't place, break or gather blocks in a dungeon, so every party finds it as it was built. A player in creative mode can still break them.
- A dungeon can open only during a seasonal event. Outside the event, its way in tells you which event it opens for.
- Each visit is a run. A run clears when your party does what the dungeon asks, such as beating its boss, finishing a task inside or lasting a set time, and it clears for everyone in your area at that moment.
- A run fails when its time runs out, when everyone in it leaves, or, in a dungeon that says so, when the whole party is down at once. When time runs out or the party falls, everyone still inside is told why.
- Added a results screen for a cleared run, with your time, what you and your party did, the bosses you beat and your completion reward, which you claim right there. Whatever doesn't fit in your bags waits on the Claims tab, and so does the reward of anyone who was dead or offline when the run cleared.
- A dungeon can pay less for a repeat clear on the same day or in the same week.
- Added a Dungeons tab to Records, ranking the runs you've started, cleared and failed across every dungeon on the server.
- A boss fought inside a dungeon gets its own tab on Records' Boss Fights, named for the fight and the dungeon.
- Quests, board contracts and achievements can count the dungeon runs you start, clear or fail, in one dungeon or in any.
- They can also count the minigame rounds you lose, such as a Kweebec Nightmare round, beside the rounds you play and win.

### When a dungeon can't take you

- A dungeon's way in tells you why it can't take you: it's switched off, it opens only during a seasonal event (and which one), your party's area is full, you're already in a dungeon or on your way into one, or it closed just as you got there.
- A dungeon that's set up wrong never sends you somewhere broken: its way in says it "cannot be entered right now", and the server log tells the server's owner what to fix.

### Locks

- A door, lever or entrance a mod locks behind something, such as a quest you haven't finished, stays shut for you, and a notice names the first thing you still need, in the same words a locked shop ware uses.
- A spot you walk into can act only for players who meet what it asks. It stays quiet for everyone else, since you might only be passing by.

### Parties

- Added parties on every server running CommonLib. Type `/zigparty` to open your party, see who's in it and invite players who are online, or use `/zigparty invite --player=<name>`, `/zigparty accept` and `/zigparty leave` from chat. A party holds up to five players, anyone in it can invite, and an invite lasts a minute.
- The party screen, Kweebec Nightmare's included, now updates on its own when someone joins, leaves or is removed, an invite arrives or runs out, or the party is disbanded.
- Fixed the party screen's member count reading "{0}/{1}" in Kweebec Nightmare.

### Claims

- Added the Claims tab to your menu, after Reputation. Rewards you've earned but not taken wait there, and the tab shows whenever one does. Pick a reward to see what it holds, then press Claim, or Claim All to take everything. Items need room in your bags, and whatever doesn't fit stays on the tab for later. Nothing on it runs out.
- A notice tells you when a reward arrives on the Claims tab, and when you log in with rewards waiting, a notice says how many.

### Boss rewards

- Boss rewards no longer land in your bags: they wait on the Claims tab. When a boss that a mod or pack pays rewards for falls, everyone who took part gets their share there, and how much you get depends on your part in the fight.
- A boss now pays its rewards for your first win each day, unless its own reward says otherwise. A win whose roll lands nothing doesn't use up your day. The day turns over at midnight UTC.

### Boards and quest pages

- Added board jobs on a character's quest page. A character can hand out a board's jobs on their own page, where you take them, hand them in and collect your pay, each tagged with its board's word, such as Bounty, and its grade. The page shows the board's wallets too.
- Bounty boards now open on the same quest page as the people who give out work, with the board's wallets, a countdown to new contracts, and Reroll on each contract you haven't taken. Here lists the board's contracts, and Mine everything you carry.
- A contract you can't take yet sits under Locked and lists everything it still needs.
- Your quest book shows a contract's board tag, its grade in the board's own colour and where it hands in, on any server.

### Reputation

- Gear that raises a reputation now speeds up what you earn there by a percent, where it added standing of its own while you wore it. Taking it off never lowers what you earned.
- Your Reputation page says by how much: pick a reputation, and the last line under How to earn tells you what your gear adds there.

### Notices, titles, shops and achievements

- A notice that arrives while a bigger one is showing no longer vanishes: it waits its turn and shows after it.
- Reward notices group the items each loot bundle rolled under that bundle's name.
- Taking a quest in a conversation now shows the Quest accepted notice on every server, where it needed a mod that added its own.
- A title a quest gives is now granted to anyone who already completed and collected that quest, the next time they join, the same way achievement titles already were.
- Shops read whole: the stock filter's list opens below its box, a ware's name gets a third line and shows in full on hover, and a locked ware's requirements wrap onto more lines, where they ended in "...".
- Fixed achievements with several steps showing each step as "Unnamed step". Each step shows its own words, and the achievements search finds them.
- Fixed conversation answers and the buttons on the party, play and results screens showing "{0}" where a number or a name belongs.

### For server owners

- `mods/ziggfreedcommon/dungeons.json` changes any setting of any dungeon on your server, such as switching one off or letting more players into an area, without touching the mod that ships it.
- `/zigdungeon list` shows each dungeon and how it's set up, and `/zigdungeon status` the copies open right now. `/zigdungeon close <copy>` closes a copy and sends everyone inside back where they came in, and `/zigdungeon enter <dungeon>` takes you into an empty area yourself.
- `/zigclaims give --player=<name> --lootable=<loot table>` rolls a loot table and puts what it lands in a player's Claims, online or not.
- Every time the server starts, the log names each walk-in spot in a pack whose requirement can't be read, with the pack and the file, since the game would let everyone through it.
- A damaged Claims file is never overwritten: the server leaves it as it is, so what players earned is still there once it's repaired. Until then, Claims earned that session last only until the server restarts.
- A damaged pending rewards file is no longer overwritten on the next reward, so nothing waiting in it is lost.
- `/zigtitle grant --player=<name>` now finds the offline player you typed, never an online player whose name only starts with it.
- `/zigquest status` now spells a quest's cooldown in your language, such as "2h 5m", "2d 1h" or "under a minute", where it read "49h 0m".

### For mod and pack authors

- A dungeon is one file in your mod's asset pack, `Server/ZiggfreedCommon/Dungeons/<Id>.json`. Name its area's prefab, what clears a run (`Complete`: bosses, an objective written like a quest step, or a time to survive) and what a clear pays (`Rewards`). Put the way in on a block's interaction chain (`ZigEnterDungeon`), a character's press F or a conversation line (`"Open": {"Type": "Dungeon", "Dungeon": "<Id>"}`).
- A boss's script pays a Claim through the game's own reward files: a `GrantReward` naming a reward of `"Type": "ZigClaim"`, with its loot table and what a repeat win pays.
- `ZigRequires` checks a player's requirements in an interaction chain, such as before a door opens or a dungeon lets them in, and as a trigger volume's condition.
- A conversation checks the same requirements with the `Requires` condition, `{"Type": "Requires", "Requires": { ... }}`: a line or a beat shows only while they pass, and the server log names a block that asks for something nothing on the server registered. A `Gate` condition with a `Requirements` block, as some mods let you write it, loads too and reads the same, and the log names each line that still uses it.
- The `HasActiveBounties` condition passes while a bounty board in your world has contracts up, so a character can offer its board only when there is work.
- Any mod can pay a player a Claim, open the Claims tab with `"Open": "Claims"`, and hear when one arrives.
- A board can name the characters who host it in `Hosts` and its tag word in `Text.PillKey`, "Bounty" unless you set one. Each host's quest page lists the board's current contracts beside its own quests.
- Gear raises a reputation by a percent through a stat, in whole percent points: ship a `ReputationGain_<reputation id>` stat for one reputation, or raise the library's `ReputationGain` for every one. A reputation file's `Gear` leaf is gone: a file that still writes it loads with a warning, and its gear adds nothing.
- A Records tab a mod splits into choices can rank weeks, months and seasons by what a player gained in that period, with a number such as their level shown beside their name.
- Nearly everything built against 2.3.x links unchanged. A mod that handed the library a queue for boss rewards owed to offline players, or paid a boss's shares through the library itself, rebuilds against 2.4.0, since every boss reward is a Claim now, and so does one that built the old board page itself or called another piece the developer notes list as removed.

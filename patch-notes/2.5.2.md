CommonLib 2.5.2 lets everyone talk to a quest giver at once.

Built for Update 7, like 2.5.1, and nothing built against 2.5.1 needs a change.

## Fixes

- Fixed quest givers taking only one player at a time: while one player talked to a giver, everyone else's F did nothing, and a player behind the giver couldn't start a conversation at all. Now anyone in reach can press F from any side, and everyone's conversation stays open.
